Section 1 -- What Happens When the Issuer Calls the Bond

1.1 What is the Issuer Call Option?
The Issuer (LCL Opportunities 3 S.à r.l.) has the right -- but not the obligation -- to redeem some
or all outstanding Series AC Digital Bonds before the scheduled Maturity Date of 15 May 2030.
This means that the digital bonds are returned to the Issuer and your principal and accrued interest
is returned to you. This is called exercising the "Call Option."
The Issuer may exercise this right on any date from 15 May 2028 onwards. You will not be asked
for your consent and cannot opt out or refuse. Once the Issuer has given notice it is irrevocable
and the bonds must be returned.

1.2 Could the Issuer call only some bonds and not mine?
Yes. The IM permits the Issuer to call all or some of the outstanding bonds. If a partial call is made,
only the holders of the bonds selected for redemption will be affected -- the remaining bonds
continue until maturity or a further call. If the Issuer calls bonds held on your behalf by Tokinvest
we will notify you directly that your bonds are included in a call.

1.3 How much notice will you receive?
The Issuer must give at least 7 calendar days' notice before the Call Option Redemption Date.
Tokinvest will receive the notice on your behalf and advise you via your registered contact details
held in our records for you. The notice is irrevocable once issued -- the Issuer cannot withdraw it.
Redemption takes effect 7 calendar days after the notice period expires. Notice may also be
published on the LynxCap Investments website (https://www.lynxcapinvestments.com).

1.4 What will you receive?
For each bond called you will receive:

   · 1.00 -- the full face value (principal) per bond
   · Plus all interest that has accrued and has not yet been paid, up to (but not including) the Call

       Option Redemption Date

Payment will be made up to 10 Business Days after the Call Option Redemption Date. Funds will
be returned to your account with Tokinvest where you can withdraw to your bank or invest in other
products on our platform.

1.6 What happens to your bond tokens?
This bond is implemented as a digital token on the Ethereum blockchain (smart contract:
0x83eCECE38d58C7CCa6085462F2437Ad67c7c338F). Tokens for called bonds will be returned
to the Issuer where they may be cancelled ("burned") on-chain automatically. You do not need to
take any action. If only some of your bonds are called, the tokens for the remaining bonds are
unaffected.

Section 2 -- Early Redemption for Tax Reasons or Force Majeure Event

2.1 What are these redemptions?
Separately from the Call Option, the Issuer has the right to redeem all Series AC digital bonds
early if;

i) a change in tax law or its official interpretation means the Issuer would be required to make a
withholding or deduction from payments to bondholders -- for reasons outside its control and after
taking reasonable steps to avoid it. This is called an "Early Redemption for Taxation Reasons" and
is described in Section 9.11.3 of the IM documentation
(* note that this is an edge-case protection for the Issuer against unforeseen tax changes. It has
nothing to do with your personal tax position).

OR

ii) An event occurs due to circumstances outside the Issuer's reasonable control that prevent it
from operating normally -- for example, extreme events that disrupt its business or ability to
perform its obligations under the bond. Such events are known as Force Majeure Events. This is
called exercising the FME Option and is described in section 9.11.6 of the IM documentation

Like the Call Option, these redemptions are the Issuer's rights, not yours. You will not be asked for
your consent in the case of an Early Redemption for Taxations Reasons or the Issuer exercising
the FME Option.

2.2 Key features of a Tax Redemption
   · The Issuer may only use this right if the tax change becomes effective after the Issue Date
       (15 May 2026).
   · Unlike the Call Option, a tax redemption must redeem all outstanding bonds -- the Issuer
       cannot selectively redeem some.
   · Notice: the Issuer must give between 30 and 60 Business Days' irrevocable notice to
       bondholders.
   · Redemption occurs on the next Interest Payment Date following expiry of the notice period.
   · You will receive: 1.00 principal per bond plus accrued interest to (but not including) the
       redemption date -- the same amount as a standard early redemption.

You do not need to take any action if a tax redemption is triggered. The process is automatic, and
proceeds will be paid to your account with Tokinvest in the same way as a standard redemption.

2.3 Key features of a Force Majeure redemption

    · The Issuer may only use this right while a Force Majeure Event is continuing.

    · Notice: the Issuer must give at least 7 calendar days' notice to bondholders. Once given,
         this notice is irrevocable.

    · Redemption Date: redemption takes place 7 calendar days after the notice period expires
         (the "FME Redemption Date").

    · Scope: the Issuer may redeem all or only some of the outstanding bonds -- similar to the
         Call Option, this is not necessarily a whole-series redemption.

    · Amount you will receive: 1.00 principal per bond, plus interest accrued (but not yet paid)
         up to (but not including) the FME Redemption Date.

You do not need to take any action. If your bonds are included in a Force Majeure redemption, you
will be notified directly, and proceeds will be paid to your registered payment details in the usual
way, with settlement 10 Business Days after the FME Redemption Date.

Section 3 -- Exercising Your Put Option

3.1 What is the Put Option?

The Put Option gives you -- as the bondholder -- the right to require the Issuer to redeem your
bonds early on specific dates. Unlike the Issuer Call (which the Issuer decides), the Put Option is
your personal right. You choose whether to exercise it; other investors are not affected by your
decision.

3.3 How much will you receive? -- The Put Option Redemption Amount

For each bond you hold, the Put Option Redemption Amount is calculated as follows:

   · 1.00 -- the full principal amount per bond
   · Plus accrued and unpaid interest from the last interest payment date up to (but not including)

       the Put Option Date
   · Less any pro-rata share of fees, costs and expenses incurred and recharged by the Issuer in

3.4 How do you give notice? -- The notice mechanics

Your Put Option Notice must specify the number of bonds that you wish to redeem and should be
sent to Tokinvest directly ops@tokinvest.capital.

3.5 Minimum size -- Can I exercise on only part of my holding?
Yes. You may exercise the Put Option on some or all of your bonds, provided the amount you are
putting represents at least 25% of your total Series AC bond holding. You cannot exercise on less
than 25% of your holding.
