**Full reasoning in `_draft_allocation-and-float.md`. Full evidence in `Aurumix_Protocol_Landscape.md`.**

1. 🔴 **THE MISSING COUNTERPARTY, and it gates everything.** The model names the vault three times and the seller zero times. Brink's, Loomis and Malca-Amit are vaults; they do not sell gold. **The sharpest version is the buyback**, which §3.2 says is funded by "the custodian liquidating exactly those grams". Custodians do not liquidate. Aurumix needs a **two-way dealer**, a vault (named), an independent assayer, and a title register.
2. ✅ **Mining Events: solved.** Split into **Contribution Date** (own anniversary) → **Allocation** (first LBMA AM fix after cleared funds, T+1) → **Bar Procurement Cycle** (internal) → **Monthly Allocation Report**. **Scarcity layer deleted** on four grounds: no economic sense, premium not assurable, security classification risk, limits market-cap growth.
3. ✅ **The gold float is the mechanism that makes it work.** Working inventory on the metal side, not a reserve. Decouples the investor's ticket from the treasury's purchase, so a 20 USD saver works where PAXG needs 120 USD and XAUT needs 170k USD. It also fixes price risk (30 days → under 24 hours), makes a live quotable price possible, and absorbs buyback outflow. **Launch dealer-carried (the Aurus model), migrate to own capital later.**
4. ✅ **1 AURX = 1 gram, permanently.** Token count is gram count, the invariant reads directly (`float + allocated ≥ tokens outstanding`), and it matches every gram-denominated product in the set. **CG's "10 gram units" is the Indian price-quoting convention, not a token size.** Three fixes owed to their doc: delete "at launch", change 0.01 g to 1 g, and reclassify `price = vault gold × fix ÷ tokens` as a verification identity rather than the price definition.
5. 🔄 **SUPERSEDED, pending client agreement. Now: recover custody at ENTRY and EXIT, never off the metal**, because a monthly cash bill dies on persistency (~38% still contribute at month 61, so the bill outlives the touchpoint). Reasoning in `Aurumix_Process_Maps_Custody_Fee.md`. ⚠ **Under Option A there is now a second reason not to deduct grams: the customer owns them, so selling a slice needs express contractual authority.** Original wording kept below for the audit trail. ~~**Custody fee must be CASH**, skimmed from SIP inflow.~~ Fixed weight rules out grams-in-kind and fee-by-dilution. **The SIP gives Aurumix a monthly cash touchpoint no other gold token has**, which is why cash-only works here and fails for the category.
6. ✅ **Dividend → Gold Rewards.** The current design (out of operating profit, weighted by Investment Value, 80% to top 10%) is a profit share and forces a security classification. Replace with a **fee rebate credited in grams**, funded from **interchange and credit revenue**, **capped at what that customer generated**, **sized by ICS tier earned by behaviour not amount**, never called a yield. **Removing Investment Value from ICS weighting also kills the second double-count.** Realistic size: 0.10 to 0.75% by tier at launch.
7. 🔴 **THE PREMIUM IS ZERO. Model it at zero everywhere.** Liquid markets arbitrage it away; illiquid markets have no price to express it; and either way it is not Aurumix's to set, so promising it creates securities risk. Midas XGZ and ORO both trade at a **discount**. Exposure is one-directional: above NAV holders sell to each other and Aurumix gains nothing, below NAV they all redeem at once.
8. ✅ **Spot: restrict benefits, not supply.** Capping supply looks like a share sale. Denying ICS, dividend, credit ratio and card tier is a loyalty programme. Price the difference (spot ~4 to 5% entry, redemption fee decaying over 6 to 12 months). **Large spot tickets help the treasury.**
9. ✅ **Entry fee must sit at the top of the 2 to 5% range at launch.** Build-up at Y1 on 100 g bars: fabrication premium 3.0% + price-gap risk 0.36% + float cost of capital 0.49% + rail 0.30% = **4.15% before margin**. **A 2% fee loses 0.71 USD on every 75 USD contribution.** The fee then falls to ~4% (Y3) and ~3% (Y10) as bar denomination improves, which is a real scale economy to sell.
10. ✅ **Token standard: permissioned base (ERC-3643) + optional wrapper.** **Lead with wind-down, not rights-survival:** Cache Gold published a wind-down plan, followed it, and still stranded **96% of supply**, because it had no holder registry and never burned redeemed tokens. **An anonymous bearer token cannot be wound down.** ⚠ If a wrapper ships, the rights delta must be in the wrapper's own terms (Kinesis's wrapper strips gold title and yield silently). 🔄 **REVISED 2026-08-04, twice over.** First, **a wind-down plan is mandatory under VARA Company Rulebook Part VII.A**, not optional differentiation. Rule 1.k requires that selling client assets is "explicitly excluded from, and not necessary for" completing it. So Aurumix must build one regardless; **publishing it is free differentiation on work already required.** Second, **the permissioned decision is now load-bearing for the licence, not just for ICS** (decision 24).
11. ⚠ **"LBMA" means two things and the client's doc conflates them.** A **Good Delivery bar** is ~12.4 kg / ~1.4M USD and Aurumix will never hold one. A **bar from an LBMA-accredited refiner** is 1 kg or 100 g and is freely obtainable in Dubai. **Copy XAUm's wording: "99.99% LBMA-accredited gold held in 1 kg bars."** UAE local refiners are **UAEGD**-accredited, a separate voluntary standard.
12. ⚠ **Sourcing is a live risk, not a theoretical one.** **Emirates Gold DMCC was suspended from both the UAEGD and LBMA lists in July 2023.** Naming a single refiner without an **accreditation covenant and substitution clause** (use Aurus's LBMA/DMCC/LPPM multi-list wording) would have broken the core promise overnight.
13. ✅ **Distribution is the actual moat.** **Not one of nineteen protocols has a savings plan, recurring purchase, or retail referral programme.** PMGT's post-mortem names zero distribution as cause of death. **Anti-MLM answer from Aurus:** pay each tier for a function performed, not for recruitment depth, with graduation instead of overrides. Combine with the IRDAI trailing-commission structure for two independent structural defences.
14. ⚠ **Failure precedent: tokens die of revenue starvation, not regulators.** PMGT was **not** killed by AUSTRAC (the Mint began exit talks before any public action and was cleared in July 2025); it charged zero fees, so it had no revenue and no internal advocate. Digix zeroed its own demurrage in 2019 and lived on its ICO treasury until holders voted it away. **Counter-example worth copying: DGLD**, where holders own the gold outright at law, which is why six years of dormancy harmed nobody.
15. ⚠ **rwa.xyz is reliable for enumerating the market and unreliable for describing an issuer.** It invented an ORO issuer, gave AZG four wrong fields, and labelled two protocols "Bankruptcy Remote" with nothing supporting it. **Use it to find protocols, never to describe them.**
16. ⚠ **"Audited" almost never means the gold was checked.** The published audit is usually a **smart-contract** audit while the **reserve attestation** is absent, stale or self-signed. Only DGLD, PAXG and VNXAU have credible independent reserve examination; XAUT's BDO Italia ISAE 3000 is the strongest in the set. **Separate the two explicitly in every deliverable.**
17. ⚠ **SIP and agent precedent, now three models not two.** Indian **insurance** enforces persistency. Indian **mutual-fund SIP** does not penalise at all. Indian **health insurance** supplies the third and most useful piece: the **cumulative bonus**, a status earned slowly by behaviour, capped, lost by a specific event, sitting on a **base that is never touched**. **Aurumix = MF SIP wrapper + life-insurance discipline + health-insurance reward ladder.** Agent model = high first-year commission + renewal commission payable only while the policy stays in force + upline override, under IRDAI 2023 caps. ✅ **Verified against the IRDAI Master Circular:** grace is **15 days monthly mode / 30 days otherwise** (clause 8(a)), revival window is **3 years** from first unpaid premium. ⚠ **The IRDAI 2023 commission caps and override tables are still unretrieved** and still block agent economics.
18. ✅ **SIP and spot are transaction types on one account, not classes of investor.** The old separation was supply rationing and it died with the queue. Two classes also deadlock the front door: spot is the stated entry point for new investors, spot earns no ICS, and spot access is ICS-gated, so a newcomer cannot pass a gate that only prior participation opens. One account, one KYC, one receipt, one score. **The gold must never differ by channel**, only the fees and services wrapped around it. **Differentiate on three levers and no others: price** (entry fee by tier), **credit** (LTV by tier, none for spot), **time** (decaying redemption fee on spot only).
19. ✅ **The contractual lock-in is deleted**, replaced by a declared savings goal that scores nothing. **A commitment with no penalty for breaking it is free to make**, so every rational investor selects the longest term and the choice carries no information about them. It also bought nothing: **credit activated at month 6 in all six rows of the client's own §6.2 table.** Removing it costs no retention (breaking the lock and simply not paying were already identical) and gains real acquisition, which is why MF SIPs scale. **Confirmed SIP survives as a backward-looking threshold, not a promise:** stopping early forfeits nothing because nothing was promised. 🔄 **REVISED 2026-08-12, then REVERSED 2026-08-13 (Abdur). Confirmed SIP is 6 *consecutive* counted periods, and it gates the score itself, not only the benefits.** Below the gate there is no score and no tier, so the score can never contradict the gate — the same problem the 2026-08-12 redefinition solved, closed a stricter and simpler way (decision 46, scoring draft §1.6). ⚠ **Never write "6 month commitment" anywhere. Write "6 consecutive contributions"**, which is the original wording. **The 2026-08-12 "6 contributions" sweep is cancelled: do not run it**, and if a file already carries the shortened phrase, restore it.
20. ✅ **ICS measures behaviour only. Investment Value is removed entirely.** If the score rises with amount, then entry fee, credit ratio and rebate all improve with capital, which is a return proportional to investment. **Amount already sizes the base; behaviour sets the rate.** Components: **continuity and tenure primary** (a streak and elapsed time, they fail in opposite directions and cannot be merged). 🔄 **SUPERSEDED IN PART 2026-08-12: referrals, family portfolios and Masterclass are removed from scoring entirely** — they corrupt the one clean number, they are already paid twice over (agent commission, will/family discount ladder), and a *status* bonus for recruiting is the MLM shape decision 13 exists to defeat. The two primaries survive as **Record** and **Standing** and are now combined by a **minimum**, not a sum, so neither can substitute for the other (decision 46). **Binding and testable, and it still passes: a 20 USD/month saver who never misses must be able to reach the top tier** — now with *no* acceleration route at all, so contributions are the only path for anyone.
21. ✅ **The miss-and-recover ladder, plus one financial rule.** 5-day grace (🔄 2026-08-10, was 15; never expires on a weekend or holiday), **step-down never reset**, rebuild slower than decay, 12-month revival window, and **credit as a climbing ladder from month 6 rather than a switch** (a switch means month 7 and month 60 hold identical borrowing power, so it does no retention work after it fires). Governing promise, in these exact words: **you can lose your status, you can never lose your gold.** 🔴 **Arrears must price at the fix on the day they clear, never at the missed period's fix**, or revival becomes a free one-directional look-back option on gold. Magnitudes (step-down size, rebuild rate, revival restoration) deliberately wait for the formula.
22. 🔴 **Persistency is the number that governs the calibration.** Indian life insurance retains **~79% at month 13 and ~38% at month 61**. Six in ten gone within five years, steepest fall in year two. Aurumix has the same year-one economics: the entry-fee build-up leaves **~1% net margin on a contribution**, so a customer is barely profitable in year one and only becomes valuable by persisting. **Churn kills this model, not fee levels.** This is why ICS is the hub block.
23. ✅ **Direct-ownership ARVA (Option A), not stable-value.** VARA's issuance guidance splits gold tokens in two, and the client's Individual Gold Receipt already describes the first. **Choosing it removes the Reserve Asset regime entirely**, and with it the 2%-of-reserves capital component, which at the Year 10 target could be ~USD 4M locked. The stable-value branch also silently deletes the Gold Receipt, because a claim holder is a creditor. **The price is Annex 2 III.B.1: prove title transfers with the token.** No UAE statute or case establishes that an on-chain transfer moves title to vaulted gold. **This is the biggest open legal question in the project and it gates the build.**
24. ✅ **The token is the trigger, not the proof, and this collapses the token-standard question.** Five components with divided labour: **allocation + bailment terms CREATE** the ownership; the **title record PROVES** it exists and is beyond Aurumix's reach; the **permissioned token** keeps every holder identified so ownership is provable at all times; **attestation** proves the customer-level split is honest. ⚠ **Do not overstate the register: it is evidence, not title.** DMCC calls a warrant a document of title, but that sits in DMCC's contract framework, not a statute, and no court has tested it. **An anonymous bearer token cannot satisfy the test at all**, because if anyone can hold it you cannot say who owns the gold. **So ERC-3643 and Option A are one decision, not two.** 🔴 **Their app is due early September. We do not think freeze-and-seize satisfies Option A, because it is a *control* power not an *identity record* and VARA's test is proving title. **Put it to counsel as an explicit alternative, because if it were sufficient it would save real work.**
25. ⚠ **Entity structure: three routes, route 2 recommended, pending one legal answer.** (1) Aurumix holds as caretaker under contract, which is what Kinesis does and what Comtech attempts; (2) a passive DIFC or ADGM vehicle holds title, where **statute says a transfer in cannot be undone by the transferor's bankruptcy** (DIFC Trust Law Art 14(2), ADGM Foundations Art 33); (3) customers named directly at the vault, **ruled out at retail** because each would need their own vault account or Tradeflow membership and a 1 kg bar cannot be split across eighty named owners. **Route 2 only because onshore UAE could not answer whether fungible gold is reclaimable from a bankruptcy estate.** **Nobody in the category uses route 2, so it is unoccupied ground.** ⚠ **Design assuming route 2 and simplify down** if counsel permits; retrofitting a vehicle into the buyback and credit pledge later is much harder. ⚠ **The vehicle is passive as a business and busy as a mechanism:** allocation, buyback, credit pledge and Digital Will all now route title through it, and the existing drafts assume Aurumix holds the metal directly.
26. ⚠ **Title record: Tradeflow preferred, vault books are a real fallback.** Three options: a **Tradeflow warrant** (Comtech, Dubai-only, natively transferable and pledgeable), the **vault's own books plus a weight list** (PAXG with Brink's London, the category norm, USD 1.79bn running on it), or **an own permissioned chain** (Kinesis, but not independent of the issuer). **Under route 2 the warrant is held by the vehicle**, with the customer-level split beneath it, which is how retail custody works everywhere. **We would be the first to make a title registry load-bearing in a token ownership claim**, and the only existing Tradeflow user put 19 kg of 111 kg on it. ⚠ **NOT ESTABLISHED whether Tradeflow supports sub-accounts, fractional interests or a beneficial-owner layer.** That decides how much of route 3 is available and it is a DMCC question, arguably more important than the fees. **LBMA-accredited bars are about what you buy and have nothing to do with the ownership record.**

28. 🔴 **Payments: two hard rules, and one changes the product.** First, **since 14 June 2025 the CBUAE confines foreign payment tokens to purchasing virtual assets**, so USDT cannot pay for goods or services. **AURX is a virtual asset, so the token-purchase leg may sit inside that carve-out, but the fee legs cannot** and must settle in fiat or a licensed Dirham Payment Token. Second, **a card rail costs ~4.25% of a USD 20 contribution against the 0.30% assumed in the entry-fee build-up**, which pushes break-even above 8% against a stated 2 to 5% fee. **A card-collected USD 20 SIP is loss-making before any gold is bought.** So: default to account-to-account (🔄 2026-08-10: now AANI Request to Pay plus prefunding, not UAEDDS, per decision 43; local collection accounts abroad), make the minimum ticket rail-dependent, and treat card as a large-ticket convenience. ⚠ **The product also has no blocked-country list**, which will not survive a licence application. ⚠ **No UAE PSP publishes a policy on gold or virtual-asset merchants**; this cannot be closed by desk research and sits alongside the dealer as a commercial unknown. **Banks that do serve VASPs: Zand (also Comtech's bank) and Commercial Bank of Dubai. Approaching a bank pre-licence creates a refusal record visible to other banks, so sequence it.**

29. ✅ **The money question is solved, and it is simpler than the client's plan. VARA's Client Money Rules (Part IV) permit Aurumix to receive and hold customer fiat directly, with no separate CBUAE licence** to collect payment for its own token. Conditions: client money is not owned by Aurumix and sits outside its estate on insolvency; into a designated Client Account **within one calendar day**; UAE clients' money with third-party banks **in** the UAE; overseas clients' money may land abroad but must move to a UAE bank **within 24 hours**. ⚠ **The rule governs location and segregation, not currency.** The product is USD-priced and gold settles on a USD fix, so **run USD as the primary client account with AED alongside**. **Confidence: High.** ⚠ **A separate CBUAE Non-Objection Registration is reportedly required for VARA licensees that custody, transfer or convert stablecoins** (Confidence: Medium). ⚠ **Four jobs, not one provider:** company banking (Aurumix's own money), the client money account (customers'), collection (UAEDDS domestically, overseas collection accounts abroad), and the stablecoin signpost. **Undesigned: when the entry fee stops being client money and moves to the company account.** VARA excludes money "immediately due and payable to a VASP for its own account", so a split rule is permitted but must be written.
30. ✅ **Stablecoin: yes in substance, no in instrument.** The CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation permits a Foreign Payment Token **only** where (a) the issuer is **CBUAE-registered** and (b) it buys a virtual asset. **Both limbs must hold and USDT fails the first.** Only **USDU** (Universal Digital, ADGM, 29 Jan 2026) was found registered; no evidence Tether or Circle applied or were refused. 🔴 **The PTSR binds "No Person", so the merchant is in breach, not just the token issuer.** Two workarounds tested and **ruled out**: off-ramping it yourself fails because conversion is itself a licensable service and **a licensed processor does not cure an unregistered token**; an embedded widget with a referral fee makes the exchange Aurumix's agent. ✅ **What works: the customer converts on their own account at a licensed exchange and pays by bank transfer.** Aurumix publishes a **signpost list**, takes no fee, passes no data, and never touches a token. **The single test: whose bank account sends the money.** Verified from official documentation: **Binance FZE** (direct AED via ADCB, 10 AED fee), **Crypto.com** (10 AED, 0 for Prime), **OKX Middle East** (75 AED). All three enforce own-name accounts themselves. ⚠ **The global on-ramps are unusable here:** Banxa, MoonPay, Transak, Ramp and Alchemy Pay are all regulated in the EU/UK and **none holds any UAE registration**. Banxa works for Kinesis because Kinesis is in Cayman. ⚠ **Market evidence to use on the call:** the protocols that accept USDT for issuance are the unlicensed ones; the licensed ones take bank transfers.

32. 🔴 **VARA prohibits charging anything on the way out, and the decaying spot redemption fee is dead.** **Rule III.E.4, Annex 2, verified verbatim at `rulebooks.vara.ae/rulebook/e-redemptions`: "VASPs Licensed to issue ARVAs shall process and complete redemption requests without charging any fees."** All of III.E is conditional on the opening words "to the extent an ARVA provides... a right of redemption", so redemption is optional. **Do not take that escape.** A published formulaic Buyback Floor will be read as a redemption right in substance, and no redemption right means no price floor, which is the Midas XGZ discount case. ✅ **The replacement is a tenure rebate:** full fee at entry, up to ~1.5% paid back **in grams** at 12 months, FIFO lot accounting, unvested forfeited on early exit. Same four rules as Gold Rewards, so it passes for the same reasons. ✅ **And zero-fee exit is affordable, because an exit does not sell gold.** Grams return to the float and the next buyer consumes them, so the dealer spread is paid **on net outflow only, not gross exits**. In a growing book that is zero. **Make this argument in exactly this form: it converts a regulatory constraint into an argument for the float the client is already being asked to fund.** ⚠ III.E.1 requires **AED payout always**, USD only if disclosed in the whitepaper. ⚠ III.E.3's "reasonable period" is now the **only** pressure valve, since III.E.4 removed the price valve: use a disclosed size-tiered settlement window, never a fee. ⚠ **This also kills the exit half of decision 5.** Custody recovery is now an open conflict with four options and no clean one. 🆕 **LOCKED 2026-08-06, and mapped in `Aurumix_Process_Maps_Redemption_Fee.md` (1 diagram).** Stop looking for a route around it. **Two independent economic reasons, both of which would stand even if VARA permitted a fee:** (a) **an exit fee is the width of the discount band.** Today it breaks the peg in realised value (1 AURX = 1 gram in title, 0.985 g in cash); the day any venue exists it breaks it in price. PAXG trades at NAV because its exit is credible, Midas XGZ restricts its exit and trades at a discount. (b) **If the economics need a redemption fee, the product is not a fully backed redeemable claim.** Under Option A the customer already owns the grams, so charging to receive their own property is incoherent: redemption is a handover, not a service. ⚠ **(b) is policy logic, not a published VARA position. VARA gives no rationale for III.E.4. Never put it in quotation marks in front of the client.**
33. ✅ **The token ledger and the title register are two halves of one ledger, and this is real engineering work nobody has scoped.** **Rule III.B.1.c, verified at primary source**, requires an issuer to "implement mitigating measures to ensure all transactions in the ARVA result in a corresponding transaction in the Reference Asset being legally settled, completed and/or transferred." **There is no such thing as a token movement that is only a token movement.** Three consequences: the register write must **gate the mint** (if title cannot be recorded the token must not exist); a peer-to-peer transfer needs **both parties registered**, so the permissioning argument applies after issuance and not just at issuance; and 🔴 **an open DEX listing is incompatible with Option A**, because any venue must be an approved participant with registered customers. **Tell the client before he builds a listing plan around it.** New invariant owed to the build: `sum(register sub-accounts) = tokens_outstanding`. ✅ **Separately verified and it confirms decision 23 against rule text rather than guidance:** III.C opens *"VASPs Licensed to issue ARVAs **which purport to maintain a stable value**..."*, so the Reserve Asset regime attaches to the stable-value branch only.

46. ✅ **B4 IS CLOSED. The ICS score is `min(Record, Standing) × Retention`, five tiers, and it replaces the 2026-08-11 formula in full (2026-08-12, Abdur's calls throughout).** Full detail in `_draft_ics-scoring.md`; live model in `Aurumix_ICS_Score_Calculator.xlsx`.

**Two facts and one penalty, all readable off the ledger.** **Months** = counted periods over the account's life, *what you contributed* — only paying moves it and **nothing ever reduces it**. **Recent** = counted periods in the trailing 12 months, *whether you are contributing now*. **Sold** = `1 − (grams held now ÷ [grams held 12m ago + grams acquired since])`, *whether you kept it*. Each measures exactly one thing, which is what keeps the score hand-computable.

**Mapped to 0–100 and combined:** `Record` = 4.17/month to month 12 (=50), then 1.04/month to month 60 (=100), capped. `Standing` = Recent × 8.33. `Retention` = 1 below a **30% annual withdrawal allowance**, then linear to 0 at full liquidation. Thresholds **25 / 50 / 75 / 100**; Silver permanent once Confirmed SIP is earned. Perfect saver arrives **month 6 / 12 / 36 / 60**.

**🔴 THE LOAD-BEARING CHOICE IS THE OPERATOR, AND IT IS THE ONE THING NOT TO UNPICK.** `min(A,B) ≥ x` is exactly `A ≥ x AND B ≥ x` — **minimum is the arithmetic of AND.** A weighted sum lets components substitute, which is not a calibration error but what addition *is*, and it is precisely how a ten-year saver who had not paid in a year still scored near the top. The 2026-08-11 draft patched that with a Tenure cap; the patch worked only because the cap silently converted the sum into something else. Taking the lower of two capped numbers does the same job honestly, with no weights to argue about and no substitution possible in either direction. **Bonus property: the score names its own constraint** — if ICS equals Standing, discipline binds; if it equals Record, only time does. And for a saver who never misses, **Standing is always ≥ Record** (proved in §7.4), so a good customer's score is purely their Record and Standing appears only when something has gone wrong.

**Five things deleted, each because the structure now does the work:**
