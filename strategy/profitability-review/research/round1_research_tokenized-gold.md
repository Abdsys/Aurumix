# Tokenized Gold as a Business: Market, Margins, and How It Compares with Digital Gold (Oct 2026)

**Confidence key:** (H) = primary or attested source · (M) = reputable secondary source or aggregator · (L) = our own estimate or a single weak source

## 1. Bottom line

- **Tokenized gold is a crypto trading and collateral product of about $5bn. Retail savers are not its market.** It has about 478k holders worldwide. One UAE digital-gold app (OGold) has 1.5m users on its own.
- **Issuance fees are very small.** XAUT and PAXG each earn an estimated USD 2–6m a year in mint and redeem fees on USD 1.8–3bn of supply (L). Both run as side products inside businesses that make their money on stablecoin reserves.
- **In the tokenized chain, exchanges keep the margin (spot trading plus gold perpetuals), not issuers.** Every small pure-play issuer we found has shut down or stayed tiny.
- **Digital gold earns 20–50x more per purchase.** Spreads run 4–7% versus 0.125–0.25% for tokenized gold. That margin goes to whoever owns the customer relationship. Aurumix's 5% SIP entry fee is priced like digital gold, so its true competitors are OGold and Jar, not XAUT.

## 3. Who holds it

- **Holders:** 478k in total, with 178k monthly active addresses (H, rwa.xyz, 29 Oct 2026). That is up 3.6x year on year (M).
- **Profile:** exchange custody wallets, crypto traders, Tether-ecosystem treasuries and lenders (Antalpha, Ledn), and funds. CEX.IO puts it this way: "PAXG attracts capital that sits; XAUT attracts capital that moves" (M).
- **Issuer access is wholesale only:**
  - XAUT direct purchase needs a 50 XAUT minimum (about $200k) plus a 150 USDT verification fee. US, Canadian and Singaporean persons are barred.
  - Physical delivery comes only as whole bars (about 430 oz), delivered in Switzerland ([TokenizedWire](https://tokenizedwire.com/explainers/paxg-vs-xaut/)) (M).
  - Retail buyers can only get it through exchanges.
- **Compare the retail apps:** OGold has 1.5m users (Jul 2026) ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/corporate-news/ogold-crosses-15-million-users-as-digital-gold-and-silver-investing-surges-1.500621515)) (M). Jar has 35m registered users ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/18/indian-fintech-jar-turns-profitable-by-helping-millions-save-in-gold/)) (M).

**Verdict:** we found no pure tokenized-gold issuer with public evidence that it is profitable on its own (M). A rule of thumb: at 0.25% in and 0.25% out, with supply turning over about once a year at the issuer, every $100m of supply earns about $0.5m in fees (L). A regulated issuer probably needs $0.5–1bn of supply before fees cover its fixed costs (L).

## 5. Infrastructure plays (relevant to CG's "protocol" idea)

- **Securitize, the category leader, is close to break-even.**
  - Revenue was $18.8m in 2024 and $55.6m in the first nine months of 2025 ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/01/29/tokenization-firm-securitize-reports-841-revenue-growth-as-it-prepares-to-go-public)) (H).
  - Q1 2026 revenue was $19.5m, with a **net loss of $7.9m** and adjusted EBITDA of $0.8m ([Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/securitize-revenue-rises-39-ahead-160200756.html)) (M).
  - It has $3.4bn of tokenized AUM. Its SPAC deal values it at $1.24bn, and it guides to $110m revenue and $32m adjusted EBITDA for 2026 (M).
  - Its money comes from fund managers through asset servicing and tokenization fees, not from commodities.
- **Tokenization-as-a-service pricing:** $50–100k setup, $5–25k a year for transfer-agent services, and 1–3% when distribution is included ([TokenizeStartup](https://tokenizestartup.com/platforms/tokenization-platform-fees/)) (L).
- **SafeGold (India's B2B2C gold infrastructure behind PhonePe, Tanishq and banks):** FY25 throughput was ₹6,867 Cr against gold cost of ₹6,809 Cr, a gross margin of about 0.8%. **EBITDA was ₹2 Cr, about 0.03%** ([Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/safegold-clocks-rs-6867-cr-in-gold-transactions-in-fy25-turns-ebitda-positive-10814304)) (M). The infrastructure layer of gold earns a very thin margin.
- **WGC and BCG's "Gold as a Service" and Standard Gold Unit (19 Mar 2026)** propose shared industry rails for custody, reconciliation and redemption ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/world-gold-council-framework-tokenized-gold)) (M). Any new protocol would compete with the industry body itself.
- **The white-label model that works is Paxos's PYUSD,** because it earns interest on cash reserves. Gold pays no interest, so there is no reserve income to share.

|---|---|---|

| What it's used for | Hedging or trading inside crypto, collateral, settlement | Habit saving, SIPs, gifting, converting to jewellery |
| Ticket size | $200k minimum direct from Tether; any size on exchanges | ₹10 a day (Jar) (M). UAE average purchase is Dh700, and 64% of buyers spend under Dh500 ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/digital-gold-rush-uae-invest-through-apps)) (M) |

| Customer cost | 0.125–0.25% mint/redeem, plus about 0.1% exchange spread or fee | 4–7% buy-sell spread plus 3% GST in India ([IPO Market](https://www.ipomarket.in/news/digital-gold-india-paytm-phonepe-tanishq)) (M). UAE spreads are not disclosed |
| Velocity | About 16x market cap traded per quarter | Buy and hold; withdrawals jump when trust breaks |
| Regulation | PAXG under OCC; XAUT under El Salvador's CNAD | India: unregulated. SEBI's warning on 8 Nov 2025 tripled withdrawals ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/11/10/sebi-warning-digital-gold-investment-risks/)) (M). UAE: DMCC, bank-led, VARA |

## 7. What this means for Aurumix

- **The token itself will not make money.** At a few tens of millions in AUM, ERC-20 issuance fees are a rounding error. They do not justify the cost of VARA licensing, attestations and an open-chain setup unless the token is listed and traded.
- **Aurumix is a digital-gold business that happens to use a token.** Its 5% entry fee and 0.75% B2B fee should be benchmarked against OGold, Jar and SafeGold. OGold already has 1.5m users, a gold Mastercard, SIPs, nominees, an annual gold yield of about 3%, and an Aspora partnership aimed at the Indian diaspora (M). Aurumix's current feature set does not set it apart.
- **Margin follows distribution.** Without a channel partner (exchange houses, remittance apps, employers, banks), customer acquisition cost will consume the spread. This matches the existing Monte Carlo finding that zero paths pay back without B2B partners.
- **Building the protocol or infrastructure layer is the lowest-margin seat in this market.** SafeGold earns about 0.8% gross; Securitize is losing money; the WGC is building shared rails.
- **The money is in adjacent products:** jewellery and branded products, credit, and card spend. The issuance itself earns little.
- **Price risk now affects retention.** Gold is down 27% from its January peak, so recent SIP buyers are sitting on losses. That puts pressure on a 6-payment gate that only 53.5% of accounts currently pass.
