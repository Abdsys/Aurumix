What is this?

This Product Disclosure Statement outlines an 18-month leasehold interest in
Hottathanafantasy, a New Zealand thoroughbred. Evolution Stables has secured a 5%
leasehold stake in the horse and is making fractional participation available to investors
through its digital-syndication model.

Who is this for?

Individuals seeking exposure to racehorse ownership in a simplified, digital format--no prior
experience required.

How does it work?

You lease a share of our 5% stake in Hottathanafantasy. Your one-time fee covers all costs for
the term. You receive 75% of any revenue generated from racing, sponsorship, or commercial
activity, proportional to your share.

Structure
The 5% leasehold interest in Hottathanafantasy is:
  Split into 20 digital tokens (each representing 0.25%)
  Fixed for the term 1 Jan 2026 to 30 June 2027
  Fully managed by Evolution Stables
  Digitally issued and administered via Tokinvest (regulated by VARA, Dubai)

guarantees, and you may not recover your original investment.

4. Revenue Streams

Your share in the Hottathanafantasy Syndicate gives you access to potential earnings
generated by the horse during the lease period. While returns are variable and not guaranteed,
your investment entitles you to a portion of all eligible income the horse may produce.

How Distributions Work

All revenue generated by Hottathanafantasy during the lease period is paid by the horse's
owner to Evolution Stables as the leaseholder. Under the lease arrangement, 75% of gross
revenue is allocated to the syndicate (token holders), with the remaining 25% retained by the
owner.

Distributions to token holders are made pro rata according to each participant's shareholding.
All distributions are made in NZD and processed via the Tokinvest platform, which may deduct
applicable transaction or platform fees.

5. Ongoing Costs and Expenses

There are no ongoing costs. Your upfront lease fee is all-inclusive. Evolution Stables manages
all expenses related to the horse throughout the lease period. You will not be asked to
contribute additional funds, regardless of race schedule, training demands, or location.

6. Minimum Amount

The minimum investment is 0.25%, representing one digital token in the 5% leasehold stake
secured by Evolution Stables. Each 0.25% token costs $280 NZD. Investors may purchase
multiple tokens, subject to availability.

8. Insurance

Evolution Stables has not secured mortality insurance for the 5% leasehold stake in
Hottathanafantasy . Because this is a fixed-term leasehold interest and not an equity stake, the
primary risk to the investor is the loss of the initial lease fee, rather than the capital value of the
horse. In the event of the horse's death, the lease will terminate, and unspent funds will be
refunded pro-rata as outlined in Section 15.

9. Valuation

No independent valuation of the horse's capital value has been undertaken for this offering.
The pricing of the tokens is based entirely on the wholesale operational cost of leasing the
horse ($70 per 1% per month) over the 18-month term, rather than the intrinsic capital value of
the bloodstock.

12. Risk Disclosure

Participation in racehorse syndication is highly speculative and carries significant risks.
  Performance Risk: The horse may not perform well, may not win prize money, and may

      not generate any revenue.
  Health Risk: The horse may suffer injury, illness, or death, preventing it from racing.
  Liquidity Risk: Digital tokens represent a fixed-term lease. While secondary trading may

      be available on the Tokinvest platform, there is no guarantee of an active market or that
      you will be able to sell your tokens.
  Capital Loss: You may lose the entirety of your initial investment.

13. Responsible Investment

Evolution Stables holds itself and its partners to the highest standards of care. Animal welfare
remains central to all decisions made during the lease. All training, spelling, and veterinary
decisions are made by licensed professionals. All post-racing arrangements are the

14. Legal Structure & Governance

The Syndicate is governed by the Syndicate Agreement and the Rules of Racing administered
by New Zealand Thoroughbred Racing (NZTR). Evolution Stables acts as the Manager.
Tokenholders possess a leasehold right to revenue but possess no voting, operational, or
governance rights over the horse or its racing career.

15. Termination

The lease naturally terminates on 30 June 2027. It will terminate earlier if the horse is sold by
the owner, retired, injured, or otherwise permanently unable to race. In the event of early
termination, leaseholders will receive a pro-rata refund of their original lease fee based on the
number of full months remaining in the term. If the horse is sold, leaseholders will receive a
buyout calculated at 3x the remaining lease value, distributed pro-rata.
