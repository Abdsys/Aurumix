## 1. Aurumix in brief

Aurumix lets you save in real gold from USD 20 a month. Each gram is held for you in a vault in Dubai. You receive one AURX token for every gram you own.

**What you get:**

- **Real gold by the gram.** 99.99% pure bars from LBMA-accredited refiners, serial-numbered and held in Dubai.
- **Ownership outside Aurumix.** A trust holds the gold for AURX holders, apart from Aurumix's own assets.
- **An easy way in.** A monthly plan from USD 20, plus one-off purchases.
- **An easy way out.** Sell back for cash at the next gold price. No exit fee. No storage fee.
- **Benefits for steady saving.** Your Investor Conviction Score (ICS) tracks how regularly you save. A higher score gives better terms.
- **Services on top.** A credit line, a Gold Card and family transfers, open to every customer.

Holding AURX earns no payments. Steady saving changes only the terms you get on Aurumix's services.

## 2. The problem: saving in gold today

Millions of Gulf households, especially South Asian families, save in gold every month. Few routes serve them well.

| Route | Where it falls short |
|---|---|
| Jewellery | Making charges add to the price. Resale is marked down. No ownership record |
| Coins and small bars | High mark-ups at small sizes. You store them yourself. No committed buyer |
| Bank gold accounts | Usually unallocated. The bank owes you gold, but no bars are held for you |
| Most gold tokens | Built for traders. High effective minimums. No savings plan |

## 3. How Aurumix works

### 3.1 The gold

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Metal | Gold, 99.99% pure |
| Source | Refiners accredited by the London Bullion Market Association (LBMA) |
| Form | Serial-numbered bars. 100 g bars at launch, moving to 1 kg bars as volumes grow |
| Storage | Allocated bars in a Dubai vault, separate from any gold Aurumix owns |
| Checks | An independent assayer checks each bar's serial number, weight and purity on arrival |
| Price | The LBMA Gold Price, per gram [1] |

### 3.2 One token, one gram

One AURX always equals one gram of gold. If your app shows 7.8200 AURX, you own 7.8200 grams.

Aurumix never takes fees out of your grams. Your balance changes only when you buy, sell, receive or send gold.

### 3.3 What you own and who holds it

You own **your share of identified, serial-numbered bars**, recorded to the fraction of a gram. These bars are held only for customers.

A separate trust in the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) is the legal owner of the bars. It holds them for whoever holds AURX at any moment. The trust is designed to keep customer gold away from Aurumix's creditors (section 16.3).

### 3.4 Who does what

Aurumix is the issuer. It takes in customer money, buys gold, issues AURX, runs the buyback and publishes reports. Licensed partners do the lending and issue the card.

### 5.2 Getting started

[DIAGRAM: WP-19 getting-started]

Aurumix accepts bank money only. It does not accept cryptocurrency or stablecoins.

### 5.3 How you pay

Aurumix never pulls money from your account. You always send it.

- **One tap a month.** A payment request arrives in your banking app through AANI, the UAE's instant payment system [2]. Approve it with one tap. If you skip it, it expires with no fee.
- **Prefund.** Send one transfer to cover several months. Aurumix draws each month's amount on your date. The unused balance stays yours.

### 5.4 What happens when you pay

Every purchase follows one order: **money, then title, then token.** If the gold cannot be placed in your name, no token is created.

- The price is the first LBMA Gold Price after your money clears. Nobody chooses it.
- The entry fee becomes Aurumix's money only once the gold is yours.
- Aurumix aims to finish within 24 hours of your money clearing.

### 5.5 A worked example

John pays USD 75 into his monthly plan. He has no tier yet, so his entry fee is 5%.

| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Payment | USD 75.00 |
| Entry fee (5%) | USD 3.75 |
| Goes to gold | USD 71.25 |
| Gold price at the next fix | USD 109.31 per gram |
| Gold and AURX John receives | **0.6518 g / 0.6518 AURX** |

A year later John reaches the Gold tier. His fee falls to 4.2%, so the same USD 75 buys 0.6573 g at the same price.

The entry fee is the only charge. Aurumix adds no mark-up to the LBMA price.

### 5.7 The gold float

Dealers sell whole bars. A 100 g bar is worth about USD 11,000, but savers buy fractions of a gram. The **gold float** bridges the gap.

- It is a small stock of bars Aurumix buys with its own money.
- Your grams move from the float into the trust the same day you buy.
- Gold sold back refills it, so most exits never need a dealer sale.

The float never counts as backing for AURX. Aurumix carries its gold price risk.

## 6. Selling your gold back

You can sell any amount back to Aurumix for cash at any time. Aurumix does not deliver physical gold.

- **Price:** the next LBMA Gold Price after your request.
- **Fee:** none.
- **Payment:** to your own bank account, in AED, or USD where available.

Checks run before the price is set, including that the grams are not pledged to a loan.

*Example: Sophie sells 10 g at USD 110.00 per gram. She receives USD 1,100.00. Nothing is deducted.*

[DIAGRAM: WP-06 buyback-sequence]
Sequence diagram. Participants: Saver, Aurumix, Token ledger, DIFC trust, Gold float, Saver's bank. Steps: sale requested, checks run (identity, sanctions, residence, bank name, no pledge), price set at the next LBMA fix, AURX burned, grams return to the float, cash paid to the saver's own bank. Note: "No exit fee. No physical delivery."

### 6.1 How long it takes

| Sale size | Paid within |
|---|---|
| Small | The next business day |
| Medium | Up to three business days |
| Large | Up to five business days, possibly in stages |

Size bands are in the app. With an active Gold Card, a sale waits 48 hours so card payments can settle.

### 6.2 If many people sell at once

- New purchases cover most sales, through the float.
- Sales pause only if the gold market closes, the vault fails, or an event outside anyone's control occurs. A cash shortage is never a reason.
- If Aurumix could not pay cash, you would still own your gold in the trust.

## 7. The Investor Conviction Score

### 7.1 What the score does

The ICS runs from 0 to 100. It measures how you save, never how much. A saver paying USD 20 a month and one paying USD 2,000 reach the top tier on the same day.

Your tier sets your terms. The amount you hold sets only their size, such as your credit limit.

### 7.2 The first six months

Your score starts after **six monthly payments in a row** of at least USD 20. This is **Confirmed SIP**.

- It is permanent. You start at Silver (score 25) and never fall below it.
- Before it, you can still buy, sell, and use credit and the card at base terms.

[DIAGRAM: WP-07 the-first-six-months]
A six-step countdown (months 1 to 6, app shows "4 of 6") leading to a door marked "Confirmed SIP, permanent". Behind it, a dial starts at 25 (Silver). A side path shows a miss before month 6 sending the counter back to 1, noted "your gold is untouched".

### 7.3 How the score works

Three measures feed the score:

| Measure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Record** | Months paid since your six-month run began. Reaches 50 after year one and 100 after five years. Never goes down |
| **Recent** | How many of the last 12 months you paid |
| **Kept** | How much of your gold you still hold compared with a year ago |

[DIAGRAM: WP-22 ics-score-formula]
Left-to-right flow of 5 boxes: Record and Recent feed "take the lower", then "reduce only if over 30% of gold left this year" (fed by Kept), then "score 0 to 100", then "tier".

- **Any amount from USD 20 counts the same.** Lowering your payment is free.
- **You have a 5-day grace period.** A missed month stays missed.
- **AURX moved to your own wallet counts as gold leaving.**
- **The score is tied to you.** It does not move with your tokens.

### 7.4 The tiers

| Tier | Score | What it takes | Earliest month |
|---|---|---|---|
| No tier | None yet | Confirmed SIP not yet reached | |
| **Silver** | 25 | Six payments in a row | 6 |
| **Gold** | 50 | 12 months paid, and 6 of the last 12 | 12 |
| **Platinum** | 75 | 36 months paid, and 9 of the last 12 | 36 |
| **Sovereign** | 100 | 60 months paid, 12 of the last 12, and no more than 30% sold in the last year | 60 |

Thresholds are fixed, so other customers cannot push you down. Your tier updates once a month.

[DIAGRAM: WP-08 tier-ladder]
Five-rung ladder: No tier, Silver (month 6), Gold (12), Platinum (36), Sovereign (60). Each rung shows months paid and paid months in the last 12. Side note: "the amount you save plays no part".

### 7.5 What a missed month costs

A missed payment lowers your Recent count for 12 months, then drops out. Mark has reached Sovereign:

| Months missed in a row | Mark's tier |
|---|---|
| 1 to 3 | Platinum |
| 4 | Gold |
| 7 or more | Silver (the floor) |

When Mark resumes, his record is still there. About nine steady months restore Platinum. A new saver needs 36.

**A lower score changes your benefits only. It never touches your gold.** Your grams fall only when you sell or send gold, or when gold is sold to repay a loan (section 9.4).

[DIAGRAM: WP-09 a-missed-month]
24-month timeline for Mark. Months 1 to 4 missed: tier steps down from Sovereign to Platinum, then Gold. Payments resume at month 5 and the tier steps back up. A flat line below shows his grams unchanged throughout.

If a rule stops Aurumix accepting your money, for example because you become resident in India, your account pauses. You keep your gold, your score freezes, and existing credit runs to its end with no new draws.

## 9. Credit and the Gold Card

### 9.1 One credit line, two ways to use it

One gold-secured credit line lets you use your gold's value without selling it:

- **Cash:** draw money to your bank account.
- **Card:** spend with the Gold Card, which draws on the same line.

[DIAGRAM: WP-11 one-credit-line-two-ways]
One pool, "gold-secured credit line", on top of pledged grams, with two outlets: "cash to your bank" and "Gold Card spend". Beneath, four roles: licensed lender (owns the loans), card issuer (issues the card), card processor (approves each tap), Aurumix (app, valuation, servicing).

### 9.2 Your borrowing limit

**Limit = eligible grams × gold price × your loan-to-value (LTV).**

- **Eligible grams** are held at least 90 days and not already pledged.
- **LTV** is 40% for everyone, rising to 50%, 65% and 80% at Gold, Platinum and Sovereign.
- **Your ratio is set when you open the line** and reset at an annual review, never below 40%.

Emma holds 100 g, all eligible, at USD 109.31 per gram:

| Emma's tier | LTV | Limit on USD 10,931 of gold |
|---|---|---|
| No tier or Silver | 40% | USD 4,372.40 |
| Gold | 50% | USD 5,465.50 |
| Sovereign | 80% | USD 8,744.80 |

[DIAGRAM: WP-23 borrowing-limit]
Left-to-right flow of 4 boxes: eligible grams (held 90 days, unpledged) × gold price × LTV by tier = your limit.

Pledged grams stay locked in your account until the loan is repaid, and still count toward your score. The lender shows its costs before you draw.

### 9.3 The Gold Card

Every customer can have the L1 card. Each card payment is checked live against your gold, so a lower gold price lowers your available limit.

[DIAGRAM: WP-24 gold-card-ladder]
Ladder, four rungs. No tier and Silver: L1, standard terms. Gold: L1, 2.0% FX, AED 1,000 ATM. Platinum: L2, 1.5% FX, AED 2,500 ATM. Sovereign: L3, 1.0% FX, AED 5,000 ATM.

A card upgrade follows three months in a row at the higher tier. The card never downgrades, and a tier fall never closes it. Card fees follow your tier on each statement.

### 9.4 If the gold price falls

A lower gold price raises your loan as a share of your pledged gold. Aurumix warns you early and sells only what is needed.

| Loan as a share of pledged gold | What happens |
|---|---|
| Up to 80% | Normal |
| **85%** | An app notice |
| **88%** | A formal notice. You have **14 days** to add gold, repay or add cash |
| **92%** | Aurumix sells just enough gold to return the loan to 88%. You keep the credit line |

Sales are at the LBMA Gold Price, and any surplus is yours. How far gold must fall before a sale, at a full limit:

| Tier | LTV | Fall before a sale |
|---|---|---|
| No tier and Silver | 40% | 57% |
| Gold | 50% | 46% |
| Platinum | 65% | 29% |
| Sovereign | 80% | 13% |

Gold can fall 13% within a year. Borrowing less than your limit gives you more room.

## 10. Gold Rewards

**Gold Rewards is cashback in gold on card spending.** Each month your tier's rate is applied to your Gold Card spend and credited in grams.

| Tier | Rate on card spend |
|---|---|
| No tier and Silver | None |
| Gold | 0.15% |
| Platinum | 0.45% |
| Sovereign | 0.75% |

*Example: Emma is at Platinum and spends USD 800 on her card in March. Her reward is USD 3.60. At USD 109.31 per gram, she receives 0.0329 g.*

How it is funded:

1. **From card revenue.** Merchants pay a small fee per card payment, called interchange. Aurumix's share funds the rewards.
2. **Capped** at the revenue your own card and credit use brings in.
3. **Based on spending and tier only.** Your gold balance plays no part.

Reward grams are yours like any other gold. They do not count as a monthly payment.

Steps: card payment, merchant side pays interchange, issuer passes Aurumix its share, month-end tier rate applied (capped at this cardholder's revenue), reward grams allocated and credited at the next fix. Note: "cashback on card spending, funded by card revenue".

## 11. Family Portfolio and succession

| **Beneficiary Transfer Instruction** | A standing instruction to move gold to named people when a set event happens |

### 11.2 Triggers

| Trigger | Example | When gold moves |
|---|---|---|
| **A date** | A child's 18th birthday | On the date |
| **A condition** | Starting university, marriage | Once the event is shown to have happened |
| **Death** | | After a court grant of probate |

Probate is the court process that confirms who inherits. Aurumix cannot avoid it. Because beneficiaries are verified in advance, the transfer can follow within days of the grant.

Contracted agents must tell customers they are paid. Their share of the entry fee is taken back if the customer sells out within 12 months. Aurumix answers for what they say.

### 12.3 Member referrals

- The reward is paid **once**, in grams, to **both** people.
- It is paid only when the new customer reaches Confirmed SIP on their own account.
- **One level only.** If Anna introduces Ben and Ben introduces Chris, Anna receives nothing for Chris.
- Referrers only share a code. They never place orders or give advice.

The programme runs only once VARA has confirmed it.

## 13. The AURX token

### 13.1 Token facts

### 13.2 Identity at the two doors

Identity is checked when AURX is created and when it is sold back. In between, AURX moves like any ERC-20 token.

AURX may trade on other venues at prices that differ from the gold price. Aurumix does not set those prices or promise any listing.

## 14. Safety and transparency

### 14.1 The backing rule

**Gold held in the trust must always at least equal the AURX in circulation.** The system checks this continuously. Minting stops automatically if the float cannot supply gold, the vault's confirmation lapses, or the title record cannot be confirmed.

### 14.2 The monthly Allocation Report

Every month Aurumix publishes:

- the serial number and assay certificate of every bar,
- total AURX in circulation and total gold held for customers,
- the gold prices used,
- the float balance, shown separately.

[DIAGRAM: WP-17 layers-of-protection]
Protective layers around the saver's gold, inside out: allocated bars, trust terms in the customer agreement, the DIFC trust, an independent title register. Beside it, a monthly Allocation Report icon and a gauge: "gold held ≥ AURX outstanding".

### 14.3 Separation and layers of ownership

Your money sits in a UAE bank client account until the gold is yours. Four layers then record that the gold is held for you:

1. **Allocated bars** with a serial-numbered weight list.
2. **Your customer terms**, which include the trust.
3. **The DIFC trust.** DIFC trust law protects properly settled trust assets from the settlor's insolvency [4].
4. **An independent title register**, kept outside Aurumix's systems.

If one layer is challenged, the others still stand.

### 14.4 If Aurumix winds down

Aurumix keeps a wind-down plan at all times. It never depends on selling customer assets. Holders come forward within a published claim window and are bought out.

### 16.3 Positions that rest on legal reading

These positions rest on Aurumix's reading of the law. They are being confirmed with legal advisers and, where needed, the regulator. None has been tested in court.

| Position | What the design assumes |
|---|---|
| **Ownership moves with the token** | The trust holds the gold for whoever holds AURX, so ownership passes with each transfer |

- Customers get no share of Aurumix's profits.

## 17. Risks

| Risk | What could happen | How it is limited |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold price** | Your AURX falls in value with gold | Nothing removes this. Every trade uses the LBMA price |
| **Borrowing** | A price fall forces a partial sale. At Sovereign with a full limit, a 13% fall is enough | Two notices, 14 days to act, partial sales only. A tier fall never triggers a sale |
| **Legal structure** | A court rejects a reading in section 16.3 | Four ownership layers. Holding can be limited to verified customers |
| **Aurumix failure** | Aurumix fails as a business | Gold in the trust, money in a client account, a wind-down plan |
| **Buyback pressure** | Many sell at once in a falling market | The float absorbs daily sales. Large sales paid in stages |
| **Partners** | The dealer, vault, lender or card issuer fails or leaves | Other accredited refiners allowed. Gold sits in the trust |
| **Card systems** | Payments approved without a live gold check during an outage | Backup approvals are small, capped, with no ATM or cross-border use |
| **Secondary markets** | AURX trades below the gold price elsewhere | The LBMA-price buyback stays open to verified holders |
| **Regulatory change** | New rules affect the product or who can hold it | Upgradeable contract. Accounts pause if eligibility changes |
| **Payments** | Providers change prices, affecting the USD 20 minimum | Missed payments cost nothing. Prefunding offers a second route |
