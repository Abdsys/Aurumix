Key Information Summary

What is this?

This Product Disclosure Statement outlines an 18-month leasehold interest in Prudentia, a New
Zealand thoroughbred. Evolution Stables has secured a 5% leasehold stake in the horse and is
making fractional participation available to investors through its digital-syndication model.

Who is this for?

Individuals seeking exposure to racehorse ownership in a simplified, digital format--no prior
experience required.

How does it work?

You lease a share of our 5% stake in Prudentia. Your one-time fee covers all costs for the term.
You receive 75% of any revenue generated from racing, sponsorship, or commercial activity,
proportional to your share.

Structure
The 5% leasehold interest in Prudentia is:
  Split into 20 digital tokens (each representing 0.25%)
  Fixed for the term 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2027
  Fully managed by Evolution Stables
  Digitally issued and administered via Tokinvest (regulated by VARA, Dubai)
Returns

Returns are variable, based on race performance and commercial revenue. There are no
guarantees, and you may not recover your original investment.

Fees
  $292.50 NZD per 0.25% token (fixed, covers all costs)
  Fees: 7% Tokinvest, 5% Evolution Stables (deducted from upfront payment)
Race Schedule Expectation

Prudentia carries a pedigree built for performance in Australasian racing conditions. Returning to
training in early January 2026, she offers a high-quality ownership experience. Syndicate
members can expect regular updates and potential race entries as she resumes her racing career
shortly after the lease term begins.

Risks

The horse may underperform, suffer injury, or be retired early. Early termination may result in a
pro-rata refund. Limited liquidity--resale may not be immediate or available. You could lose the
full amount of your investment. (See Section 12.)

How to Invest

Applications are made directly through Evolution Stables. Once your allocation is confirmed, it will
be securely recorded and administered via our partner Tokinvest, a licensed and regulated digital
issuance platform. Identity verification is required under AML/CFT laws. (See Section 19 ­
Investment Details.)

Manager

2. About the Horse - Prudentia

Prudentia (NZ) is a New Zealand-bred four-year-old mare who has already recorded a maiden
victory and has competed across a range of distances and track conditions. Her win came over
1400 metres at Tauranga, where she handled testing Heavy conditions to score decisively.

Since breaking her maiden, she has stepped into Rating 65 Benchmark company, continuing her
preparation against stronger opposition. Her race record reflects early progression through
maiden grade and exposure to open competition within New Zealand's domestic programme.
Prudentia is trained by Wexford Stables. Wexford Stables is a name synonymous with excellence
in New Zealand racing history, continuing its legacy under the leadership of Lance O'Sullivan
ONZM and Andrew Scott. Operating out of the world-class facilities in Matamata, Wexford Stables
maintains one of the best strike rates in the country, ensuring Prudentia receives every advantage
possible.

3. Upfront Costs and Expenses

The Prudentia Syndicate is structured as a simple, fixed-cost lease, with no ongoing costs for the
term of the lease. You pay one upfront amount to participate--that's it.

Total Lease Cost: $292.50 per 0.25% token

How This is Calculated:

  Based on the standard syndication model, the underlying operating cost is estimated at $65
      per 1% per month.

  For the 5% leasehold stake, the monthly cost is $325.
  Calculated across the 18-month lease term (1 Jan 2026 - 30 Jun 2027), the Total Wholesale

      Lease Value is $5,850.
  This total value is divided equally into 20 tokens, resulting in a fixed upfront price of $292.50

      per 0.25% token.

This cost includes:
  Training and care
  Syndicate management and reporting
  NZTR registration and compliance
  Tokinvest platform and digital issuance/administration
  All other routine costs associated with the horse for the full term

4. Revenue Streams

Your share in the Prudentia Syndicate gives you access to potential earnings generated by the
horse during the lease period. While returns are variable and not guaranteed, your investment
entitles you to a portion of all eligible income the horse may produce.

Revenue sources may include:
  Prizemoney from official race results
  Sponsorship or endorsement deals
  Digital media rights or content-related income
  Commercial opportunities such as naming rights, merchandising, or syndicate-specific

      promotions

How Distributions Work

All revenue generated by Prudentia during the lease period is paid by the horse's owner to
Evolution Stables as the leaseholder. Under the lease arrangement, 75% of gross revenue is
allocated to the syndicate (token holders), with the remaining 25% retained by the owner.

Distributions to token holders are made pro rata according to each participant's shareholding. All
distributions are made in NZD and processed via the Tokinvest platform, which may deduct
applicable transaction or platform fees (see Appendix A for details).

5. Ongoing Costs and Expenses

There are no ongoing costs.

Your upfront lease fee is all-inclusive. Evolution Stables manages all expenses related to the
horse throughout the lease period. You will not be asked to contribute additional funds, regardless
of race schedule, training demands, or location.

Any deductions from potential revenue (e.g. admin fees, platform costs) are applied only if income
is generated--and are outlined in full in Section 4 and Appendix A.

8. Insurance Information

The horse is insured by the owner for mortality and other risks during the lease term. Leaseholders
are not beneficiaries under the policy.

If the horse dies or is permanently retired during the lease period, the lease ends immediately. In
this case, leaseholders will receive a pro-rata refund of the unused lease fee based on the
remaining lease term.

12. Risk Disclosure

Participation in the Evolution Stables ­ Prudentia Syndicate involves inherent risks. While
Evolution Stables has structured this offering to provide a clear and limited exposure through a
fixed-term lease, you should carefully consider the following:

  Performance Risk: Prudentia may not perform to expectations on the track. Poor race
      results can reduce or eliminate potential revenue.

  Injury or Retirement: The horse could suffer injury, illness, or be retired early. In such cases,
      the lease may terminate early, and you may receive a pro-rata refund of the unused portion
      of your lease fee--but no compensation beyond that.

  Regulatory Risk: NZTR has the authority to suspend or deregister a horse or syndicate.
      The lease will automatically terminate if NZTR accepts a deregistration (Form SR24) for
      Prudentia.

  Forfeiture Risk: Leaseholders may forfeit their rights if they provide false or misleading
      information, fail AML/CFT requirements, or are declared ineligible under the Rules of Racing
      (including NZTR Rule 411(b)). In such cases, participation may be cancelled without refund.

  No Ownership Rights: Leaseholders have no legal ownership of the horse and no
      entitlement to insurance proceeds or decisions regarding the horse's future.

  No Guaranteed Returns: Revenue is variable and cannot be predicted. You may not receive
      any distributions and may not recover your original investment.

  Liquidity Limitations: Resale of any lease interest is only permitted in accordance with
      Tokinvest's rules.

You should not invest unless you can afford to lose the full amount of your lease fee. We
recommend you seek independent financial or legal advice before participating.

15. Termination & Forfeiture

The Syndicate and each leaseholder's participation will end on 30 June 2027.

Early Termination:
  The lease will terminate if Prudentia is retired, deregistered, or otherwise made ineligible to

      race under the Rules of Racing.
  A lease will also be deemed terminated if the registered owner or Syndicate Manager

      submits, and NZTR accepts, Form SR24 deregistering the horse.
  NZTR may, at its discretion, suspend or cancel the Syndicate in accordance with the Rules

      of Racing.

Forfeiture:
Leaseholders may forfeit their rights in the Syndicate if they:
  Provide false or misleading information during the application process; or
  Are declared ineligible to participate under the Rules of Racing, including NZTR Rule 411(b).
In the event of forfeiture, the Syndicate Manager may cancel the leaseholder's participation in the
Syndicate in accordance with NZTR requirements.
