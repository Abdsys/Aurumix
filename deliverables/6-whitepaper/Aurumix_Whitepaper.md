## 1. Aurumix in brief

Aurumix lets you save in real gold from USD 20 a month. Each gram is held for you in a vault in Dubai. You receive one AURX token for every gram you own.

- **Real gold by the gram.** 99.99% pure bars from LBMA-accredited refiners.
- **Ownership outside Aurumix.** A trust holds the gold for AURX holders.
- **An easy way in.** A monthly plan from USD 20, plus one-off purchases.
- **An easy way out.** Sell back for cash at the next gold price.
- **Benefits for steady saving.** Your Investor Conviction Score (ICS) rewards regular saving with better terms.
- **Services on top.** A credit line, a Gold Card and family transfers.

## 2. The problem: saving in gold today

Millions of Gulf households, especially South Asian families, save in gold every month. Few routes serve them well.

| Route | Where it falls short |
|---|---|
| Jewellery | Making charges add to the price. Resale is marked down |
| Coins and small bars | High mark-ups at small sizes. You store them yourself |
| Bank gold accounts | Usually unallocated. No bars are held for you |
| Most gold tokens | Built for traders. No savings plan |

## 3. How Aurumix works

### 3.1 The gold

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Metal | Gold, 99.99% pure |
| Source | Refiners accredited by the London Bullion Market Association (LBMA) |
| Form | Serial-numbered bars, checked by an independent assayer on arrival |
| Storage | Allocated bars in a Dubai vault, separate from any gold Aurumix owns |

### 3.2 One token, one gram

One AURX always equals one gram of gold. If your app shows 7.8200 AURX, you own 7.8200 grams. Aurumix never takes fees out of your grams.

### 3.3 What you own and who holds it

You own your share of identified, serial-numbered bars, recorded to the fraction of a gram. A separate trust in the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) is the legal owner of the bars. It holds them for whoever holds AURX, away from Aurumix's creditors.

### 3.4 Who does what

Aurumix issues AURX, buys the gold and runs the buyback. Licensed partners do the lending and issue the card.

[DIAGRAM: WP-19 getting-started]

Aurumix accepts bank money only. It does not accept cryptocurrency or stablecoins.

### 5.3 How you pay

Aurumix never pulls money from your account. Each month you approve a one-tap request in your banking app through AANI, the UAE's instant payment system [2]. Or you prefund several months in one transfer.

[DIAGRAM: WP-20 two-ways-to-pay]

### 5.4 What happens when you pay

Every purchase follows one order: money, then title, then token. The price is the first LBMA Gold Price [1] after your money clears. Aurumix aims to finish within 24 hours.

[DIAGRAM: WP-04 purchase-sequence]
Sequence diagram. Participants: Saver, Saver's bank, client account, Aurumix, Gold float, DIFC trust, Token ledger. Steps: saver approves payment, money lands in the client account, price set at the next LBMA fix, grams move from float to trust, AURX minted, fee moves to Aurumix, ICS month recorded. Note: "money, then title, then token".

### 5.5 A worked example

John pays USD 75 into his monthly plan. He has no tier yet, so his entry fee is 5%.

| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Payment | USD 75.00 |
| Entry fee (5%) | USD 3.75 |
| Goes to gold | USD 71.25 |
| Gold price at the next fix | USD 109.31 per gram |
| Gold and AURX John receives | **0.6518 g / 0.6518 AURX** |

At the Gold tier his fee falls to 4.2%, so the same USD 75 buys 0.6573 g.

### 5.6 Large purchases

Larger purchases need more checks. Very large buyers can purchase a whole bar at a quoted price.

[DIAGRAM: WP-21 purchase-size-ladder]

### 5.7 The gold float

Dealers sell whole bars, but savers buy fractions of a gram. The gold float is a small stock of bars Aurumix buys with its own money to fill small purchases. It never counts as backing for AURX.

[DIAGRAM: WP-05 gold-float-cycle]
A loop. The dealer sells a whole bar into the float. Purchases draw small amounts into the trust, each becoming AURX. Buybacks return grams to the float. When it runs low, Aurumix buys another bar. Label: "Aurumix's own gold, never counted as backing".

## 6. Selling your gold back

You can sell any amount back to Aurumix for cash at any time, at the next LBMA Gold Price. There is no fee. Cash goes to your own bank account, and Aurumix does not deliver physical gold.

*Example: Sophie sells 10 g at USD 110.00 per gram. She receives USD 1,100.00.*

[DIAGRAM: WP-06 buyback-sequence]
Sequence diagram. Participants: Saver, Aurumix, Token ledger, DIFC trust, Gold float, Saver's bank. Steps: sale requested, checks run (identity, sanctions, residence, bank name, no pledge), price set at the next LBMA fix, AURX burned, grams return to the float, cash paid to the saver's own bank. Note: "No exit fee. No physical delivery."

### 6.1 How long it takes

Most sales are paid the next business day. Large sales take up to five business days and may be paid in stages.

### 6.2 If many people sell at once

New purchases cover most sales through the float. Sales pause only if the gold market closes or the vault fails, never for lack of cash. Your gold stays yours in the trust either way.

## 7. The Investor Conviction Score

### 7.1 What the score does

The ICS runs from 0 to 100. It measures how steadily you save, never how much. A saver paying USD 20 a month and one paying USD 2,000 reach the top tier on the same day.

### 7.2 The first six months

Your score starts after six monthly payments in a row of at least USD 20, called Confirmed SIP. It is permanent. You start at Silver (score 25) and never fall below it.

[DIAGRAM: WP-07 the-first-six-months]
A six-step countdown (months 1 to 6, app shows "4 of 6") leading to a door marked "Confirmed SIP, permanent". Behind it, a dial starts at 25 (Silver). A side path shows a miss before month 6 sending the counter back to 1, noted "your gold is untouched".

### 7.3 How the score works

| Measure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Record** | Months paid since your six-month run began. Never goes down |
| **Recent** | How many of the last 12 months you paid |
| **Kept** | How much of your gold you still hold compared with a year ago |

[DIAGRAM: WP-22 ics-score-formula]
Left-to-right flow of 5 boxes: Record and Recent feed "take the lower", then "reduce only if over 30% of gold left this year" (fed by Kept), then "score 0 to 100", then "tier".

Any amount from USD 20 counts the same. The score is tied to you and does not move with your tokens.

### 7.4 The tiers

| Tier | Score | What it takes | Earliest month |
|---|---|---|---|
| No tier | None yet | Confirmed SIP not yet reached | |
| **Silver** | 25 | Six payments in a row | 6 |
| **Gold** | 50 | 12 months paid, and 6 of the last 12 | 12 |
| **Platinum** | 75 | 36 months paid, and 9 of the last 12 | 36 |
| **Sovereign** | 100 | 60 months paid, 12 of the last 12, and no more than 30% sold in the last year | 60 |

[DIAGRAM: WP-08 tier-ladder]
Five-rung ladder: No tier, Silver (month 6), Gold (12), Platinum (36), Sovereign (60). Each rung shows months paid and paid months in the last 12. Side note: "the amount you save plays no part".

### 7.5 What a missed month costs

Mark has reached Sovereign. If he misses 1 to 3 months in a row he drops to Platinum, and after 4 he drops to Gold. When he resumes, about nine steady months restore Platinum.

**A lower score changes your benefits only. It never touches your gold.**

[DIAGRAM: WP-09 a-missed-month]
24-month timeline for Mark. Months 1 to 4 missed: tier steps down from Sovereign to Platinum, then Gold. Payments resume at month 5 and the tier steps back up. A flat line below shows his grams unchanged throughout.

If you become resident in India, your account pauses. You keep your gold.

## 9. Credit and the Gold Card

### 9.1 One credit line, two ways to use it

One gold-secured credit line lets you use your gold's value without selling it. Draw cash to your bank, or spend with the Gold Card.

[DIAGRAM: WP-11 one-credit-line-two-ways]

### 9.2 Your borrowing limit

Your limit is your eligible grams (held at least 90 days) times the gold price times your loan-to-value (LTV). Emma holds 100 g at USD 109.31 per gram:

| Emma's tier | LTV | Limit on USD 10,931 of gold |
|---|---|---|
| No tier or Silver | 40% | USD 4,372.40 |
| Gold | 50% | USD 5,465.50 |
| Sovereign | 80% | USD 8,744.80 |

[DIAGRAM: WP-23 borrowing-limit]
Left-to-right flow of 4 boxes: eligible grams (held 90 days, unpledged) × gold price × LTV by tier = your limit.

The lender shows its costs before you draw.

### 9.3 The Gold Card

Every customer can have the L1 card. Each payment is checked live against your gold.

[DIAGRAM: WP-24 gold-card-ladder]
Ladder, four rungs. No tier and Silver: L1, standard terms. Gold: L1, 2.0% FX, AED 1,000 ATM. Platinum: L2, 1.5% FX, AED 2,500 ATM. Sovereign: L3, 1.0% FX, AED 5,000 ATM.

The card never downgrades, and a tier fall never closes it.

### 9.4 If the gold price falls

A lower gold price raises your loan as a share of your pledged gold. Aurumix warns you early and sells only what is needed.

At Sovereign with a full limit, a 13% gold fall triggers a sale. Borrowing less gives you more room.

## 13. The AURX token

### 13.1 Token facts

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name and symbol | Aurumix, AURX |
| Standard | An ERC-20 token on Ethereum [3] |
| Backing | 1 AURX = 1 gram of allocated gold |
| Supply | No fixed cap. Created when gold is allocated, burned when gold is sold back |
| Allocations | None. No team, investor or treasury tokens and no pre-mine |
| Transfers | Open, subject to a blocklist |

### 13.2 Identity at the two doors

Identity is checked when AURX is created and when it is sold back. In between, AURX moves like any ERC-20 token.

[DIAGRAM: WP-16 identity-at-the-two-doors]
Three rooms in a row. "Create": identity check, money in, gold allocated, AURX issued. "Hold and send": tokens move freely between wallets, past a blocklist gate. "Sell back": identity check, AURX burned, cash to own bank. Note: "score, credit and family see only gold in the Aurumix account".

Sending AURX moves ownership of the gold while the bars stay in the vault. Aurumix charges no fee to send, but Ethereum charges its own network fee.

### 13.3 Controls Aurumix holds

| Control | When it is used |
|---|---|
| **Blocklist and freeze** | Stop an address or balance moving, for sanctions, a court order or fraud |
| **Seize and reissue** | Where a court or the law requires, or to restore tokens to their owner |
| **Pause minting** | Automatically, if backing cannot be confirmed |
| **Upgrade** | If the law requires, for example limiting holding to verified customers |

### 13.4 Trading elsewhere

AURX may trade on other venues at prices that differ from the gold price. Aurumix does not set those prices or promise any listing.

## 14. Safety and transparency

### 14.1 The backing rule

**Gold held in the trust must always at least equal the AURX in circulation.** The system checks this continuously and stops minting if it cannot be confirmed.

### 14.2 The monthly Allocation Report

Every month Aurumix publishes each bar's serial number and assay certificate, total AURX against total gold held, and the float balance shown separately.

[DIAGRAM: WP-17 layers-of-protection]
Protective layers around the saver's gold, inside out: allocated bars, trust terms in the customer agreement, the DIFC trust, an independent title register. Beside it, a monthly Allocation Report icon and a gauge: "gold held ≥ AURX outstanding".

### 14.3 Separation and layers of ownership

Your money sits in a UAE bank client account until the gold is yours. After that, four layers record that the gold is held for you: allocated bars, your customer terms, the DIFC trust [4] and an independent title register. If one layer is challenged, the others still stand.

### 14.4 If Aurumix winds down

Aurumix keeps a wind-down plan that never depends on selling customer assets. Holders are bought out within a published claim window.

## 16. Legal and regulatory status

### 16.1 Regulators

| Regulator | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **VARA** (Dubai Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority) | Issuing AURX, this whitepaper and marketing |
| **CBUAE** (Central Bank of the UAE) | Lending and card issuance, through licensed partners |
| **DFSA** (Dubai Financial Services Authority) | The DIFC trust |

### 16.2 Licence

Aurumix intends to apply to VARA for a Virtual Asset Issuance licence [5]. AURX is designed as an Asset-Referenced Virtual Asset (ARVA) in which holders own the gold itself. AURX will be offered only after VARA grants the licence and approves this whitepaper.

[DIAGRAM: WP-25 path-to-launch]
Left-to-right flow of 5 boxes: apply for the VARA licence, licence granted, whitepaper approved, AURX offered, credit and card added once licensed partners are in place. No dates shown.

### 16.3 Positions that rest on legal reading

These positions are being confirmed with legal advisers and, where needed, the regulator. None has been tested in court.

- **Ownership moves with the token.**
- **Gold in the trust is protected if Aurumix fails.**
- **The buyback is a redemption right** under VARA's rules.
- **The trust is not an investment fund.**
- **A lender can take valid security** over a customer's gold.
- **The family features are standing instructions**, not estate or trustee services.

If a reading changes, the design changes with it, most likely by limiting AURX holding to verified customers.

### 16.4 What AURX is

- AURX represents ownership of gold. Holding it earns no payments.
- Customers get no share of Aurumix's profits.
- Benefits depend on saving behaviour and card use, never on the amount of gold held.

## 17. Risks

| Risk | What could happen | How it is limited |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold price** | AURX falls in value with gold | Nothing removes this |
| **Borrowing** | A price fall forces a partial sale | Two notices and 14 days to act |
| **Legal structure** | A court rejects a reading in section 16.3 | Four ownership layers |
| **Aurumix failure** | Aurumix fails as a business | Gold in the trust, a wind-down plan |
| **Buyback pressure** | Many sell at once | The float, and large sales paid in stages |
| **Partners** | The dealer, vault, lender or card issuer leaves | Gold stays in the trust |
| **Card systems** | An outage stops live gold checks | Small, capped backup approvals |
| **Secondary markets** | AURX trades below the gold price elsewhere | The LBMA-price buyback stays open |
| **Regulatory change** | New rules change who can hold AURX | Upgradeable contract |
| **Payments** | Providers raise prices | Missed payments cost nothing |

## Glossary
