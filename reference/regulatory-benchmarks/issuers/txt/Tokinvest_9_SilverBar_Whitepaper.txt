2. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
2.1 Overview and Reason for Public Offer
The DTFW Virtual Asset issue is a commodity-referenced token which represents pass-through
fractional ownership of a specific Reference Asset that confers legal title to the Underlying Asset.
These rights are created via the Issuer holding, upon successful completion of the subscription
process, the Reference Asset on behalf of Tokenholders. It is designed to provide eligible investors
with exposure to revenues generated by price performance of real-world commodity assets, through
conferring tokenised ownership of the warrant that relates to those assets.

The DTFW gives investors the opportunity to undertake fractional investments in the world's most
important commodities with clear oversight of the underlying asset. This differs from investments
which purport to track the change in financial value of such assets without an auditable holding of the
asset itself. This mitigates risks associated with derivative-based tracking, including tracking error and
reliance on intermediary performance, by anchoring token value to a legally identifiable and verifiable
asset.

The Issuer has created the first token backed by a DMCC-issued Tradeflow Warrant as the Reference
Asset. Tradeflow Warrants offer investors the security of knowing that the referenced commodity
asset has been inspected, verified and securely custodied by a world-leading authority and is
protected by a clear enforceable security interest.

Each issuance references a specific Reference Asset and provides tokenholders with a contractual,
time-limited right to participate in the price performance (if any) generated by the Underlying Asset
during a fixed Economic Term.

The Issuer plans to bring additional DTFW tokenised offerings of different Reference Assets that
represent underlying commodity ownership in the future. Any new token offering will represent a
different Reference Asset and necessitate a new instance of the DTFW token and supporting
whitepaper

Token Naming
The Token has been given the short name `DTFW' as it represents an issuance of tokens
fractionalising the Reference Asset; a specific DMCC Tradeflow Warrant which is issued out of Dubai.

Each Token will have its own uniquely identified DTFW name, which describes the underlying
commodity asset that is represented by the warrant.

The token name for this Issuance is `DTFW ­ 1971 Silver Bar'. This would be the name used on the
Issuer's internal platform and would be recorded in the metadata of the smart contract. The on-chain

Once an issuance is completed, the Reference Asset associated with that issuance remains fixed in

2.5 Economic Logic
The economic logic of the Token is as follows:

    · Tokenholders receive blockchain-based access to fractionalised ownership of the Reference
         Asset;

    · the Reference Asset in turn gives legal ownership rights to the Underlying Asset; and
    · the economic value of the Token reflects the market value of the Underlying Asset at any

         given time.

In cases where the Underlying Asset possesses attributes beyond its intrinsic commodity content--
such as artistic, historic or collectible significance--the value of the Token may reflect such attributes
in addition to, and independently of, the prevailing market-traded price of the relevant commodity.

The Issuer does not represent that the Token will necessarily track or correlate with commodity
benchmark prices, and no assurance is given that any numismatic, collectible or non-commodity
value attributed to the underlying asset will be realised upon sale, transfer, or liquidation.

4. REFERENCE ASSET FRAMEWORK

4.1 Overview of the Reference Asset
Each issuance of the Token references an individual defined Reference Asset.

The Reference Asset is not the Underlying Asset itself, but a DMCC Tradeflow Warrant which
provides purchasers with demonstrable legal protection by creating a clear, enforceable security
interest over the Underlying Asset held in DMCC-approved storage facilities. Each warrant is issued
only after the goods have been independently verified, documented, and placed under the control of a
licensed warehouse operator, who becomes legally responsible for safeguarding the inventory. The
warrant serves as a title-linked instrument: it evidences the holder's legal right to the goods and
ensures that the commodities cannot be moved, pledged, or released without the warrant holder's
explicit authorisation.

The value of the Reference Asset is thus derived from ownership of these rights which entitle the
Reference Asset holder, on a contractual basis, to liquidate the Underlying Asset and receive the
proceeds from the liquidation.

Whilst title to the Reference Asset will remain, upon successful completion of the subscription
process, with the Issuer at all times, Tokenholders have contractual ownership and are entitled to the
full liquidation value (net of costs) of the Underlying Asset at the expiry of the Tokens.

4.2 Underlying Asset
The Underlying Asset to which the Reference Asset confers ownership is a 1,971kg pure silver bar
minted in recognition of the 50-year anniversary of the UAE and holder of the Guinness World Record
as the world's largest silver bar by weight.
4.3 Reference Asset Holding Structure
The underlying silver bullion referenced by the ARVA Tokens is represented by an electronic
warehouse warrant issued through the TradeFlow system and held by Tokinvest DMCC.

Upon successful completion of the subscription process and transfer of the relevant warrant,

5. ECONOMIC MECHANICS OF DTFW TOKENS

5.1 Overview
Tokens are fixed-term, price-performance only digital instruments that provide Tokenholders with
contractual economic participation in the purchase and eventual liquidation of an underlying
commodity asset as described by the Reference Asset.
Each issuance of Tokens:

    · has a fixed Economic Term;
    · entitles Tokenholders to receive a final distributions of net liquidation proceeds at the

         completion of that Economic Term; and
    · expires automatically at the end of the Economic Term with no residual value upon completion

         of said distribution.

Tokens do not represent direct ownership of Underlying Asset(s), do not provide periodic yield or
other revenue payment, and do not entitle Tokenholders to any contractual rights beyond the
Economic Term.
5.2 Source of Token Performance
The performance of The Token is directly determined by the economic value of the Underlying Asset
to which the Reference Asset confers ownership rights. As The Token represents a digital
embodiment of the warrant, its value and performance are intrinsically linked to the market value,
condition, and realisable worth of the Underlying Asset recorded within the DMCC Tradeflow system.
Any appreciation or depreciation in the underlying asset--whether driven by market prices, supply
and demand dynamics, or asset-specific factors--will be reflected proportionately in the price of the
token.

5.3 Distribution Mechanics
Token distributions, if any:

    · take place only at the end of the Token Term when the Underlying Asset is liquidated by the
         Issuer;

    · are paid in fiat currency (or stablecoins where permissible) to the Tokenholder's designated
         account on the Issuer's platform; and

    · are allocated pro rata based on Token holdings as at the end of the Token Term.

Entitlement to the distribution depends solely on ownership of Tokens at the end of the Economic
Term.

5.4 Redemption and Delivery of the Reference Asset
Where an investor acquires and holds 100% of all Tokens issued in respect of a Reference Asset in a
single wallet they may, subject to the terms of the specific issuance, elect to take delivery of the
Reference Asset. Upon completion of this process, the investor will cease to hold Tokens and will
instead become the direct owner of the Reference Asset (and in turn the Underlying Asset), with the
rights and obligations attaching to such ownership under the DMCC Tradeflow framework.

Following delivery of the Reference Asset, the Tokens corresponding to that issuance will be
permanently cancelled or burned and will no longer represent any economic or proprietary interest.
No further rights or claims shall subsist under the Tokens once the Reference Asset has been
delivered to, and accepted by, the investor.

Process for Delivery
Upon receipt of a valid delivery request via the Issuer investor platform, the Issuer will verify the
investor's ownership of all outstanding Tokens and confirm satisfaction of any applicable conditions,
including identity verification, regulatory compliance, and settlement of any outstanding fees, costs, or
charges associated with delivery.
Following verification, the Issuer will burn the Tokens and initiate the transfer of the Underlying Asset
within the DMCC Tradeflow system in accordance with Tradeflow rules. From that point onward, the
investor will be recognised as the legal holder of the Underlying Asset under the Tradeflow framework.

5.5 Expiry and End-of-Term Treatment
At a period of no less than two (2) months before the end of the Economic Term, the Issuer (acting as
the registered warrant holder following successful issuance completion) will appoint an independent,
DMCC-approved sale agent to oversee the liquidation process for the Underlying Asset described in
the Tradeflow Warrant.

The liquidation and distribution process operates according to the following sequence:

1. Appointment of Sale Agent

An independent sale agent is appointed to oversee the sale process in accordance with applicable
Tradeflow procedures.

2. Asset Realisation

The Underlying Asset described in the Tradeflow Warrant is sold at prevailing market rates through
appropriate market channels.

3. Settlement of Transaction Costs

Any sale-related transaction costs associated with the liquidation of the Underlying Asset are settled.

4. Determination of Net Proceeds

The remaining proceeds following settlement of applicable transaction costs represent the net
liquidation proceeds attributable to the Token issuance.

5. Distribution to Tokenholders

Net proceeds are credited to Tokenholders via the Tokinvest platform on a pro-rata basis according to
Token holdings at the time of expiry.

6. Token Cancellation

Following completion of the distribution process, all Tokens associated with the issuance are
permanently burned. No Tokens will remain in circulation and Tokenholders will have no further claims
in respect of the issuance.

5.6 Use of Proceeds from Token Issuance
Proceeds from the issuance of the DTFW token, net of fees and commissions as detailed in this
whitepaper, are used, upon successful completion of the subscription process, to acquire full legal title
to the Reference Asset. Through this acquisition, the Issuer assumes ownership and, by extension,
obtains the rights and economic interest in the Underlying Asset represented by the Reference Asset.

This mechanism ensures that token issuance is directly linked to the transfer of Reference Asset
ownership.
Issuer Proceeds
Any profits realised by the Issuer from issuance-related activities (including brokerage, custody,
management, and platform fees) are to be retained as part of its ordinary operating revenue.
These profits will be used to:
    · Maintain and enhance the Issuer's trading and compliance infrastructure;
    · Support ongoing regulatory and audit obligations under VARA;
    · Fund research and development into tokenisation technology and secondary market
         integrations; and
    · Strengthen their capital reserves in line with regulatory capital requirements for VARA-licensed
         entities.

No portion of issuance-related profits will be reinvested into the specific Reference Asset or
distributed back to tokenholders:

5.7 Summary
In summary, Tokens provide:

    · contractual ownership of the Reference Asset;
    · potential returns based on the market value of the Underlying Asset;

6.13 Cashflow Waterfall and Fee Treatment
The financial flows associated with a Token issuance operate as follows:

    1. Subscription Phase - Investor subscription funds are received and held within the Issuer's
         designated client money accounts pending completion of the issuance and reconciliation
         checks.

    2. Reference Asset Acquisition - Upon successful completion of the subscription and
         reconciliation process, subscription proceeds (net of the Primary Issuance Brokerage Fee
         described above) are applied to acquire the Reference Asset associated with the issuance.

    3. Asset-Level Costs - Custody, storage, and insurance arrangements relating to the Underlying
         Asset are arranged by the Issuer for the duration of the Economic Term.
         These asset-level costs are set out in this whitepaper and payable by Tokenholders
         as part of the issuance structure and reflected in the overall pricing of the issuance.

    4. Platform and Token Administration Fees - Certain fees relating to the operation of the Issuer
         platform, token administration, custody of digital assets, or secondary-market transactions
         may apply as disclosed in Section 6.12.
         These fees relate to the operation of the tokenisation platform and digital asset infrastructure
         and are separate from costs associated with maintaining the physical asset.
         Platform-related fees are charged at the token or platform level and are not deducted from
         proceeds distributed to Tokenholders following liquidation of the Underlying Asset.

    5. End-of-Term Distribution - At the conclusion of the Economic Term, the Underlying Asset is
         sold and the net proceeds of sale are distributed proportionally to Tokenholders in accordance
         with their respective Token holdings.

    · support lifecycle events, including minting at the Token Generation Event (TGE) and burning

C.3 DLT Consensus Mechanism
BNB Smart Chain uses a Proof-of-Staked-Authority (PoSA) consensus mechanism under which a
limited set of validators produce and validate blocks. Validator selection is based on staking and
governance processes defined by the BNB Chain protocol.

The design of the token indicate that there are no observable risks of a variance to the rights of token
owners should other DLTs be used in the future.

BNB Smart Chain relies on a limited validator set. While this may increase throughput and efficiency,
it may also introduce concentration risk relative to more decentralised networks. Tokinvest does not
control, operate, or influence BNB Smart Chain validators.

Validators on the BSC chain receive a portion of transaction fees as well as block rewards. BSC
transaction fees are paid in BNB and are typically low and predictable but may fluctuate based on
network demand. They depend on the gas price and the limit used by each transaction.

C.9 Issuance Process and Tradeflow Warrant Registration

For each issuance, the Reference Asset is acquired and registered within the DMCC Tradeflow
system following successful completion of the subscription process.

The operational sequence is as follows:

    1. Subscription Close - the subscription period closes and the Issuer verifies that the minimum
         subscription threshold has been satisfied.

    2. Funds Reconciliation - investor subscription funds are reconciled within the Issuer's
         designated client money accounts and internal issuance records are verified.

    3. Reference Asset Acquisition and Registration - upon successful completion of reconciliation
         checks, the Tradeflow warrant representing the Reference Asset is transferred or registered
         within the DMCC Tradeflow system with Tokinvest DMCC as the warrant holder.

    4. Issuance Controls - prior to token minting, the Issuer confirms that the warrant registration,
         subscription reconciliation, and internal issuance records are consistent.

    5. Token Minting and Allocation - following confirmation of the above checks, tokens
         corresponding to the issuance are minted and allocated to investors' wallets in accordance
         with the issuance documentation.

These steps are intended to ensure that token issuance occurs only after the Reference Asset has
been secured within the Tradeflow registry and reconciliation checks have been completed.

D.10 Maintenance of Reserve Assets
The VARA regulatory framework requires issuers of certain ARVAs to maintain sufficient Reserve
Assets in accordance with Rule III.C.1. of Annex 2 of the VA Issuance Rulebook. In respect of the
DTFW token, the Issuer complies with this requirement by designating the Tradeflow Warrant (the
"Reference Asset") as the Reserve Asset.

Accordingly:

    · the Reserve Asset and the Reference Asset are the same asset (i.e., the Tradeflow Warrant),
         and are held by the Issuer on behalf of Tokenholders;

    · the Issuer maintains sufficient holdings of the Tradeflow Warrant at all times to ensure full
         backing of all issued DTFW tokens on a 1:1 basis by weight;

    · the Tradeflow Warrant represents clear and enforceable legal title to the underlying asset,
         which is insured and securely warehoused in a DMCC-approved facility;

    · the token minting process is strictly controlled to prevent over-issuance, such that DTFW
         tokens are only issued when a corresponding amount of the Tradeflow Warrant is held, and no
         additional tokens may be issued once the Reference/Reserve Assets have been fully
         tokenised.

This structure ensures that the Issuer continuously maintains sufficient Reserve Assets in compliance
with applicable VARA requirements, while safeguarding the rights of Tokenholders.
