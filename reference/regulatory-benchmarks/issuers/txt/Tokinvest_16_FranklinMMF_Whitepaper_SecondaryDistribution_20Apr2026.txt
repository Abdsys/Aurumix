1. KEY FEATURES & CORE DEFINITIONS

This Section summarises the key structural, operational, and economic characteristics of the Product and
defines the principal terms used throughout this document. Capitalised terms introduced in this Section
have the meanings given below and apply throughout.

1.1 Overview
The Product is the Franklin OnChain U.S. Dollar Short-Term Money Market Fund (the "Sub-Fund"), a sub-
fund of Franklin Templeton Investments VCC (the "Company"), a Variable Capital Company incorporated in
Singapore.
The Sub-Fund is a tokenised collective investment scheme providing exposure to a diversified portfolio of
high-quality, short-term, U.S. dollar-denominated money market instruments.
The Product is issued and managed by Franklin Templeton and is made available to investors through a
distribution framework involving Tokinvest as a secondary distributor

1.2 Product Type
The Product is a money market fund structured as a Variable Capital Company (VCC) under Singapore law.
Investor interests are recorded through:

     · a legally recognised shareholder register maintained by the Issuer; and
     · a tokenised or digital register maintained using distributed ledger technology.

Any tokenised representation reflects a record of ownership of fund shares and does not constitute a
separate financial instrument.

1.3 Investment Objective
The Sub-Fund seeks to:

     · provide a high level of current income; and
     · preserve capital and maintain liquidity

by investing primarily in high-quality, short-term, U.S. dollar-denominated debt and money market
instruments.

1.4 Underlying Assets
The Sub-Fund invests in a diversified portfolio of:

     · short-term debt securities;
     · money market instruments;
     · bank deposits and cash equivalents; and
     · government and quasi-government securities, including U.S. Treasury-related instruments.

1.5 Key Parties
The following entities are involved in the structure, management, and distribution of the Product:

     · Issuer (Company): Franklin Templeton Investments VCC
     · Manager: Templeton Asset Management Ltd
     · Sub-Manager: Franklin Advisers, Inc.
     · Registrar and Transfer Agent: Templeton Asset Management Ltd
     · Custodian: DB International Trust (Singapore) Limited
     · Primary Distributor: Pinetree Securities Pte. Ltd., which interfaces directly with the Issuer and

          facilitates subscriptions and redemptions at the fund level
     · Secondary Distributor / Platform Operator: Tokinvest DMCC, which provides investor
          onboarding, order aggregation, execution, and platform access

Tokinvest interfaces with Pinetree as its counterparty and does not interact directly with the Issuer on
behalf of underlying investors.

1.6 Role of Tokinvest
Tokinvest acts solely as:

     · a secondary distributor of the Product; and
     · the operator of the investor platform through which investors access the Product.

Tokinvest does not:

     · issue the Product;
     · manage the underlying assets;
     · determine investment strategy; or
     · guarantee performance or returns.

All investment management, asset selection, and valuation are performed by the Issuer and its appointed
Manager.

1.7 Dealing and Liquidity
The Sub-Fund operates on a daily dealing basis. Subscriptions and redemptions are submitted by
Tokinvest to the Primary Distributor (Pinetree) for execution at the fund level.

Tokinvest applies the following dealing parameters:

     · Tokinvest cut-off: 3:00pm Dubai time (GST)
     · Execution basis: forward-pricing based on the Net Asset Value (NAV) determined after the

          applicable fund-level cut-off

Instructions received after the Tokinvest cut-off are processed in the next dealing cycle.

Due to funding timelines, banking cut-offs, and operational processing across jurisdictions, transactions
are generally submitted on a T+2 basis relative to investor instruction.

Liquidity is therefore subject to:

     · Tokinvest cut-off times and operational processing timelines;
     · funding and settlement requirements (including fiat transfer timing);
     · dealing deadlines applied by the Primary Distributor and Issuer; and
     · any applicable redemption limits, suspension rights, or prevailing market conditions.

While the Sub-Fund offers daily dealing, execution and settlement for investors will reflect the operational
timelines of the distribution chain.

1.8 Valuation
The Sub-Fund is valued on each Dealing Day.
Net Asset Value (NAV) is calculated by the Issuer and forms the basis for all subscription and redemption
pricing. The applicable dealing price is typically available within two Business Days following the relevant
Dealing Day.
1.9 Currency and Foreign Exchange

     · Base currency of the Sub-Fund: USD
     · Tokinvest subscription currency: AED (initially), with additional currencies (including USD)

          expected to be supported

Subscriptions and redemptions made in a currency other than the Sub-Fund's base currency (USD) require
foreign exchange conversion. Tokinvest performs this conversion, and the cost of conversion is borne by
Tokinvest and is not passed on to investors.

Foreign exchange conversion does not, however, eliminate currency risk.
Investors remain exposed to movements in the exchange rate between AED (or any other subscription
currency) and USD between the point of subscription and the point of redemption. The UAE Dirham (AED)
is pegged to the U.S. Dollar, which may reduce, but does not eliminate, this risk.

1.10 Distributions
Distributions, if any, are at the discretion of the Directors of the Company.
As at the date of this document, the Sub-Fund does not intend to make periodic distributions, and returns
are expected to be reflected in NAV performance.

4. SUBSCRIPTION, REDEMPTION & FLOW OF FUNDS

4.1 Overview
Investors access the Product exclusively through the Tokinvest platform. Tokinvest aggregates investor
instructions and submits them to the Primary Distributor (Pinetree) for execution at the fund level.
Tokinvest acts as the sole counterparty to Pinetree in respect of subscriptions and redemptions submitted
through its platform.

4.2 Subscription Process
The subscription process operates as follows:

     1. Onboarding
          Investors complete onboarding, including AML/KYC verification and account setup on the
          Tokinvest platform.

     2. Funding
          Investors deposit funds into their Tokinvest account via approved funding rails.

     3. Instruction
          Subscription instructions are submitted through the platform prior to the applicable cut-off time.

     4. Aggregation
          Tokinvest aggregates investor instructions into a single subscription order.

     5. Execution
          The aggregated order is submitted to Pinetree for execution at the fund level.

     6. Allocation
          Following execution, holdings are allocated to investors based on their respective subscription
          amounts and recorded within Tokinvest's internal systems.

4.3 Minimum Subscription Amount
Subscription amounts are subject to:

     · minimum investment thresholds applied by the Issuer at the fund level; and
     · any minimum subscription amounts determined by Tokinvest for operational or commercial

          purposes.

Tokinvest may apply minimum subscription sizes on a per-order or per-investor basis, which may differ
from the minimum investment specified in the underlying fund documentation.

4.4 Redemption Process
Redemptions follow a corresponding process:

     1. Investors submit redemption instructions via the platform.
     2. Tokinvest aggregates redemption requests.
     3. Instructions are submitted to Pinetree for execution at the fund level.
     4. Proceeds, once received, are credited to investor accounts.

Redemptions are subject to fund-level processing, liquidity conditions, and operational timelines across
the distribution chain.
4.5 Flow of Funds

Subscription:
Investor  Tokinvest Client Money Account  Pinetree  Issuer (Sub-Fund)

     · Investor funds are held in segregated Client Money Accounts (CMAs).
     · Tokinvest transfers aggregated funds to Pinetree.
     · Pinetree executes the subscription with the Issuer.

Redemption:
Issuer (Sub-Fund)  Pinetree  Tokinvest  Investor

     · Redemption proceeds are paid by the Issuer to Pinetree.
     · Proceeds are then transferred to Tokinvest.
     · Funds are credited to investor accounts upon receipt.

4.6 Dealing Cycle
Subscriptions and redemptions are processed on a daily dealing basis at the level of the Sub-Fund.
Tokinvest applies the following dealing parameters:

     · Tokinvest cut-off: 3:00pm Dubai time (GST)
     · Instructions received after the cut-off: processed in the next dealing cycle

Tokinvest applies this cut-off to allow for:

     · order aggregation;
     · funding confirmation; and
     · submission to the Primary Distributor (Pinetree).

All transactions are executed on a forward-pricing basis using the Net Asset Value (NAV) determined after
the applicable fund-level cut-off.

Due to funding timelines, banking cut-offs, and operational processing across jurisdictions, transactions
are typically submitted on a T+2 basis relative to investor instruction, although actual timing may vary.

4.7 Settlement
Settlement is performed through standard fund subscription and redemption processes across the
distribution chain.
Funds are transferred from Tokinvest to Pinetree via:

     · telegraphic transfer (USD); and
     · digital settlement rails (e.g. USDC/USDT), where supported in future, subject to regulatory approval

          and operational readiness.

Settlement timelines are dependent on:

     · funding receipt and banking cut-off times;
     · payment processing timelines; and
     · operational coordination across Tokinvest, Pinetree, and the Issuer.
As a result, settlement will generally occur on a T+2 basis or longer, depending on payment method,
processing conditions, and upstream fund timelines.

4.8 Investor Position
Investors:

     · do not submit instructions directly to the Issuer or Primary Distributor;
     · are represented by Tokinvest in all subscription and redemption activity; and
     · receive allocations and proceeds through the Tokinvest platform.

Tokinvest maintains internal records of investor holdings and transactions within its platform, which are
reconciled against aggregate positions held via Pinetree.

Further details of applicable fees and charges are available through the Tokinvest platform and Terms of
Business.
