secured bond (or bonds) issued by the Bond Issuer and identified by its International Securities Identification

LUXEMBOURG S.C.S. | LuxSE

The underlying bond confers defined contractual rights, including entitlement to principal and any applicable
coupon payments, subject to the terms of the bond documentation. The Token does not constitute the bond

2. TOKEN OVERVIEW

2.1 Overview
The Token provides investors with access to the LCL Opportunities 3 Digital Bond, an on-chain product which
allows investors to participate in the economic performance of senior secured bonds issued by the Bond Issuer
as part of a limited retail programme (included with this submission).
The Token is issued under a legal structure which makes it pari passu and pro rata with all other creditors of the
Bond Issuer in the case of default (see sections 9.5 and 9.8 of the Token Issuer's LRP document included with
this submission).

2.2 Nature of the Token
The Token is an ARVA which references the economic performance of a specific and ISIN identified senior
secured bond issued by the Bond Issuer.

2.3 Role of Tokinvest
Tokinvest acts as a licensed distributor and provides access to the Token through its platform.
Tokinvest enables investors to:

     · purchase the Token;
     · hold the Token within a controlled custody environment;
     · receive cashflows arising from ownership of the Token; and
     · list the Token for sale to other investors on the platform via a secondary market (where available)

2.4 Investment Exposure
Investors gain exposure to the economic performance of the referenced senior secured bond, which has an
attractive fixed quarterly coupon. Returns are achieved through the yield from the referenced bond with
principal returned at the maturity of the bond.

The Token represents fractionalised exposure to the underlying bond and is offered in lower minimum
subscription denominations than other tokens issued by the Token Issuer. In consideration of the additional
structuring and operational costs associated with enabling such fractional access, the coupon payable in respect

Token issuer.

4. PURCHASE, REDEMPTION & FLOW OF FUNDS

4.1 Overview
Tokinvest investors access the Token exclusively through the Tokinvest platform. Tokinvest executes orders on
behalf of investors and interfaces with the Token issuer for purchase and delivery of the Token.

Tokinvest acts as the sole counterparty to the Token issuer on behalf of their investors.

4.2 Token Purchase Process
The purchase process operates as follows:

     1. Onboarding
          Investors complete onboarding, including AML/KYC verification and account setup on the Tokinvest
          platform.

     2. Funding
          Investors deposit funds into their Tokinvest account via approved funding rails.

     3. Instruction
          Purchase orders are submitted through the Token offering page on the Tokinvest investor platform.

     4. Execution
          The purchase order is submitted to the Token Issuer and funds transferred to their fiat account with
          their correspondent institution.

     5. Delivery
          Following execution, tokens are received in the Tokinvest corporate wallet with Hex Trust and then
          transferred to the individual investor wallet and recorded on the Tokinvest CAP table.

4.3 Minimum Purchase Amount
Purchase amounts are:

     · subject to minimum investment thresholds applied by the Token Issuer; and
     · set at EUR10k, USD10k (or currency equivalent) as dictated by the issuance currency of the Token and

          underlying bond.

4.4 Redemption Process
Under normal circumstances redemptions occur only at the expiry date of the Token which is itself dictated by
the terms of the underlying bond:

     1. The Token Issuer makes final payment of coupon and principal to all Tokenholders on its CAP table.
     2. Tokinvest receives the aggregate proceeds for all Tokens held by Tokinvest investors.
     3. Tokinvest distributes proceeds to each investor on their internal CAP table based on Token holdings.
     4. Tokens are transferred out of investor wallets and returned to the Token Issuer
     5. No further rights or distributions are retained by the Token.

However, for specific issuances the Token Issuer may include a Put and/or Call option in the terms of the bond,
as detailed in Section 9.11 of the LRP. A Put option allows the Tokenholder to request early redemption of the
Token whilst a Call option allows the Token Issuer to redeem the Token early and pay principal plus any accrued
interest to the Tokenholder.

For this instance of the Digital Bond, as per sections 18-21 of the Final Terms, the Token Issuer has implemented
a Call option as applicable from 15th May 2028 and a Put option as applicable on 15th May 2028 and 15th May
2029 specifically.

The Token Issuer also reserves the right to redeem the Token early for taxation reasons (unforeseen changes in
tax rules relevant to the Token and/or the Token Issuer) or should a force majeure event occur. Investors are
advised to refer to both documents mentioned in this Section 4.4 for full details and to confirm that either or
both of these terms are applicable.
Outside of the specific events mentioned above Tokens cannot be redeemed on demand and neither Tokinvest
nor the Token Issuer are in any way obliged to redeem or repurchase tokens at any time.

4.5 Flow of Funds
Purchase:
Investor  Tokinvest Client Money Account  Token Issuer  Bond Issuer

     · Investor funds are held in a segregated Client Money Account (CMA).
     · Tokinvest transfers funds to the Token Issuer for each executed order (net of fees).
     · The Token Issuer in turn transfers all proceeds to the Bond Issuer for investment in the underlying bond.

Redemption:
Bond Issuer  Token Issuer  Tokinvest  Investor

     · Redemption proceeds are received by Tokinvest from the Token Issuer
     · Funds are credited to investor accounts on the platform.

4.6 Accrual Start
Coupon accrual commences when the Token Issuer has received and cleared subscription funds from the
investor. Tokinvest will normally remit investor funds to the Token Issuer on the date of purchase (subject to a
cut-off time of 6pm UAE), however such remittances may be subject to operational delays and confirmation
from the Token Issuer may therefore occur on the same or a subsequent business day. Investors can view their
tokens and accrual schedule on the Tokinvest investor platform once Tokinvest has received confirmation from
the Token Issuer.

Purchase orders received after the cut-off are processed on the next business day and the coupon accrual
calculation is adjusted accordingly.

4.7 Investor Token Positions
Investors:

     · do not submit instructions directly to the Token Issuer;
     · are represented by Tokinvest in all purchase order and redemption activity; and
     · receive allocations and proceeds through the Tokinvest platform.
Tokinvest maintains the definitive internal record of their investor holdings and transactions within its platform.
