B.2 VA CHARACTERISTICS, FEATURES & USES
The BREI Token is an Asset-Referenced Virtual Asset (ARVA) representing the contractual economic rights
associated with the Property as described in this Whitepaper (the "Reference Asset").
Key characteristics include:

Economic Rights:
Tokenholders are entitled to a proportional share of:

    · The net rental yield generated by the Property.
         Distribution commences after the first month and accrues on a block-by-block basis, with token
         holders eligible to claim outstanding rental returns at any time, up to and including the corresponding
         block; and

    · The net proceeds of any future sale of the Property, following deduction of agreed costs and fees as
         set out in this White Paper.

The timing and process of the Property sale is determined as set out in Appendix B of this White Paper.

Property Reference Structure:

    · Each BREI Token corresponds to the economic rights associated with a specific Property, as
         constituted by the Reference Asset

    · The Property referenced for this whitepaper is economic contractual rights relating to UK Land
         Registry title WM656558 (the "Property")

    · Tokens are semi-fungible: those linked to the same Property are interchangeable, but tokens linked to
         different properties are not.
    · The Reference Asset for a BREI token cannot be changed under any circumstance.

Property Management:
Tokinvest has appointed the Property Manager to undertake the management of the Property Asset including
the activities set out in Appendix A of this White Paper.

Property Rights Agreement
Tokinvest has entered into a contract with the Asset Owner pursuant to which it acquires the contractual
economic rights associated with the Property, which constitute the Reference Asset referenced by the BREI
Token.

No Ownership Rights:
Ownership of the BREI token does not confer legal or beneficial ownership of the Property. Instead, tokens
represent contractual economic participation rights referenced to the Property, entitling tokenholders to
distributions derived from the Reference Asset in accordance with this Whitepaper.

Governance & Voting:
Tokenholders have no governance rights in respect of the decisions over the management or sale of the
Property. Authority of the Property sale and process for selling the Property is set out in Appendix B.

Trading & Liquidity:
BREI tokens can be traded on Tokinvest FZCO's secondary marketplaces and can be traded on regulated RWA
platforms by whitelisted investors who have passed the required AML/KYC checks.

Use Case:
The BREI token provides investors with blockchain-based access to fractionalised real estate returns,
combining rental yield with potential capital appreciation, underpinned by a regulated issuance framework.

Following the TGE, Tokens corresponding to subscriptions received up to and including the Subscription Period

B.7 VA ISSUANCE STRUCTURE
The BREI Token is issued under a regulated subscription-based issuance model operated by Tokinvest FZCO
pursuant to its VARA licence.

Each issuance follows an all-or-nothing minimum subscription framework, with a defined maximum issuance
size linked to the agreed Property Price. Tokens are issued only where the minimum aggregate subscription
threshold is achieved.

Token Price and Authorised Supply
Each BREI token has a face value of AED 100.

The maximum authorised Token Supply for the issuance is determined at the Token Generation Event (TGE),
being the close of the first Subscription Period at which the Minimum Subscription Amount is achieved, by
reference to the agreed GBP-denominated Property Price, converted into AED using a single spot AED/GBP
exchange rate captured at that TGE. The FX reference rate is sourced from Fixer.io (APILayer).

The resulting AED amount is divided by the fixed Token Price to determine the total authorised Token Supply
for the issuance. This authorised Token Supply is fixed at TGE and may not be increased.
Any subsequent Subscription Periods, if conducted, allocate Tokens from this fixed authorised supply and do
not alter the FX rate, Token Price, or total authorised Token Supply.

Subscription Parameters:
Maximum Subscription Amount
An amount equivalent to the Property Price, converted into AED at TGE. The aggregate value of all Tokens
issued for the issuance (prior to costs and fees) may not exceed this amount.

Minimum Subscription Amount
A minimum aggregate subscription amount equivalent to £5.2 million (or AED equivalent). If this minimum
threshold is not achieved by the close of the final Subscription Period, the issuance does not proceed.

Minimum Investor Subscription Amount
Each investor must subscribe for a minimum of 1,000 BREI Tokens (AED 100,000), or currency equivalent.

Subscription Process
BREI Tokens are offered through an initial Subscription Period of up to 60 days and, where required, no more
than two subsequent Subscription Periods of up to 30 days each, conducted on the Tokinvest platform.
To participate, investors must:

    · complete full AML/KYC onboarding;
    · pre-fund their Tokinvest Client Money Account (CMA); and
    · submit binding subscription instructions through the Tokinvest platform.

Subscription amounts are deducted immediately from an investor's cleared CMA balance and held in
Tokinvest's segregated Client Money Account at Zand Bank.
There are no pre-sales, discounts, or preferential allocations.

In the event of Tokinvest FZCO entering into an insolvency event, tokenholders maintain third party rights

Diagram 1. The Wind Down Plan was approved by VARA as part of Tokinvest's licensing approval. The plan
outlines all the activities and processes that the business will undertake should it become insolvent. A critical
aspect of the plan is to ensure that client assets are at all times protected and secured within named "client
accounts" which will constitute a one of two options depending on if fiat or digital assets are being custodied:

    · Fiat currency (cash) will, at all times, be securely stored with an approved third-party bank within the
         UAE. Tokinvest utilises Client Money Account(s) with Zand Bank.

    · Digital assets, where possible, are securely stored with a VARA licensed third-party custodian within
         the UAE. Tokinvest utilises Hex Trust MENA.

The purpose of client assets being held within accounts designated "client accounts", is to ringfence client
assets from company assets in the case of an insolvency.

F.11 METHODS OF PAYMENT AND VALUE TRANSFER FOR THE VA
Funding and Subscription
To subscribe to an offering of the BREI token, investors must deposit funds into their Tokinvest client account
in AED (or another approved currency equivalent) prior to subscribing. Note that all payments and settlements
are securely processed through Tokinvest's CBUAE-approved Client Money Account (CMA).

When these funds have cleared, the investor can open the offering in the Tokinvest marketplace and click the
`Subscribe' button to select the number of tokens they wish to purchase, When the subscription has been
confirmed by the investor, Tokinvest debits the investor's fiat account and then allocates to them the requisite
number of tokens to be minted at the Token Generation Event (TGE). This allocation will be displayed in the
Investor Dashboard as an Open Order

Upon TGE, BREI Tokens are minted and transferred into each investor's individual wallet within two business
days, following completion of compliance and custody verification checks. Investors will then see the tokens
with a confirmed status in their portfolio in the Investor Dashboard.

Secondary-Market Sales and Fiat Redemption
When an investor sells BREI Tokens on the Tokinvest secondary market, the platform automatically debits the
buyer's client account and credits the seller's account with the equivalent fiat proceeds in AED (less applicable
trading fees).
Settlement occurs on a delivery-versus-payment (DvP) basis: the CAP table updates immediately to reflect
new ownership, while token transfers between verified wallets are processed within two business days.

Proceeds from token sales remain in the investor's Tokinvest fiat wallet, held within the CMA. These balances
may either:

    · be used to purchase other virtual assets listed on the platform; or
    · be withdrawn to the investor's verified external bank account via standard domestic or international

         bank transfer. Withdrawals are typically processed within two business days of request.

Ongoing Cashflows
Net rental income generated by the Property accrues on a block-by-block basis following completion of the
first full month after issuance.
Distributions to tokenholders will be made in a permitted distribution currency supported by Tokinvest's
regulated client money and virtual asset infrastructure at the relevant time. Such currency may include fiat
currency or a VARA-permitted Virtual Asset, where supported by Tokinvest's licensing permissions.

Where conversion from GBP is required prior to distribution, conversion will be effected using the relevant
GBP/[distribution currency] reference rate captured at 12:00 GMT on the applicable distribution date. The
reference rate is sourced from Tokinvest's designated pricing data provider, Fixer.io (APILayer), which
aggregates exchange rate data from multiple commercial sources. Tokinvest applies the captured reference
rate consistently for distribution calculations.
Net rental yield distribution commences after the first month and accrues on a block-by-block basis.
Tokenholders are eligible to claim outstanding rental returns at any time, up to and including the
corresponding block.
FX movements between receipt of rental income and the applicable reference time may positively or
negatively affect the amount ultimately received by tokenholders. Tokinvest does not hedge foreign exchange
risk on behalf of tokenholders.

Distribution on Property Sale
Upon property sale or disposal, gross sale proceeds (denominated in GBP) will be converted into the
permitted distribution currency using the AED/GBP reference rate captured at 12:00 GMT on the applicable
conversion date, sourced from Fixer.io (APILayer).
After deduction of disclosed transaction costs and Tokinvest's capital appreciation fee, net sale proceeds are
distributed to tokenholders in proportion to their holdings via the same block-based settlement mechanism.
Following distribution of sale proceeds, all Tokens linked to the Property are redeemed and burnt in
accordance with the issuance mechanics.

Withdrawal of Funds
Investors may withdraw available cash balances at any time, subject only to the absence of compliance,
security, or operational restrictions reasonably required to protect client assets and platform integrity.

Cross-Platform Settlement
Where a sale occurs on another VARA-licensed RWA platform that meets Tokinvest's compliance and
reconciliation standards, the transaction follows the same delivery-versus-payment (DvP) process. Both fiat
and token legs are settled in AED through CBUAE-approved client accounts, with Tokinvest maintaining the
definitive record of tokenholder positions through daily reconciliation.
Trade confirmations and updated balances are reflected in investors' dashboards once final settlement is
verified by both counterpart platforms.
No settlement or value transfer is permitted with unlicensed or offshore venues.

Inter-Platform Transfers
Transfers of BREI Tokens are permitted only between Tokinvest and other VARA-licensed RWA or VASP
platforms that maintain equivalent KYC/AML, reconciliation, and settlement standards. Transfers to
unregulated or non-licensed venues are not permitted.

Reconciliation and Confirmation
All transactions (whether executed on Tokinvest or an approved VARA-licensed partner platform) are fully
reconciled through Tokinvest's internal records. Each transaction is confirmed by email and reflected in real
time on the investor's dashboard.

SECTION G ­ ADDITIONAL WHITEPAPER DISCLOSURES (FOR ASSET-REFERENCED VIRTUAL ASSETS (ARVA))
G.1 ARVA VALUE AND RIGHTS
The BREI Token is an Asset-Referenced Virtual Asset (ARVA). Its value is directly linked to the economic
performance of the Property.

Tokenholders are entitled to:

    · A proportional share of net rental yield generated by the Property; and
    · A proportional share of net proceeds from any sale of the Property.

G.2 REFERENCE ASSET TYPE AND COMPOSITION
The reference asset for the BREI Token (TVBREIBBWUK1) is the contractual economic rights associated with
the Property which is owned by the Asset Owner.

G.3 REFERENCE ASSET CONSISTENCY
The underlying reference asset will not change throughout the life of the token.

G.4 REFERENCE ASSET RIGHTS REPRESENTATION
There is no direct or indirect right of ownership of the Property or the Asset Owner. The BREI token represents
economic rights only.

G.5 REFERENCE ASSET LEGAL/REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS
There are no specific legal or regulatory requirements in respect of settlement or transfer of title of the
Property, which remains at all times remains registered to the Asset Owner.

    · At the point of Issuance the Property is not encumbered by any regulatory conditions affecting its
         transferability. Following the Issuance, Tokinvest's interest will be noted on the Property Title to
         secure it for the benefit of tokenholders. The Property will not otherwise be encumbered for the
         duration of this Virtual Asset.

    · Ownership of BREI Tokens does not affect, alter, or transfer title in the Property.
    · Changes in tokenholder ownership (e.g. via Tokinvest's secondary market) do not affect the legal

         status or ownership of the Property itself.

G.6 STABILITY OF ARVA VALUE TO REFERENCE ASSETS
The BREI Token references the economic performance of the Property.
Secondary market prices of the Token are determined by supply and demand and may differ, potentially
materially, from the net asset value (NAV) of the underlying Property.
Factors including liquidity conditions, discounting, platform risk, FX movements, regulatory developments, and
investor sentiment may cause market prices to trade at a premium or discount to NAV.
No assurance is given that the Token will trade in line with the value of the Property.

G.10 TOKEN EXPIRY PROCESS
There is no redemption of the BREI Token (TVBREIBBWUK1) during its lifecycle. Tokens will expire only upon
the sale of the Property. At that point:

    · Net sale proceeds (after deduction of applicable costs and fees) are credited to tokenholders'

         tokenholder rights.

The detailed procedure governing the property sale, including valuation, marketing, remarketing, and

    · Gross Sale Proceeds are received by Tokinvest FZCO on behalf of tokenholders.
    · Deductions are made for:

         o Property sale costs (legal, agency, conveyancing, valuation), and

    · Net Proceeds are transferred to Tokinvest's designated client money account.
    · Distribution: Each tokenholder's proportional share is credited to their Tokinvest investor account.
    · Redemption: All BREI Tokens are burnt at expiry, formally closing out the issuance.

The procedure for property valuation, marketing, sale, and proceeds distribution is described in Appendix B.

Liquidity Risk

APPENDIX A ­ PROPERTY MANAGERS ROLE

The Property Manager has been appointed by Tokinvest FZCO to act as the agent for the Property. The
Property Manager's responsibilities are designed to ensure the property is well-maintained, legally compliant,
attracts suitable tenants, and maximises rental yield.

Services provided by the Property Manager include:

    1. Property Marketing and Advertising

              · Listing the property on major UK real estate portals (e.g. RightMove, Zoopla), social media,
                   and agency websites.

              · Preparing professional photography and virtual tours.
              · Organising open houses or private viewings.

    2. Tenant Screening and Selection

              · Conducting background checks (rental history, employment verification, references,
                   creditworthiness).

              · Drafting and negotiating tenancy agreements to align with UK legal requirements.

    3. Rent Collection and Payment Management

              · Managing rent collection through automated systems.
              · Sending reminders, following up on late payments, and applying late fee policies.

    4. Maintenance and Repairs

              · Scheduling routine maintenance and inspections.
              · Coordinating emergency repairs with vetted contractors.
              · Conducting regular condition checks.

    5. Legal and Regulatory Compliance

              · Ensuring tenancy agreements comply with UK tenancy law.
              · Registering deposits under the UK Tenancy Deposit Protection scheme, where applicable.
              · Monitoring compliance with new legislation (e.g. Renters' Rights Bill expected in 2025).

    6. Tenant Relations and Communication

              · Acting as primary contact for tenant queries.
              · Managing tenancy renewals, rent adjustments, and move-out arrangements.
              · Mediating disputes between landlord and tenants.
7. Property Inspections and Deposit Handling

         · Conducting check-in / check-out inspections with full inventories.
         · Determining return or retention of deposits for damages beyond fair wear and tear.

8. Financial Reporting

         · Providing detailed statements of rental income and expenses.
         · Preparing documentation to assist with tax filings.

9. Rent Increase Management

         · Advising on lawful rent increases under Section 13 procedures.
         · Providing tenant notices in line with statutory timelines.

10. Eviction Assistance

         · Managing legal eviction processes where tenants breach contracts.
         · Coordinating with UK courts where required.

11. Insurance and Risk Management

         · Advising on appropriate property insurance and ensuring cover is maintained.
         · Recommending tenant insurance where appropriate.

12. Exit and Property Handover

         · Managing end-of-lease handovers and deposit settlements.
         · Re-marketing the property to minimise vacancy periods.
APPENDIX B ­ PROPERTY EXIT SALE PROCESS

The following process governs the property sale and settlement:

1. Property Sale

    · While the intention is for this to be a long term Virtual Asset, Tokinvest, reserves the right, in its sole
         discretion, to sell the underlying property where the Issuer reasonably determines that circumstances
         justify an early sale, including but not limited to: material changes in market conditions, regulatory
         developments, unforeseen risks to the property or the project, or opportunities to realize enhanced
         value for token holders.

In the event of such a sale, the Issuer shall apply the net proceeds of the sale (after deduction of applicable
costs, taxes, and fees) towards the early wind-up of the financial product represented by the tokens. Token
holders will receive their proportionate entitlement from the net sale proceeds in accordance with the terms
of this White Paper and any applicable offering documents.

    · Any decision to initiate a Property sale is taken in accordance with Tokinvest FZCO's internal
         governance, compliance, and conflict-of-interest frameworks, and is supported by independent
         valuation evidence and prevailing market conditions. Secondary market token prices are considered as
         contextual indicators only and are not determinative of sale timing.

2. Exit Property Valuation

    · Tokinvest FZCO will instruct no fewer than three independent, licensed real estate agents to provide
         written valuations of the property.

    · Where one valuation materially diverges from the others without objective market justification,
         Tokinvest may disregard the outlier valuation or commission an additional independent valuation to
         confirm pricing integrity.

    · The property is then marketed at the median price of those three valuations, which becomes the Exit
         Property Valuation.

3. Marketing and Sale

    · The property will be marketed at or above the Exit Property Valuation.
    · If the property is not sold within six months, Tokinvest will obtain three further valuations, determine

         a revised median valuation, and relist the property at this updated price.
    · This process will be repeated as necessary until the property is sold (the Property Sale Price).

4. Transparency of Process

    · Tokinvest FZCO will publish on its platform all legal documents, valuations, and contracts relating to
         the sale, to provide tokenholders with full transparency of the settlement process.

    · This disclosure obligation applies equally to all valuation materials, agency appointments, and sale
         documentation used in determining and executing the Property Sale.
5. Distribution of Proceeds

    · Upon receipt of sale proceeds, Tokinvest will deduct:
              o Transaction costs (legal, conveyancing, valuation, agency commissions), and
              o Tokinvest's capital appreciation fee (5%).

    · The Property will be sold in GBP. Where conversion from GBP is required prior to distribution,
         conversion will be effected using the relevant GBP/[distribution currency] reference rate captured at
         12:00 GMT on the applicable conversion date, sourced from Tokinvest's designated pricing data
         provider, Fixer.io (APILayer). FX movements between issuance and sale may positively or negatively
         impact the amount distributed to tokenholders.

    · The net proceeds will be distributed to tokenholders in proportion to their holdings and credited to
         their Tokinvest accounts.

    · The capital appreciation fee is payable only upon completion of an actual Property sale and is
         calculated solely by reference to the realised sale price relative to the Property Value at Issuance.

6. Token Redemption

    · Following completion of the sale and distribution of proceeds, all BREI Tokens relating to the property
         will be redeemed and burnt, formally terminating tokenholder rights in respect of the Property.
