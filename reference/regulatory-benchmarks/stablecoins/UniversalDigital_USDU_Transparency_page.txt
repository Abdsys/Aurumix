Frequently Asked Questions

How are USDU reserves verified?
All reserves are independently attested each month by Crowe Mak LLP, a global accounting and advisory firm, ensuring accountability and transparency.
Who audits the USDU smart contract?
The USDU smart contract is audited and certified by CertiK, a global leader in blockchain security, to guarantee code integrity and transaction safety.
Which regulators oversee Universal?
Universal Digital Intl Limited (“Universal”) is a licensed entity under the Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA) in Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) to issue Fiat-Referenced Token ("FRT").
How often are attestations published?
Independent reserve attestations are published on a monthly basis and are available for public review.
Where can I find reports and certifications?
All reserve attestations are accessible through this page for full transparency.
