**Short answer:** Yes. Holding gold makes money in a few places, but almost never because the gold itself pays anything. Steady income comes from three sources: a fee charged on the gold you hold for others, interest on loans backed by other people's gold, and using clients' unallocated metal as cheap funding. Gold that is allocated, owned and unencumbered earns nothing. Its owner only gains when the price rises.

## 1. Bullion banks: unallocated vs allocated

- **Unallocated:** the client is an unsecured creditor. The balance is "backed by the general stock" of the dealer, not by specific bars ([Wikipedia, London bullion market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_bullion_market)). Paxos's terms define it as "a liability of such institution" ([PAXG T&Cs](https://www.paxos.com/terms-and-conditions/pax-gold-terms-conditions)). **High**
- This is the real float. The bank can lend, lease or trade against the metal and keep only part of it in the vault. High trading volumes have led critics to call it fractional reserve. **Med**
- **Allocated:** the client owns numbered bars. The bank is only the custodian, so it cannot use them. It earns a storage fee only. **High**
- The usual trade-off is that unallocated accounts are often free while allocated accounts pay storage. In effect, the client pays by letting the bank use the metal. **Med**
- Lease rates were low for most of the last decade (roughly 0.1 to 0.5%) and spiked in 2025. **Low, not checked this pass.** The leasing researcher should confirm this.

## 2. Jewellers' savings schemes (Tanishq, Malabar, Joyalukkas, Kalyan)

- **How they work:** the customer pays 10 to 11 monthly instalments. The jeweller adds a bonus of up to about one instalment, and the full amount must be spent on jewellery. **Med**
- **The rule that capped them:** the Companies (Acceptance of Deposits) Rules 2014 treat an advance as a regulated deposit unless it is used against goods within 365 days. That is why schemes shrank to 12 months or less. **Med** (well known, but the rules page could not be reached). The cap came from the Companies Act, not the RBI. Non-company jewellers also fall under the 2019 BUDS Act. **Low**
- **The float is cash, not gold.** The customer usually buys at the gold price on redemption day, so the jeweller carries no gold-price risk on the money it holds.
- **It is not free money.** The average balance is about 5.5 instalments over about 10 months. A bonus of 0.75 to 1 instalment works out to roughly **14 to 20% a year**. **Med, my own calculation.** Jewellers accept that cost because the scheme locks in a sale and funds stock.
- **Size:** Titan, Kalyan and others report customer advances in their balance-sheet notes. **Not checked this pass.** Titan's FY2026 revenue was ₹88,036 cr ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titan_Company)). **Med**

## 3. Cash and payment float

- **Fintechs:** Wise "does not lend out customer money" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wise_(company))). It earns by placing safeguarded balances in low-risk assets. **Med** Revolut's 2025 revenue was £4.52bn ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolut)). **Med** Interest on balances is a large share for both, but **the exact figures were not checked this pass.**
- **Gold apps:** the cash waits only from payment to purchase, which is minutes to about T+1. PAXG terms say USD is "promptly converted" into gold ([PAXG T&Cs](https://www.paxos.com/terms-and-conditions/pax-gold-terms-conditions)). **High** Indian digital-gold providers like SafeGold earn a 2 to 5% spread, not interest (our market research). **Med**
- **Example (my own assumptions):** $1M a month of inflow, held 2 days at 4%, gives an average float of about $66k. That earns about **$2.6k a year**. At $10M a month it is about $26k a year. This is a rounding error. **Med**
- In the UAE, who keeps interest on safeguarded money depends on the licence terms. **Low, needs counsel.**

## 5. Gold loans: earning on gold that is not yours

- Muthoot holds borrowers' jewellery as collateral and earns loan interest. The RBI caps loan-to-value at 75% (our market research). **High**
- **FY2026 (consolidated):** revenue ₹31,209 cr and profit ₹10,607 cr, up from ₹5,352 cr in FY25. Total assets were ₹1.96 lakh cr ([Screener](https://www.screener.in/company/MUTHOOTFIN/consolidated/)). **Med** (third-party aggregator)
- The gold price rally lifted loan sizes, so profit roughly doubled.
- This is the biggest income pool linked to gold. The income is a credit spread, and the gold only secures it.

## 6. Tokenized gold

- **XAUT:** 707,747 oz worth **$3.30bn** at 31 Mar 2026. The gold is "owned by XAU₮ token holders, not by the Company" ([Tether](https://tether.io/news/tether-gold-surpasses-3-3-billion-as-reserves-surge-36-in-q1-driven-by-flight-to-hard-assets/)). **High**
  - Holders pay no custody fee. Tether charges 0.25% to mint and 0.25% to redeem. **Med**
- **XAUT float in the mint cycle:** only 559,599 oz had been sold, so about **21% (about 4.6 t, about $0.7bn)** sits unsold with an affiliate ([Protocol Landscape](deliverables/1-market-research/Aurumix_Protocol_Landscape.md)). **Med** That stock earns price gains and a selling spread. It pays no income.
- **PAXG:** no yield and no storage fee. Creation fees are currently waived. The terms allow a future storage fee charged by issuing new tokens ([Paxos fees](https://support.paxos.com/articles/2899561282-pax-gold-fees)). **High**
- **No major regulated gold token earns interest on its reserves.** That includes XAUT, PAXG, XAUm, VNXAU and WisdomTree. **High**
- The only exceptions lease out holder gold: ORO and Streamex GLDY, through Monetary Metals. They are covered in the leasing brief.

## 7. Bottom line

| Source | Earns income? |
|---|---|
| Custody fee charged in metal (GLD, Goldmoney) | **Yes.** Steady, and it rises with the gold price |
| Gold-backed loans (Muthoot) | **Yes.** The largest pool. It is a credit business |
| Bullion banks using unallocated client metal | **Yes.** Cheap funding plus lease or trading spread. It means fractional exposure |
| Jeweller scheme cash | **Partly.** The cash funds stock, but the bonus costs about 14 to 20% a year. The real gain is the locked-in sale |
| Payment cash float at a gold app | **Close to zero.** The window is too short |
| Allocated, unencumbered gold (PAXG, XAUT, the issuer's own stock) | **Zero.** Only price gains |

**What this means for Aurumix:**
- The working gold float in our own design earns nothing. It carries a capital cost (about 0.49% of purchases in our draft) and price-gap risk.
- The places that pay are a metal-denominated custody fee and the credit book.
- Leasing would earn a yield, but it breaks the "100% allocated" claim.
