              9.3.7. Consensus mechanism on Ethereum

              Ethereum transitioned from a Proof-of-Work (PoW) to a Proof-of-Stake ("PoS") consensus mechanism
              in September 2022, a significant upgrade known as "The Merge." This shift aimed to enhance the
              network's security, scalability, and energy efficiency.

               Validator participation

              Staking Requirements: To become a validator, an individual must stake a minimum of 32 ETH. This
              stake acts as collateral, incentivizing proper behavior within the network.

               Block proposal and finalization

              Epochs and Slots: Time in Ethereum's PoS is divided into epochs and slots. Each epoch consists of 32
              slots, with each slot lasting 12 seconds. During each slot, a validator is selected to propose a block.

              Attestations: After a block is proposed, other validators attest to its validity. Once a block receives
              sufficient attestations, it is added to the blockchain.

               Incentives and penalties

              Rewards: Validators earn rewards for proposing blocks and for attesting to the validity of blocks
              proposed by others.

               Penalties

              Validators can be penalized for malicious actions or inactivity. Severe infractions can lead to
              "slashing," where a portion of the validator's staked ETH is forfeited, and the validator is removed
              from the network.

               Energy efficiency

              The transition to PoS has significantly reduced Ethereum's energy consumption, making it more
              environmentally friendly compared to the previous PoW mechanism. ethereum.org

              This PoS mechanism enhances Ethereum's security and scalability while reducing its environmental
              footprint.
