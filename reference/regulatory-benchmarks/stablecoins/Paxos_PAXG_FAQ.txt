WHY PAX GOLD
A digital token, backed by physical gold
One PAXG is one ounce of tokenized gold, held on your behalf and redeemable for physical gold. PAXG is issued by Paxos Trust Company, N.A., a U.S. national trust bank regulated by the OCC, and is available on Ethereum and Solana.
Buy any amount
A full gold bar costs far more than most people want to put into gold at once. PAXG lets you buy a fraction of an ounce up to 18 decimals, so you can own real, London-vaulted gold at almost any size.

FEDERALLY REGULATED
Tokenized gold, issued under U.S. federal oversight
PAXG is issued by Paxos Trust Company, N.A., a U.S. national trust bank regulated by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency. Reserves are held bankruptcy-remote and segregated from corporate assets, a standard of supervision few tokenized assets can claim.
