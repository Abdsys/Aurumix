**Why none of them can sustain margins** (my arithmetic, Low)
- Secondary trading pays the issuer nothing. DEX fees go to liquidity providers and exchange fees go to MEXC and the like. GoldZip's 0.01% only applies to on-chain transfers.
- If the issuer charges 0% on holdings and 0.2% on minting and burning, covering a lean $1M a year needs **about $500M of mint plus burn flow every year**.
- If it charges about 0.25% a year on holdings, covering $1M needs **about $400M of AUM**.
- All three sit at $5M to $10M, which is **40 to 80 times too small**.
- Pure-play gold-token issuers at this size survive only when a bullion parent treats the token as marketing and a sales channel, or until they run out of money.
