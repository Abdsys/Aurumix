# Digital Gold (non-tokenized) as a business: who makes money and why

Confidence tags: **(H)** primary filing or company disclosure, **(M)** reputable secondary source or my own arithmetic on disclosed figures, **(L)** estimate, an aggregator figure, or an old data point. FX is about ₹88/USD (M).

## 1. Bottom line

- **The party that owns the customer's payment habit keeps most of the spread.** In India that means the UPI apps (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm) and Jar. Infrastructure providers keep less than 1% of flow, and refiners make their real money on B2B bullion volume. Digital gold is a feeder channel for them.
- **The two markets work differently.** Indian digital gold is mass micro-savings. The average UPI ticket is about ₹98 (~USD 1.10) and revenue comes from a one-off 3-6% spread. Western digital gold is AUM custody for wealthy holders: BullionVault users average roughly USD 80k each, and revenue comes from 0.12-0.5%/yr storage plus about 0.5% to trade.
- **Jewellers have the most profitable model in Indian gold savings.** Their 10-11 month schemes cost about 7.5% in bonus, and redemption is forced into jewellery that carries making charges.
- **The UAE is already being occupied by distribution owners, each plugging in a white-label supplier.** e& money uses SafeGold (Apr 2026), Botim uses O Gold (Aug 2025), and Emirates NBD and Emirates Islamic run their own in-app products.

## 2. India

### Market size and flow

- UPI digital gold purchases ran from ₹762 cr/month (Jan 2025) to ₹1,410 cr (Sep 2025). They dropped 47% month on month to ₹1,215 cr in Nov 2025 after the SEBI warning (M) ([Open](https://openthemagazine.com/business/indias-digital-gold-rush-will-2026-be-the-year-of-regulatory-reckoning)).
- The peak was ₹3,926 cr in Jan 2026. March 2026 hit a record 254m transactions worth ₹3,172 cr (M) ([Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/digital-gold-via-upi-hits-record-254-mn-transactions-in-march-11744848), [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/digital-gold-via-upi-hits-254-million-transactions-in-march-value-at-3-171-crore)).
- May 2026 was flat at 249m transactions and ₹2,451 cr (M) ([Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/phonepe-gpay-and-paytm-record-their-highest-upi-volumes-in-may-digital-gold-purchases-remain-flat-12039425)). That implies an average ticket of about ₹98 (M, derived) and a run-rate of roughly ₹30,000 cr (~USD 3.4bn) a year (L).
- Three refiners or vaulting companies back almost all of it: MMTC-PAMP, Augmont and SafeGold. The consumer apps are front-ends (M) ([Entrackr/Open summary](https://openthemagazine.com/business/indias-digital-gold-rush-will-2026-be-the-year-of-regulatory-reckoning)).
- The industry self-regulator, DPMACI, covers 80m+ customers (M) ([The Ken](https://the-ken.com/the-collection/all-that-glitters-in-augmonts-ipo-is-not-digital-gold/)).

### Pricing paid by the customer

- 3% GST on every purchase, which is not refunded on sale (H).
- Buy/sell spread (M) ([ipomarket](https://www.ipomarket.in/news/digital-gold-india-paytm-phonepe-tanishq), [Jumpp](https://jumpp.finance/blog/growing-your-wealth/digital-gold/digital-gold-charges/)):
  - PhonePe about 4-6%
  - GPay and Paytm about 5%
  - MMTC-PAMP direct 3-4%
  - Typical buyback is 1.5-3% below the IBJA rate
- Storage is free for 3-5 years, then about 0.3-1%/yr (M).
- All-in, a customer starts 5-7% under water (M).

### Customer acquisition and retention

- **Jar:** 35m registered users, 60% in Tier 2-3 cities, 95% saving formally for the first time (M). Its daily ₹10 UPI-autopay save is the main product, at more than 1m autopay transactions a day (M) ([Kuwi](https://www.kuwi.news/p/a-conversation-with-misbah-ashraf)).
- **Jar's paid CAC:** ₹48.5 cr of FY25 ad spend for roughly 15m net new registrations works out to about ₹30-35 (~USD 0.40) per registration (L). Registrations are not active savers, so treat this as a lower bound.
- **SafeGold:** about 50m people have bought through its partners, but only about 9m hold a live balance, roughly 18% (M) ([Founder Thesis](https://www.founderthesis.com/p/a-banker-walks-into-a-gold-bar)). It now claims 67m users worldwide (M) ([TechAfrica](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/13/e-money-launches-digital-gold-investment-platform-in-uae-with-safegold-partnership/)).
- **Takeaway:** most digital gold users are one-time buyers. Profit comes from scale and a near-zero CAC. Lifetime value is low.

### Jeweller savings schemes (Tanishq Golden Harvest and peers)

- **How the scheme works:** 10 fixed monthly payments, minimum ₹2,000. The customer gets up to 75% of one payment as a bonus, which is about 7.5% of the total, and must redeem in jewellery within 400 days (H) ([Tanishq GHS](https://www.tanishqgoldenharvest.co.in/online/home), [BankBazaar](https://www.bankbazaar.com/gold-rate/tanishq-gold-schemes.html)).
- **Share of business:** these schemes were once 25-30% of Titan jewellery sales, and are now said to be about 20-25% (M/L) ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/feature/a-golden-chance-1951)).
- **Why it pays:** the jeweller gets customer lock-in, about a year of interest-free float, and redemption into products that carry making charges.
- **Legal limit:** the Companies Act 2013 and the 2014 Deposit Rules keep these schemes under 365 days. That is why they run for 10-11 months (M) ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/corporate-and-company-law/1570654/laws-governing-gold-savings-schemes-by-jewellery-brands)).

### The SEBI/RBI regulatory gap

- **2021 broker ban:** on 3 Aug 2021 SEBI said brokers selling digital gold breached the SCRR. NSE then barred its members from selling it from 10 Sep 2021. At the time the industry was about ₹5,000 cr a year (H) ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/markets/nse-bans-members-from-selling-digital-gold-after-sebi-flags-concerns-121082501011_1.html), [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2021/08/223-nse-fintech-digital-gold-sebi-3/)).
- **Nov 2025 warning:** on 8 Nov 2025 SEBI said digital gold sits entirely outside its remit and carries counterparty and operational risk (H) ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/11/10/sebi-warning-digital-gold-investment-risks/)).
- **Effect of the warning:** withdrawals roughly tripled and UPI purchase value fell 47% (M) ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/commodities/digital-gold-fintechs-face-scrutiny-amid-sebi-warning-125111101962_1.html)).
- **No framework coming:** on 21 Nov 2025 the SEBI chair said no framework was under consideration (M) ([SafeGold blog](https://www.safegold.com/blog/digital-gold/digital-gold-regulation-2026/)). RBI does not regulate digital gold either. The rules that apply are GST, contract law, consumer protection and the voluntary DPMACI audits.
- **Complaints:** 112 complaints in 2025, up from 17 in 2020 (M).
- **The regulated route lost on distribution:** SEBI-regulated Electronic Gold Receipts have traded about ₹100 cr in total since 2022. Unregulated digital gold moved about ₹4,000 cr in a single month in 2026 (M) ([The Ken](https://the-ken.com/the-collection/all-that-glitters-in-augmonts-ipo-is-not-digital-gold/)).

## 5. Who makes money and why

1. **Distribution owners with near-zero CAC.** PhonePe, GPay and Paytm sit on UPI, and Jar owns the autopay habit. They take most of the 3-6% spread, worth about 4.7-8.5% of Jar's sales. Jar still needed about 4 years, a switch to principal status and USD 63m of funding to reach quarterly profit.
2. **Infrastructure providers do not.** SafeGold's ₹6,867 cr of flow produced about ₹58 cr gross and ₹2 cr EBITDA, because partners hold the pricing power. Its response is to expand abroad (UAE, Thailand, Korea) and add leasing yield.
3. **Refiners and bullion houses make real absolute money on thin margins**, mostly from B2B volume. Augmont made ₹348 cr PAT in FY26. Digital gold is a channel that adds demand for their bars and refining.
4. **Jewellers make the most per customer.** Savings schemes cost about 7.5% and are recovered through making charges and lock-in.
5. **Western custodians are profitable only on large balances and gold rallies.** At 0.12-0.5%/yr, a USD 80k average balance earns USD 100-400 per user each year. Goldmoney's FY26 jump came mostly from the gold price.

## 6. What this means for Aurumix

- **The 5% SIP entry fee is high for the UAE.** It sits at the top of India's spread band, while UAE banks show zero fees and charge 0.5-2% spreads and Western players charge 0.5-1.8%. Diaspora customers can compare against ENBD and Liv.
- **The B2B platform fee puts Aurumix in SafeGold's seat,** the thinnest-margin position in the chain. SafeGold is already live in the UAE through e& money.
- **AUM fees on small savings balances produce very little.** A balance of about USD 500 a year, charged 0.75%/yr, earns cents to a few dollars per customer.
- **The proven margin pools are elsewhere:**
  - Redemption into jewellery at partner jewellers, with a share of making charges. This is the "minted or branded product" idea in practice.
  - The spread on leasing customer gold to jewellers. O Gold pays customers 3% against lease benchmarks around 3.25%.
  - Owning a distribution channel, through a payroll, remittance or exchange-house tie-up, rather than renting one.
