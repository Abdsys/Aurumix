1. Purpose of this Notice
This notice provides a description of the risks associated with investing in and trading Virtual Assets,
including LynxCap LCL Opportunities 3 Digital Bond (the "Token").

2. Risk of Loss
Investment in the Token involves a risk of loss of part or all of the invested capital.
Returns are not guaranteed.
The value of the Token reflects the performance of the underlying LCL Opportunities Bond and may
fluctuate due to changes in market conditions, interest rates, credit conditions, and liquidity dynamics.
Investors may receive less than their original investment, or nothing at all.

3. Nature of the Product and Rights
The Token is a digital representation of an investor's economic exposure to a Luxembourg issued
Senior Secured Bond accessed through Tokinvest's platform.
The Token:

     · does not represent legal or beneficial ownership of the Bond or underlying assets;
     · does not constitute a claim on the issuer of the Bond (outside those assets specifically

          identified in the Bond prospectus); and
     · does not constitute a claim on the general assets of Tokinvest.

Tokenholders have contractual rights solely against Tokinvest in respect of:

     · platform access;
     · submission of purchase and sale (where permissible) instructions; and
     · receipt of economic outcomes as recorded in Tokinvest's internal systems.

All rights are contractual in nature and dependent on the proper functioning of the platform and
upstream distribution arrangements.

4. No Direct Relationship with Bond Issuer
Investors:

     · are not registered as Token holders in the Cap Table of the issuer;
     · have no direct contractual or legal relationship with LCL Opportunities 3; and
     · cannot exercise rights directly against the issuer.

Access to the Token is entirely intermediated through Tokinvest and any other distribution partners
appointed by the issuer.

Bond-Specific Risks

34. Bond Risks
The underlying bond is subject to:

     · interest rate risk;
     · credit risk;
     · liquidity risk; and
     · counterparty risk.

The underlying bonds have senior secured status which, although typically lower risk than unsecured
instruments, may incur losses and do not guarantee capital preservation.

35. Token Pricing Risk
The Token is intended to reflect the market value of the underlying bond; however, pricing may
diverge due to:

     · operational timing differences between bond and token pricing;
     · fees and charges applied at platform or transaction level;
     · foreign exchange movements (where applicable); and
     · supply and demand dynamics in any secondary market.

Whilst Token redemption at maturity is contractually fixed at the same value as the underlying bond, if
earlier redemption is sought via a secondary market investors may realise proceeds above or below
the underlying bond price at the time of execution depending on market conditions.
