## 2. Ranking

The reference saver is our assumption: **USD 40/month SIP (USD 480/yr), USD 1,500 average balance by Y3, USD 250/month card spend, USD 1,200/yr spent abroad.** All dollar figures in the right-hand column are **our estimates [L]**.

**What the ranking shows** [M/L]:
- **The high-margin pools need something Aurumix doesn't have.** Branded products need a scarce brand. Small bars need retail shelf space. Jewellery needs craft and stores. Gold loans need a balance sheet.
- **The pools Aurumix can reach each pay single-digit dollars per customer.** At USD 40/month, no fee design produces much: the binding constraint is ticket size and balance.
- **Card FX and interchange is the largest reachable pool.** It is ahead of the entry fee once the fee's cost base is netted out.
- **Manual handling sets a hard ceiling.** On a USD 40 SIP, every USD 0.40 of per-transaction handling cost wipes out 1 point of margin. A USD 1 manual touch costs 2.5% of the ticket, which is about 60% of the 4.15% cost build-up.

## 3. Does branding, minting or personalisation create margin for a diaspora savings app?

**Verdict: not as a profit line. It is useful as a retention and acquisition tool.** Confidence [M].

1. **The branding premium belongs to the owner of a trusted, scarce brand.** The Trump proof's ~23% sits on a US Mint product with a one-per-household limit. Coins sold under the Trump name with no gold in them (the USD 1 coin) sell on novelty alone. An "Aurumix bar" has no secondary-market premium. At buyback, dealers pay melt minus a spread, typically **under 1% on 24K** [L] ([Himmath](https://himmathgold.com/how-to-sell-gold-in-dubai-buyback-rates-spreads-and-what-youll-actually-be-paid/)). The customer loses the full premium, which cuts against Aurumix's fair-buyback positioning.
2. **The fabrication-to-retail gap is real but small in dollars.** A 1g bar costs about 2.6% to make and retails at +20-60%, but that 1g is only USD 130. A 5g keepsake at a retained 5-8% is USD 30-50, once every few years.
3. **India customs point to jewellery, not bars.** Budget 2026 removed the value caps on duty-free *jewellery*, leaving **20g for men and 40g for women** [H] ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/people/india-eases-gold-customs-rule-indian-expats-in-uae-welcome-update-in-budget-2026-1.500429410)). **Bars and coins pay ~6% duty from the first gram** [M] ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/can-nris-carry-gold-coins-bars-duty-free-from-uae-after-india-hikes-import-duty)). Dubai retail gold is also **8-12% cheaper than India** [M] ([Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/commodities/gold-rate-dubai-vs-india-gold-prices-on-october-29-2026)). So the NRI wedding purchase is jewellery bought in the UAE and carried home.
4. **The margin is in converting saved gold into jewellery.** Jar's Nek line brings in ₹100 cr+ a year. SafeGold redeems into Tanishq and CaratLane, and Augmont into Kalyan and CaratLane. Titan shows coins are its lowest-margin line. **Recommendation:** redeem-to-jewellery with UAE Indian jewellers (Malabar has **67 UAE outlets** [M], [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malabar_Gold_&_Diamonds)), with the referral paid out of the jeweller's 8-25% making charge.
5. **Occasion gifting earns its place through retention.** Eid, Diwali, Akshaya Tritiya, births and weddings, plus "gift grams to family", can be used to push accounts past the 6-payment gate that only 53.5% pass. That is worth more than any keepsake markup [L].

## 5. Tokenized vs digital gold

- **Tokenized gold** is wholesale and crypto-native. Fees run 0.02-0.25% and are being waived, and the two issuers' fee income is tiny (XAUT ~USD 5m/yr) [M].
- **Digital gold** is retail, with **2-5% spreads**. The profit goes to whoever owns the customer and the redemption (Jar), not the infrastructure (SafeGold's 0.03% EBITDA) [H/M].
- **Aurumix's customers are digital-gold customers.** The ERC-20 token is a settlement rail, and it brings in no margin by itself.

## 6. Points for Friday

1. **A protocol or infrastructure layer is the lowest-margin seat in the chain:** SafeGold 0.03% EBITDA, Augmont 0.34% net, XAUT ~0.2%. Build it only if the partners bring the volume.
2. **Move the B2B pitch from "AUM fee" to "retention product for remitters".** Exchange houses already spend on gold as prizes. Price per active user, and expect OGold to compete for the same partners.
3. **Add redeem-to-jewellery with UAE jewellers.** It is the one high-margin pool Aurumix can reach without inventory.
4. **Gold credit:** India gold lending earns ~30% ROE. Explore NRI-to-family gold-loan referrals with Muthoot or Manappuram, building on the LuLu precedent [L].
5. **Raise the balance per account:** spot buyers and pooled family accounts. Then automate every per-transaction touch.
