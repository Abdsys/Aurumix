# Aurumix: what gets it to profit

*Partner briefing for Tony (tomorrow) and the client meeting with Gaurav and CG (Friday). Confidence tags: (H) primary source or our model, (M) reputable secondary source, (L) estimate.*

## 1. The one-paragraph answer

Aurumix makes money when it runs as a digital-gold business that gets customers cheaply and earns when the gold gets used. Today it runs as a token issuer that pays to win retail customers, and the numbers do not work. A UAE saver costs USD 73 to win and brings in USD 32.80 a year. About a third leave each year. Each one loses us USD 10.48 a year (H). The token adds fixed cost. Under VARA it also bans a fee on the sell side, which is half of the spread that every digital-gold business earns. To fix this, work in four steps:
1. **Stop the cash going out.** Freeze VARA spend until counsel reports, and stop paid acquisition in the UAE.
2. **Fix the cost stack.** Bar sourcing, payment rails, KYC and support.
3. **Earn when the gold gets used.** Jewellery referrals, gifting, a card for trips home, and credit by referral.
4. **Win customers through one exchange house or payroll (WPS) partner**, so acquisition costs close to nothing.

A protocol, a paid AI agent and branded bars all sit in the thinnest-margin seats. They should wait.

## 2. Tokenized gold vs digital gold

| | Tokenized gold (XAUT, PAXG) | Digital gold (Jar, PhonePe, OGold, SafeGold) |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer** | Crypto traders, exchanges, treasuries. About 478k holders worldwide (H) | First-time mass savers. Jar has 35m registered users. OGold has 1.5m in the UAE alone (M) |
| **Use case** | Trading, hedging inside crypto, collateral. About 16x market cap traded per quarter (M) | Habit saving, gifting, converting to jewellery. Buy and hold |
| **Distribution** | Exchange listings. The issuer never meets the end user. XAUT direct purchase needs about USD 200k (M) | The app, bank or wallet owns the user: PhonePe, Botim, e& money, ENBD |
| **Fee level** | 0.125-0.25% to mint or redeem. PAXG creation fees are waived until Jan 2027. No storage fee (M) | India: 2-7% spread plus 3% GST. UAE banks: 0.5-2% spread with "AED 0 fee" on screen (M/L) |
| **Who profits** | Exchanges (spot and perps) and the issuer's core business (stablecoin float). Issuance is roughly break-even (M) | The app that owns the customer and the redemption. About half of Jar's ₹208 cr operating revenue is jewellery. Infrastructure earns under 1% (M) |
| **Regulation** | PAXG: OCC trust bank. XAUT: El Salvador. Dubai: VARA asset-referenced token (ARVA), AED 1.5m capital, no redemption fee (H) | India: unregulated (SEBI warning, Nov 2025). UAE: a DMCC entity working through CBUAE-licensed banks and wallets (H) |
| **Size** | About USD 5.2bn (H) | India UPI run-rate about USD 3.4bn a year (L) |

**What this means for Aurumix:**
- **Our customers are digital-gold customers.** OGold, SafeGold and the bank apps are our competitors. XAUT is not.
- **The token earns almost nothing.** XAUT's real customer fee income is probably well under USD 5m a year on a token worth about USD 3bn. The widely quoted USD 5.2m included Tether's own treasury mints (M).
- **The token costs us money.** ARVA status means AED 1.5m of capital, about AED 300k a year in fees and a fee-free buyback (H). SafeGold already runs in the UAE without a token: it is a DMCC company selling through e& money's CBUAE-licensed wallet (H).
- **Margin follows distribution.** OGold already has Botim (8.5m UAE users), Aspora (an Indian diaspora remittance app), a Mastercard and a 3% yield (M). Our features do not set us apart from that.

**The pattern:** the high-margin pools need a scarce brand, retail shelf space, craft or a balance sheet. Aurumix can reach jewellery, the spread and card FX through partners. Each pays single-digit dollars per saver. That makes cheap acquisition and a low cost to serve essential.

## 4. Why Aurumix is not profitable today

1. **We buy customers we cannot pay back in the UAE.**
   - CAC is USD 73 and churn is 36.5%. Replacing leavers costs USD 26.67 per payer a year, against USD 32.80 of revenue (H).
   - Acquisition is USD 1.79m, 49% of the Year 7 cost base (H).
   - Retail alone needs 146,289 paying customers. The plan reaches 88,594 (H). The old "~45k accounts" figure predates decisions D28-D35. Do not quote it.
2. **The ticket is too small for the payment rail.**
   - The modelled average SIP is USD 33.60 (H).
   - An AED 5 direct debit costs USD 1.36, so each debit nets −USD 0.42 before any loyalty discount (H).
   - The breakeven ticket is about USD 49, or USD 89 for a tiered customer (H).
3. **The token setup gives away half the spread and adds fixed cost.**
   - Buyback must be fee-free under ARVA rules. Storage is free.
   - So 64.7% of the Year 7 book (130,638 holders against 71,208 payers) pays nothing but still costs vault and AML screening (H).
4. **Revenue rests on lines with no proof behind them.**
   - The B2B fee is 34.7% of revenue with zero partners signed. It charges 0.75% a year, while PAXG and XAUT charge 0% storage (H).
   - ATM fees are USD 570k (12.7%) against card spend of USD 215 per card a year (H).
   - Interchange is negative after scheme costs: we keep 0.72% of spend and pay 0.89% (H).
   - The 15% family-plan attach rate has no evidence (H).
5. **The cost of people is missing from the model.**
   - Headcount is not modelled. Adding only the Year 3 benchmark (USD 1.34m) turns Year 7 profit from USD 0.85m to about −USD 0.02m (H).
   - Manual handling, such as the ~USD 1.35 per buyback, is "assumed inside headcount" (H). That is where "people touching it" hides.
6. **The only profitable region is closed, and the funding gap is large.**
   - India makes +USD 14.92 per customer, and the India route is closed (H).
   - p90 funding need is USD 4.82m. 43% of paths pay back by Year 7. With no partners, no path pays back and the need rises to USD 7.09m (H).

## 5. The path to profitability

### Do now (weeks 0-12)

**1. Stop the cash going out**
- **What:**
  - Freeze VARA spend.
  - Get a counsel memo on launching as digital gold first: a DMCC entity working with a CBUAE-licensed partner, with the token deferred.
  - Stop paid UAE acquisition and Oman/Bahrain marketing.
  - Re-run the Monte Carlo on that basis.
- **Why it pays:** it removes the largest cost line and reopens a 0.5-1.5% spread on buyback.
- **Rough economics:**
  - VARA: AED 100k to apply, AED 200k a year, AED 1.5m capital (H).
  - Oman/Bahrain: 18% of the marketing budget is spent from month 1, and the region opens at month 13 (H).
- **Time to cash:** immediate savings.
- **Dependency:** a counsel view on CMA (which replaced SCA on 1 Jan 2026) and CBUAE Article 62. The founder has to accept that the token may wait.

**2. Fix the cost stack**
- **What:**
  - Buy through a dealer's pooled allocated account, moving to kilobars at scale.
  - Collect quarterly or set an AED 250 minimum. Use Aani for top-ups.
  - UAE PASS KYC.
  - AI-first WhatsApp support in Hindi, Urdu and Malayalam, with a guaranteed route to a human.
  - Mint on a layer-2 network if the token stays.
  - Add an honest headcount line to the model.
- **Why it pays:** it removes the per-transaction leaks that partners would otherwise squeeze.
- **Rough economics:**
  - Sourcing saves USD 3-4 per payer (H/M).
  - Digital KYC costs AED 1.5-4 a check against AED 80-250 manual (L).
  - Klarna-style support cut cost per transaction from USD 0.32 to 0.19 (M).
  - Layer-2 minting costs ~USD 8.5k a year against ~USD 1.7m on mainnet (H).
- **Time to cash:** 0-3 months.
- **Dependency:** vendor contracts; DIFC trust allocation and attestation must hold.

### Build next (months 3-12)

**3. Redeem-to-jewellery and occasion gifting**
- **What:**
  - Sign one UAE Indian jeweller group to take grams as payment for jewellery.
  - Launch "gift grams" to UAE recipients for the spring 2027 Eid and Akshaya Tritiya season.
- **Why it pays:**
  - This is where the "shaped gold" premium exists for this customer.
  - India lets 20g (men) and 40g (women) of jewellery in duty-free (H).
  - Dubai gold is 8-12% cheaper than India's (M).
  - Gifts lift the ticket above the AED 5 rail breakeven.
- **Rough economics:** a 3-5% referral out of the making charge. A 20g redemption (~USD 2,600) earns USD 78-130, about USD 3-8 per saver a year (L).
- **Time to cash:** 3-6 months.
- **Dependency:** a jeweller contract, and counsel confirming the making charge is not a redemption fee. No inventory.

**4. One distribution partner (exchange house or WPS agent)**
- **What:**
  - Put our SIP, family and buyback product inside an exchange house or payroll flow.
  - Charge a setup fee plus a per-active-user fee.
- **Why it pays:**
  - Acquisition cost goes close to zero.
  - Exchange houses already spend on gold as prizes (Al Ansari, LuLu) (M).
  - A payday top-up avoids the AED 5 debit.
- **Rough economics:** price near SafeGold's level, about USD 3-5 per active user a year (L). 2.5 partners cover the modelled fixed costs (H).
- **Time to cash:** 6-12 months (L, unsourced).
- **Dependency:** a licensed setup that the partner's compliance team accepts. Kill criterion: no signed LOI by end of Q1 2027.

**5. Reposition the Gold Card for the trip home**
- **What:** sell the card as the cheapest way to spend in India or Pakistan. Drop the cashback race and the ATM revenue line.
- **Why it pays:** FX is the largest card pool we can reach.
- **Rough economics:**
  - Net USD 7-13 per active cardholder a year after scheme costs (M).
  - Raising card take-up from 18% to 30% is worth USD 1.32m in the simulation, before this repricing (H).
- **Time to cash:** 6-12 months, set by the issuer's timeline.
- **Dependency:** a CBUAE-licensed issuer. NRIs can now pay with UPI from NRE accounts, which takes some India spend off cards (M).

### Later / option

**6. Credit as pure referral**
- **What:** refer gold-backed credit to a CBUAE-licensed lender. No servicing, no first-loss deposit.
- **Why it pays:** lending is the richest pool, and the lender carries the risk.
- **Rough economics:** small for now. Today's credit limit is about USD 205 and lending is 1% of revenue (H). It grows with balances.
- **Time to cash:** 9-15 months.
- **Dependency:** lender appetite and an Article 62 analysis.

**7. Strategic fallback**
- **What:** if no partner signs, a working NRI gold-savings book has logical buyers in Joyalukkas (a jeweller that also owns an exchange house) or Al Ansari (M).
- **Dependency:** a clean book and clean compliance records.

## 6. Verdict on the four ideas on the table

| Idea | Verdict | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Manual ops / automation** | Pursue | Real and cheap, and partners will push us to SafeGold-level pricing. It saves cost and creates no new margin, so pair it with the CAC and rail fixes. |
| **Branding / minted products** | Pursue narrowly | The Trump name adds only ~8% over a standard US Mint proof. An Aurumix bar buys back at melt. Use gifting SKUs and jewellery conversion for ticket size and retention only. |
| **CG's infrastructure / protocol** | Park | It is the thinnest seat in the chain (SafeGold 0.03% EBITDA, Securitize loss-making), and the WGC is building shared rails. If anything, use someone else's backend. |
| **Paid AI agent manager** | Park as a product, pursue as a feature | Willingness to pay is weak: Origin sells at USD 1 for year one, and ~70% of US adults distrust AI money advice (M). Once the agent makes decisions it needs a licence. Use rule-based nudges and debit retries to lift the 53.5% gate pass rate. |

## 7. What to stop or deprioritise

- **Paid retail acquisition in the UAE.** It drives the whole −USD 10.48.
- **Oman and Bahrain marketing before month 13.**
- **USD 20 monthly direct debits.**
- **The 0.75% AUM fee for partners as 35% of base-case revenue.** Price per active user instead.
- **ATM revenue (USD 570k) and the Gold Rewards cashback race.** The rewards cost is understated about twentyfold in the model (H).
- **Mainnet minting, an exchange listing, or leading any pitch with the token.**
- **Risk-share lending, leasing customer gold, and gifting to India.** Each needs a licence or route we do not have.
- **A visible 5% fee next to ENBD's "AED 0".** Price through the spread like every competitor.
