Campaign Review: Prudentia ·
Q1/Q2 2026

Prudentia joined the Evolution Stables team coming off a spell after her previous campaign. Manager and
Owner Kylie Bax, together with the team at Wexford Stables under Lance O'Sullivan and Andrew Scott,
have brought her into the strong form we are now seeing.

THE JOURNEY
The campaign opened at Tauranga on 14 March with a fifth in Rating 65 company -- a solid resumption.

Four weeks later at Te Rapa she produced the standout performance of the campaign: from midfield
under Masa Hashizume she delivered a gritty come-from-behind win over 1300m on Heavy9, taking the
Rating 65 by a neck and earning a five-point rating rise. It was her first win for Evolution Stables on the
Tokinvest platform and proved she can dig in when the ground turns testing.

She was stepped straight into Rating 75 and answered immediately with a close third on debut over
1400m at Te Rapa on 2 May. A sprint over 1200m on 30 May saw her pocketed along the rail for a gutsy
fifth -- the margin understated the effort. The campaign closed at Tauranga on 27 June on a heavy, sticky
surface that did not suit her running style, finishing an honest sixth.

She placed in two of her five starts, including a win, and made a rapid step up from Rating 65 to Rating 75
-- placing third on debut at the higher grade. Full stakes and distribution details are in the financial
summary.
