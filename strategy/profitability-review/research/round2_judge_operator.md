## 3. Economics that look made up or stretched

- **Old Gold Desk spread.**
  - The whole business depends on keeping 2.4%, and that number is untested (L).
  - Himmath gives an example of 100g of 22K jewellery reselling for AED 43,896 (AED 439/g) when 24K was AED 489/g. That is about 98% of the gold content at the board rate (M, [Himmath](https://himmathgold.com/how-to-sell-gold-in-dubai-buyback-rates-spreads-and-what-youll-actually-be-paid/)).
  - At today's rates that works out to about AED 438/g. A refiner would pay us only about AED 436/g (L).
  - Jewellers can pay that much because they reuse the gold and earn making charges on new pieces. A desk that only sells scrap to a refiner may have 0-1% to work with, not 2.4%.
- **Gift Gold B2B at AED 570.**
  - That is the jeweller's retail price. A company buyer can get the same bar from the mint for AED 498 (H).
  - So the 10.9% gross margin, and the AED 14k per order built on it, are overstated. About 5-6% is realistic (L).
- **Gold Loan Desk.**
  - "The lender pays us 9% a year of the book" has no UAE precedent, and Rupeek's partners kept most of the interest.
  - "Gold Souk pawnbrokers at 15-24%" comes only from an SEO site (L).
- **Gold Credit Line.** The AED 499 fee, the 30% share of customers who borrow, and the AED 90 vault cost are all assumptions. Nobody checked what bank lockers cost.
- **Wedding Plan, own brand.**
  - A 14% making charge for an unknown brand is optimistic.
  - The AED 270 acquisition cost comes from the earlier Aurumix model, not from the market.
  - The 25% early-exit rate is a guess (L).
- **Wedding Plan in payroll apps.** The 1% jeweller commission and the 60% of savers who convert are both L, and no jeweller has signed anything.
- **Crypto-to-bar.**
  - It cites a "Binance P2P snapshot", but that page cannot be snapshotted.
  - Break-even needs about 1,000 kg a year, and there is no evidence anyone wants that much.
- **Gold lease desk.** It charges jewellers 4.5% a year, but its own text says lease rates are about 1-2% (L). The idea contradicts itself.
- **Jar "about half of revenue from jewellery".** TechCrunch does not support this. It gives FY24 operating revenue of ₹2.08bn and total revenue of ₹24.5bn. Its jewellery line, Nek, "crossed ₹1bn last year", and no share is given. "About half" only comes out if you divide Nek's sales by the app's operating revenue, which mixes different years and different kinds of revenue (M, [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/18/indian-fintech-jar-turns-profitable-by-helping-millions-save-in-gold/)).

## 4. Top two picks

**Context: where the money in gold sits.** There are two places:
- **Making charges at jewellers.** Kalyan's operating margin is 7%, and Titan's jewellery EBIT is 11.2% (M, from the idea notes, not re-checked).
- **Interest on gold loans.** Muthoot's ROE is about 31% (M, not re-checked).

The businesses in between earn very little:
- SafeGold: 0.03% EBITDA (M).
- Gold.com (formerly A-Mark): 1.92% gross margin (M).
- Envela consumer segment: 3.5% operating margin (H).

My two picks sit in that thin middle. They are the fastest and cheapest way to make money on every sale from the first month. They also give us the licence, the trust, the data and the customers we need to reach lending later.

### Pick 1: Gift and Rewards Desk (ideas 3 and 8 merged into one)

**Why:**
- Customers pay before we buy anything.
- We buy from the mint the same day, so we hold no stock and carry no price risk.
- It is a plain sale of goods under a dealer licence (the Ministry of Economy's precious-metals dealer registration, DPMS, with goAML reporting). No CBUAE, VARA or CMA licence is needed.
- It uses the founder's network of South Asian-run companies and exchange houses.
- Capital is about USD 0.2-0.3m (L).

**First offer:**
- **What the customer buys:** "Festival gold for your staff or customers." Minted 24K 1g bars in a logo card, delivered by a fixed date, with a written buyback price.
- **Price:** about **AED 539 per 1g bar (L)**. That is about 8% above the mint's AED 498 (H) and 5% below the jeweller's AED 570 (H).
- **How we earn on day one:**
  - Payment is 100% upfront on the purchase order, and the gold price is fixed that day.
  - Our cost is about AED 508 per bar: mint price AED 498 plus card and delivery AED 10 (L).
  - Gross is about AED 31 per bar (5.8%). A 300-bar order earns about AED 6k after selling and handling costs (L).
  - With the founders selling and one operations hire, fixed cost is about AED 250k a year. That needs about 40 orders a year to break even (L).
- **Timing:** Diwali on 8 Nov is too close. The first windows are Eid al-Fitr (around 9-10 Mar 2027, L), Akshaya Tritiya and year-end awards.
- **Limit:** this is a small business on its own. Exchange-house prize campaigns earn only about 1.5%. Its real value is cash plus a working relationship with every exchange house, which Pick 2 needs.

### Pick 2: Old Gold Desk, but only if a two-week test passes

**Why:**
- Every ticket earns on the day it happens.
- It uses the same DPMS licence, compliance setup and refiner relationship as Pick 1, so the extra cost is small.
- Every household has old gold. That is a larger, repeating market than corporate gifts.
- It puts us in front of the same families who would later borrow against their gold.

**First offer:**
- **What the customer buys:** "See your price. Get paid in 15 minutes."
  - A kiosk inside a partner exchange-house branch.
  - Purity test (XRF) done in front of the customer.
  - Paid by instant bank transfer, or sent straight home through the host exchange house. The exchange house earns its FX margin on that transfer, which is why it would host us (L).
- **Price:** today about **AED 427/g for 22K (L)**. That is about 5.4% under the AED 451.75 board rate (M).
- **How we earn on day one:**
  - We fix our selling price at the moment we buy.
  - The refiner pays about 99.4% of the gold content, about AED 436/g, within 1-2 days (L).
  - On a 25g ticket that is about AED 219 gross, minus about AED 122 for transfer, KYC, insurance and acquisition, leaving **about AED 97 (USD 26)** (L).
  - Without shop rent, fixed cost is about AED 330k a year. Break-even is about 3,400 tickets a year, or **about 11 a working day (L)**.
- **The test:** before signing for any space, mystery-shop 10 souk buyers and jewellers for their cash price on 22K.
  - If they pay more than about AED 427/g, a desk that sells only to refiners does not work.
  - In that case, sell lots to a jewellery manufacturer that pays more than a refiner (L), or drop the idea.

**Together:**
- Both picks run on one licence and need about USD 0.6-0.8m (L).
- That leaves most of the USD 1-3m seed for the bigger prize: open talks with a CBUAE-licensed bank about the Gold Loan Desk in month 1.
- Kill rule: if there is no bank term sheet within 6 months, stay a trading business.

**Not recommended:** issuing a token, the lease desk, the jewellery-plan ideas, and our own finance company before we have a loan book and a partner.
