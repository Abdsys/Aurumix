**Bottom line:** none of the three earns enough from its gold token to pay for itself.
- **Kinesis** is the only real operating gold-token business here. Its token fees cover only a small part of its costs, so it is very likely losing money and living on outside capital.
- **Goldfish (GGBR)** depends on I-ON Digital, a US-listed company that supplies the in-ground gold behind the token. I-ON's SEC filings show losses, a going-concern warning, and Goldfish revenue falling to $0 in Q2 2026.
- **AZG** is not really a gold-token business. It is a way to fund mining loans. Nothing about it can be checked against filings.

**What this means for the "start from scratch" question:**
- Outside PAXG and XAUT, nobody here makes a living from gold-token fees.
- **Kinesis** charges on activity (sending and trading gold). With $356M of gold it still makes about $2M a year, too little for a full team.
- **Goldfish** makes money only when it sells new tokens. Once sales stopped, revenue went to $0.
- **AZG's** token is just how its mining-loan business raises money.
- A basic offering only works if the money comes from something other than the token: lending against the gold, card spending, or owning the customer relationship, as Jar and Muthoot do.
