    29. Nature of the Reference Asset

         documentation.
         Holders are exposed to the risks inherent in:
              · The enforceability and interpretation of the warrant structure;
              · The performance and solvency of relevant counterparties; and

         The Reference Asset may confer limited rights relative to direct physical ownership and may
         be subject to contractual limitations, conditions precedent, or operational requirements.
         Investors are required to read and carefully consider the relevant sections of the Whitepaper
         before making any investment decision. The Whitepaper forms an integral part of this Risk
         Disclosure and contains important information regarding the project, the token, and the
         associated risks.

    30. Underlying Asset Characteristics
         Where the Underlying Asset is a collectible commodity rather than a standardised
         commodity asset, such as high purity silver bar of historical relevance, it may possess both:
              · Commodity value (based on silver content and prevailing market prices), and
              · Numismatic / collectible value (based on rarity, provenance, historical importance,
                   artistic merit, and market demand).
         The combined valuation basis creates unique risk characteristics and investors must
         understand the unique valuation risks associated with commodity assets that possess
         numismatic value, including the potential for volatility, illiquidity, and price divergence from
         the underlying commodity due to changes in collector demand

    31. Dual-Valuation Uncertainty
         The Underlying Asset's value may materially diverge from the spot price of the underlying
         commodity asset due to its collectible attributes. Conversely, collectible premiums may
         diminish independently of such price movements.
         There is no assurance that:
              · Numismatic or historical premiums will be sustained;
              · Collectible demand will remain stable; and
              · Market participants will value the asset consistently across time or jurisdictions.
         In distressed or forced-sale conditions, the asset may potentially trade at or below melt
         value.
