# Consumer-franchise view: where Aurumix's margin actually sits

## 1. Thesis

1. Aurumix sells to digital-gold customers, and in digital gold the margin goes to whoever owns the saver's habit and the moment the gold gets spent. For this diaspora that moment is a wedding, a festival or a trip home.
2. Tokenized gold is a USD 5bn crypto trading product with 478k holders, and its issuers earn about 0.2%. Digital gold is mass saving with 2-7% spreads. Jewellery making charges of 8-25%, dated goal plans and family subscriptions are what NRI households already pay for without arguing.
3. Package the gold around those moments, borrow distribution from partners who already reach the NRI customer, and keep the product and the account under the Aurumix name.

## 2. The moves

### Move 1: "Wedding Gold" goal plans that mature into jewellery
- **What it is:** a 10-12 month plan with a fixed maturity date (the LIC habit) that redeems at a partner Indian jeweller in the UAE. Malabar has 67 UAE outlets, and the Joyalukkas group also owns a 34-branch exchange house. The jeweller pays a maturity bonus the way Tanishq Golden Harvest does (about 7.5% of a year's payments) and earns it back through making charges.
- **Why it makes money:** customers already pay AED 15-60/g (3-12%) in Dubai and 7-13% on plain jewellery at Malabar and Kalyan. India's customs rules push them toward jewellery: 20g (men) and 40g (women) come in duty-free, while bars pay about 6% from the first gram. Dubai gold is also 8-12% cheaper than India. Jar's Nek jewellery line brings in about ₹100 cr of its ₹208 cr operating revenue.
- **Numbers:** a 40g bridal set is about USD 5,200 of gold at USD 130/g. A 3-4% referral is USD 155-210 per conversion. If 25% of accounts convert once every four years, that is USD 10-13 per account per year (USD 5-8 for 20g sets). The jeweller pays the bonus, so it costs Aurumix nothing and can replace part of the ICS discount (USD 3.11 per payer).
- **Effort:** 3-6 months to sign one jeweller. Revenue starts with the first conversion. No inventory.
- **Licence or partner:** a jeweller partner. The buyback stays fee-free at the LBMA fix and the jeweller pays the commission, so the customer pays Aurumix nothing on exit. Counsel needs to confirm this sits outside VARA's redemption-fee ban. Making charges carry 5% VAT.
- **Biggest risk:** the jeweller moves the customer into its own scheme. Ask for exclusivity within each jeweller group and keep the balance in the Aurumix account.

### Move 2: Occasion gifting (gift grams plus minted keepsakes)
- **What it is:** "send grams" for Eid, Diwali, Akshaya Tritiya, births and weddings, delivered into a new account. Optionally, a 1-10g Aurumix-branded bar in a gift box, shipped direct by the mint.
- **Why it makes money:**
  - Bigger tickets. UAE app purchases average Dh700. A Dh500 gift (about USD 136) earns a USD 6.80 entry fee on one debit. The modelled USD 33.60 SIP earns USD 1.68 and loses money after the AED 5 rail (break-even ticket about USD 49).
  - Retail markup. Emirates Minting's making charge on a 1g bar is AED 12.60 (about 2.6%), and Dubai shops sell 1g at 20-61% over the city rate.
  - Each gift recipient is an account acquired at almost no cost.
- **Numbers:** a 5g keepsake (about USD 650) at a retained 5-8% earns USD 33-52 per unit, or about USD 2-3.5 per customer per year. The acquisition effect is bigger. Replacement marketing costs USD 26.67 per UAE payer per year (USD 73 CAC × 36.5% churn). If gifts and referrals bring in a fifth of new accounts, that line falls by about USD 5.
- **Gimmick or real:** the Trump USD 250 proof sells about 23% over melt because the US Mint controls scarcity (one per household). The Trump USD 1 "gold" coin contains no gold and sells on novelty alone. Aurumix has neither kind of brand. An Aurumix bar resells at melt minus under 1%. The premium is earned once, at the point of gift, for the occasion, the box and the engraving. Never market it as holding value.
- **Effort:** digital gift grams in 6-8 weeks [experience, unverified]. Physical keepsakes in 3-4 months. Aim for the spring 2027 Eid and Akshaya Tritiya season.
- **Licence:** counsel must confirm that a making charge on a minted item is not a redemption fee.
- **Biggest risk:** fulfilment turns into the manual handling Tony is worried about. The mint ships direct, and Aurumix never holds stock.

### Move 3: Aurumix Family, one subscription with the Will and an AI gold concierge
- **What it is:** a paid tier at about USD 5-7/month (my proposal). It bundles the family portfolio, the digital will and nominee, and a rules-based WhatsApp concierge in Hindi, Urdu and Malayalam: payday top-ups, retries on failed debits, goal tracking and festival reminders.
- **Why it makes money:** in Year 7, 64.7% of accounts (130,638 holders against 71,208 payers) pay nothing, because storage is free and exit fees are banned. A subscription earns from those households whatever their ticket size. Robinhood bundles its AI into a USD 5 plan, and Sarwa is profitable with a USD 7/month minimum.
- **Numbers:** at USD 6/month and 15-25% attach, USD 11-18 per account per year, against USD 5.21 in the model today. Software subscriptions usually run 70-85% gross margin [experience, unverified]. Each extra 10pp of retention adds USD 270k in Year 7 (simulation).
- **Gimmick or real:** sold alone, it is a gimmick. Origin had to sell at USD 1 for the first year, and about 70% of US adults have little confidence in AI money advice. Its real value is keeping the debit running.
- **Effort:** 2-4 months.
- **Licence:** keep it non-discretionary, acting only on rules the user sets. Making decisions for the client needs SCA portfolio-manager status or a VARA Management & Investment Services licence (AED 280k-1.5m capital). The Will needs a legal partner.
- **Biggest risk:** the 15% attach rate came from a client instruction with no evidence behind it. Run a paid pilot before it goes into the model.

### Move 4: Rebuild the Gold Card around the trip home
- **What it is:** position the card as the cheapest way to spend in India or Pakistan on a visit, with a 1.5-2% FX markup against the 3.14% UAE banks charge. Drop the cashback race.
- **Why it makes money:** card FX is the largest pool Aurumix can reach. Interchange loses money after scheme costs (Aurumix keeps 0.72% of spend and pays 0.89% in cross-border scheme fees). Fasset's 6% XAUT cashback is paid for with USD 1m from Tether. Our own rewards cost is understated about twentyfold.
- **Numbers:** USD 18-24 per active cardholder per year on USD 1,200 of spend abroad. Raising card take-up from 18% to 30% is worth USD 1.32m (simulation).
- **Effort:** 6-9 months, set by the issuer's timeline.
- **Licence:** a CBUAE-licensed issuer (already in the plan).
- **Biggest risk:** the ATM line (USD 570k, 12.7% of revenue) assumes the card becomes the customer's salary card. If it does not, card spend stays near today's USD 215 a year.

### Move 5: Co-brand inside a channel that already reaches the NRI customer
- **What it is:** a white-label package (goal plan, family tier and card) under a joint brand for exchange houses, jewellery groups and WPS payroll agents. Candidates: Joyalukkas Exchange (owned by a jeweller), LuLu (already collects Muthoot gold-loan repayments), and Al Ansari (444 branches, already gives away gold coins as prizes).
- **Why it makes money:** acquisition is 49% of the Year 7 cost base. These partners already spend marketing money on gold. They would pay for a retention product instead.
- **Numbers:** price per active user, for example USD 1/month (my proposal). A partner with 50k active savers then pays USD 600k a year. The simulation shows 2.5 partners cover fixed costs.
- **Effort:** a 6-12 month sales cycle [experience, unverified].
- **Biggest risk:** if the partner owns the brand, Aurumix ends up in SafeGold's position (0.03% EBITDA). OGold already sits inside Botim and Aspora. Insist on the joint brand and on Aurumix keeping the account.

## 3. Stop or deprioritise

- **The protocol or infrastructure idea.** SafeGold makes 0.03% EBITDA, Securitize is losing money, and the WGC is building shared rails.
- **The token as the product.** Mint on a layer 2 (about USD 8.5k a year, against about USD 1.7m on mainnet). Make the VARA spend earn its keep before applying.
- **Gold Rewards cashback and the USD 364k loyalty ladder** (8.1% of revenue). A jeweller-funded bonus can replace both.
- **USD 20 monthly debits.** Collect quarterly or set an AED 250 minimum.
- **Oman and Bahrain marketing.** 18% of the budget is spent from month 1 for a region that opens in month 13.
- **The 0.75% AUM fee for B2B, and ATM revenue in the model** until there is evidence customers will use it as a salary card.
- **Basic fixes the ops team owns regardless:** fractional allocation on kilobars (the premium costs USD 4.99 per payer), UAE PASS KYC, and AI-first support.

## 4. What changes in the breakeven picture

First, a correction. The "~45k accounts" figure is out of date: it predates decisions D28-D35. Today retail alone needs 146,289 paying customers, and the plan reaches 88,594. The p90 funding need is USD 4.82m, 43% of paths pay back by Year 7, and none do without partners.

- **Retail alone:** at 71k payers that is USD 0.7-2.1m of contribution. Set it against roughly USD 0.35m of modelled fixed costs plus USD 1.34m of headcount that is not modelled. The retail-alone threshold falls to about 56k-169k payers. At the top half of the range, retail breaks even inside plan scale. At the low end, partners are still needed.
- **Funding:** the moves cut CAC directly. That is the variable that breaks every partner count at 1.66× and lets 11 partners clear at 17% lower. My guess is the p90 need falls toward the workbook's USD 2.3m. That is unverified, so re-run the Monte Carlo before Friday's plan is signed.

## 5. The 60 seconds to the founder

"Your customers don't want a token. They want their daughter's wedding set, gold for Eid and Diwali, and something that looks after the family. In this community people already pay 8 to 25% at those moments without arguing, and today we earn nothing on any of them. The token, the vault and a protocol all earn under 1%. That's SafeGold's seat, at 0.03% EBITDA.

So, five moves. Every SIP becomes a dated plan that matures into jewellery, with the jeweller paying the bonus and our commission. Customers gift grams, and every gift brings in a new account. A family subscription carries the Will and a concierge that keeps the debit running. The card becomes the cheapest way to spend at home. And all of it goes inside an exchange house or jeweller that already has the customers, with our name next to theirs.

We stop the cashback war, the USD 20 debits and the protocol. Today each UAE customer costs us USD 10 a year. These moves can make it plus 10 to 30. That's the difference between needing partners to survive and using partners to grow."
