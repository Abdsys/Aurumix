3.4 BRIDGED STABLECOINS
3.4.1 A USD Stablecoin may be made available on a blockchain in a bridged form that is deployed and issued by a third party rather than by Paxos (each, a "Bridged Stablecoin"). A Bridged Stablecoin is created when a third-party bridge provider locks or holds a USD Stablecoin on one blockchain and issues a corresponding token intended to represent that USD Stablecoin on another blockchain. A Bridged Stablecoin is not issued, minted, or redeemed by Paxos, and references in this USD Stablecoin Agreement to USD Stablecoins issued by Paxos do not include Bridged Stablecoins except where expressly stated.
3.4.2 The "
Bridged Stablecoin Standard
" is the set of technical and contractual specifications that must be incorporated into the smart contracts of a Bridged Stablecoin at the time of, and as a condition of, its deployment. The Bridged Stablecoin Standard preserves Paxos's ability, in its sole discretion, to obtain ownership and control of a Bridged Stablecoin's token contract and to convert that token from a third-party-issued Bridged Stablecoin into a USD Stablecoin issued natively by Paxos ("
