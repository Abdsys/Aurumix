**Investor scorecard: 13 gold business ideas for a Dubai seed team (29 Oct 2026)**

Web search was unavailable because the session's 200-search limit was already used. Instead I fetched the cited source pages directly and checked 6 key claims. The scores are my judgment as a GCC fintech investor who used to work at a regulator.

**How to read the scores.** 5 is best. For capital, 5 means the least money needed. The weighted total counts licence ×2, capital ×1.5, defensibility ×1.5 and the rest ×1, so the brief's priorities count more. Maximum: raw 30, weighted 40.

**One-line reasons**
- **Ranks 1-2 (gift and rewards desk):** a simple sale of goods. Clean licence, no stock, cash on the first order. But a mint sells the same bar to anyone online.
- **Rank 3 (Old Gold Desk):** clean licence and earns on every ticket. The spread is thinner than the idea claims (see section 4).
- **Rank 4 (Send Gold Home):** earns 1.6-2% per order and needs about 25k orders a year (L). Demand is unproven.
- **Rank 5 (crypto-to-bar):** the licence itself is easy. The hard part is keeping a bank account for crypto money buying gold, which banks treat as a top money-laundering warning sign.
- **Ranks 6-7 (loan desk, credit line):** the team fits well. But a customer-facing loan desk is not "pure technology", so it needs a CBUAE facilitation licence or must sit inside a lender's outsourcing approval. Revenue is 9-12 months away.
- **Rank 9 (own finance company):** the best economics and a real moat. It needs USD 10-25m (L). Treat it as phase 2.
- **Ranks 10-11 (wedding plans):** incumbents already own this. The app version earns about AED 44 per saver a year (L).
- **Ranks 12-13 (lease desk, token):** risk-adjusted they lose money, or they lose money outright.

**The trade-off.** None of the 13 is both defensible and doable at seed. The ones a seed team can do are trading businesses with little moat. The defensible ones (lending) need licences and capital beyond a seed round.

### 3. My top 2 (they share one licence, one compliance setup and one refiner or mint)

**Pick 1: B2B festival gold desk (merge Gift Gold and the Rewards Desk)**
- **Why:**
  - Least capital: USD 0.1-0.25m (L).
  - Simplest licence: a gold trading licence, plus registration as a Dealer in Precious Metals and Stones (DPMS) with the Ministry of Economy, plus goAML reporting. Bars of 99% purity or more are VAT zero-rated (M, [PBZ](https://www.pbz.ae/insights/vat-for-gold-and-jewellery-traders-uae-reverse-charge)).
  - Clients pay upfront, so there is no stock and no price risk.
  - The founder's network of South Asian-run businesses and exchange houses is the sales channel.
- **The first offer:** a company's HR or marketing team orders, say, 200 × 1g 24K minted bars. Each comes in an Eid, Diwali or long-service card with the company logo, delivered on a fixed date. The price is fixed on the purchase order. The card prints a buyback price of spot minus 1%.
- **Price:** spot plus a flat AED 65 per piece, so about AED 544 at today's spot of AED 478.8/g (H, derived). That is about AED 46 above Emirates Minting's plain bar at AED 498.25 (H) and AED 26 below Al Fanan's AED 570 (M).
- **How we earn on day one:** we buy at the mint the same day for about AED 501-509 all-in (spot + AED 12.60 making + 0.5-1% dealer premium + pack and delivery; L). We keep about **AED 35-43 per piece**. That is about AED 7-8.6k gross per 200-piece order, or AED 3-5k after sales time (L).
- **Kill test:** 5 signed purchase orders for Eid al-Fitr 2027 (about 9-10 Mar, L) that pay at least AED 35 per piece over mint cost. Otherwise stop.
- **Limits:** it is seasonal and has no moat. Treat it as a cash-generating wedge, not a venture-scale company.

**Pick 2: Old Gold Desk, run as a kiosk inside a partner exchange-house branch**
- **Why:**
  - No CBUAE licence, as long as we never give the seller a right to buy the gold back. That would be pawn lending.
  - Revenue in 2-3 months (L).
  - Every ticket earns money.
  - It builds what a bank would want to see before letting us run gold-loan desks: appraisers, XRF testing, a refiner relationship and trust among NRI families.
- **The first offer:** "Sell your old gold, watch the test, get paid in minutes." We run the XRF test in front of the customer, show the weight and live price on screen, and pay **97.5% of fine-gold value** by instant bank transfer. No fee and no hidden deductions. Today that is about AED 428/g for 22K (H, derived), against the 22K shop selling rate of AED 451.75 (M, [AngelOne](https://www.angelone.in/news/commodities/gold-rate-dubai-vs-india-gold-prices-on-october-29-2026)).
- **How we earn on day one:**
  - We lock a sale to a Dubai refiner at about 99.4% of fine value (L; get a written quote).
  - Gross is about 1.9%, or AED 208 on a 25g ticket.
  - About AED 186 is left after testing, transfer and insurance (L).
  - About AED 136 is left after a AED 50 community referral (L).
  - Break-even is about 9 tickets a day against about AED 451k of fixed cost (L).
- **What it depends on:** souk buyers already pay about 98% of fine value (L-M). So we would win on trust, language and speed, not on price. That is an untested assumption.
- **Kill test before signing a lease:** mystery-shop 10 souk buyers and get a refiner quote for 1-2 kg weekly lots. Do not open if souk buyers pay 98.5% or more with honest testing, or if the refiner pays under 99%.

**Why the Gold Loan Desk is not in the top 2:** it has the biggest prize and the best team fit. But revenue is 9-12 months out, it needs a CBUAE structure, and it depends on a lender giving us about 9% a year of the book. Rupeek's partner-lender history is the warning (H). Keep it as an option. Spend at most USD 50k (L) on a counsel memo and talks with 3 lenders while Pick 2 runs. Move it up only when a lender signs a term sheet that pays us at least 6% servicing.

### 4. Economics that look made up or unsupported
- **Old Gold Desk:** the 97% payout is about 1 point below the souk benchmark. At 98% the gross falls to 1.4% (AED 153). Contribution after AED 100 of acquisition is about AED 31, so break-even rises to about **40 tickets a day**, not 9 (L). The 99.4% refiner payout is unverified.
- **Gift Gold B2B:** charging companies AED 570 for a 1g piece, the same as shop retail, does not hold up. Emirates Minting sells a plain 1g bar online at AED 498.25 (H). A realistic margin is AED 22-42 per piece, not AED 62, so break-even rises from about 28 orders to roughly 40-80.
- **Gold Loan Desk:** the lender paying us 9% a year (6% servicing plus the fee) has no precedent we could find. The 16% APR is set against bank rates whose only source is an SEO site with no citations (L).
- **Gold Credit Line:** the AED 499 annual fee, the 30% draw rate and the vault slot cost are all assumptions. Nobody has tested bank locker prices.
- **Wedding Plan, own brand:** it counts interest on customer money held in escrow (may not be allowed), borrows a AED 270 acquisition cost from an old model, and assumes 25% of customers exit early. "Advance bookings from day one" is unverified.
- **Wedding Plan in apps:** assumes a 60% conversion rate and a 1% jeweller commission, with no jeweller signed. Even on those numbers it earns AED 44 per saver.
- **Crypto-to-bar:** sizing demand as "0.23% of USD 56bn UAE crypto inflow" is a top-down guess. That inflow is mostly exchange flows. The 0.15% wholesale bar cost is L.
- **Gold lease desk:** it assumes lessees pay 4.5% while its own notes put lease rates at about 1-2% in late 2026 (L). The spread may not exist.
- **Gold Lombard:** a 2.5% spread is about 4 times what Maple keeps (H, per the idea). Neither borrower demand nor lender demand is evidenced.

**Do not fund:** the gold token, the lease desk, or either wedding plan as a seed bet.
