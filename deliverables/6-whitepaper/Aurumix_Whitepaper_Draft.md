## 1. Aurumix in brief

Many families in the Gulf already save in gold. They buy jewellery, coins and small bars, often a little at a time. But small buyers pay high making charges, get no record of ownership, and find it hard to sell at a fair price.

Aurumix lets you save in real gold from USD 20 a month. Every dollar you put in, after a clearly shown entry fee, buys allocated gold. Each gram is held for you in a vault in Dubai. You receive one AURX token for every gram you own.

**What you get:**

- **Real gold, recorded by the gram.** 99.99% pure gold from LBMA-accredited refiners, in serial-numbered bars, held in a Dubai vault.
- **Ownership held outside Aurumix.** A trust holds the gold for AURX holders, apart from Aurumix's own assets.
- **A simple way in.** A monthly savings plan from USD 20, plus one-off purchases whenever you like.
- **A simple way out.** Sell your gold back to Aurumix for cash at the next published gold price. No exit fee. No storage fee.
- **Benefits for steady saving.** Your Investor Conviction Score (ICS) tracks how regularly you save and how much of your gold you keep. It never looks at how much money you have. A higher score gives you a lower entry fee, better credit and card terms, Gold Rewards on card spending and discounts on family services.
- **Services on top of your gold.** Every customer can open a credit line against their gold and a Gold Card. You can also name family members to receive your gold.

AURX is an open ERC-20 token. Identity checks happen when tokens are created and when they are sold back. In between, you can hold AURX in your Aurumix account or move it to your own wallet.

Holding AURX earns no payments. The price of your gold is the gold price. What your steady saving changes is the terms you get on Aurumix's services.

## 2. The problem: saving in gold today

Gold is the default way to save for millions of households in the Gulf, especially South Asian families. They think about gold in grams. They send money home every month. Many already pay into insurance-style plans on a fixed schedule.

What they cannot easily do is build up real gold in small monthly amounts, at a fair price, with a clear record of what they own and a reliable way to sell. Each existing route falls short.

| Route | Where it falls short |
|---|---|
| Jewellery | Making charges of 10 to 25%. Resale is marked down. No ownership record. No services |
| Coins and small bars | High mark-ups at small sizes. You must store them yourself. No committed buyer |
| Bank gold accounts | Usually unallocated. You are owed gold by the bank, and no specific bars are held for you |
| Most gold tokens | Built for traders and large buyers. High effective minimums. No savings plan |

The result is simple. A family that saves USD 75 a month in gold either pays heavily for jewellery or has no good option at all.

## 3. How Aurumix works

### 3.1 The gold

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Metal | Gold, 99.99% pure |
| Source | Refiners accredited by the LBMA (London Bullion Market Association) |
| Form | Serial-numbered bars, 100 g bars at launch, moving to 1 kg bars as volumes grow |
| Storage | Allocated bars in a vault in Dubai, kept separate from any gold Aurumix owns |
| Record | Each bar's serial number, weight and purity is recorded and checked by an independent assayer on arrival |
| Price | The LBMA gold price (the "fix"), per gram |

If a refiner loses its accreditation, Aurumix is already authorised to replace its bars with bars from an accredited refiner.

### 3.2 One token, one gram

One AURX always equals one gram of gold. Your token balance is your gram balance. If your app shows 7.8200 AURX, you own 7.8200 grams.

The ratio never changes. Aurumix never takes fees out of your grams and never charges you for storage. Your gram count only changes when you buy, sell, receive or send gold.

Small amounts still work. A USD 20 payment buys a fraction of a gram, and the app shows your balance to four decimal places.

### 3.3 What you own

A USD 75 monthly saver will not own a whole bar for many years. So Aurumix records your ownership as **your share of identified, serial-numbered bars**. Your share is recorded individually, to the fraction of a gram. The bars are held only for customers and are never mixed with Aurumix's own gold.

### 3.4 The trust

Aurumix does not hold your gold on its own balance sheet. The gold is held by a separate trust vehicle in the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC).

- The trustee is the legal owner of the bars on paper.
- The trustee holds them for whoever holds AURX at any moment.
- The token ledger is the list of people the gold is held for.

When you receive 5 AURX, the trust holds 5 grams for you. When you send them on, the trust holds those grams for the new holder. The bars stay in the vault. Only the list of holders changes.

The trust is designed to keep customers' gold out of reach of Aurumix's creditors if Aurumix ever fails. Section 16 explains which parts of this structure rest on Aurumix's legal reading and are still being confirmed.

Your **country of residence** decides whether you can open an account, whatever your passport.

|---|---|

| India (residents) | No. Indian residents cannot be onboarded |
| United States | No |

Non-resident Indians living in the UAE can join. If you later move back to India, your account is paused. You keep your gold, and your score is frozen. Section 7.6 explains how this works.

Every customer must pass identity (KYC) checks, sanctions screening and an eligibility check before any gold is bought. Money that arrives before these checks are complete waits in a client account. No gold is bought until you are verified.

## 5. Buying gold

### 5.1 Two ways to buy

You have one account and two ways to buy.

| | Monthly savings plan (SIP) | Spot purchase |
|---|---|---|
| When | Once a month, on a date you choose | Any time |
| Amount | From USD 20. You can change it every month. No maximum | Any amount from USD 20 |
| Counts toward your ICS score | Yes | No |
| Entry fee | Base fee, less your tier discount | Base fee, less the same tier discount |
| The gold | Identical | Identical |

Only your monthly plan builds your score. Once you have a tier, its entry-fee discount applies to every purchase, monthly or spot. Extra payments in the same month count as spot purchases.

### 5.2 Getting started

1. **Open your account** and record your country of residence.
2. **Pass identity checks** (KYC, sanctions and politically exposed person screening).
3. **Register a bank account in your own name.** Aurumix only accepts money from a bank account that matches your name.
4. **Accept the terms**, including the trust terms.
5. **Set up your plan**: choose your date and amount.
6. **Pay each month** with one tap in your banking app, or prefund several months at once.

### 5.3 How you pay

Aurumix never pulls money from your account. You always send it.

- **One tap a month.** Each month you receive a payment request in your own banking app through AANI, the UAE's instant payment system. You approve it with one tap. If you do not pay, the request simply expires. No fee. Nothing bounces.
- **Prefund and forget.** You can send one transfer covering several months. Aurumix draws that month's amount on your date. The unused balance stays your money and you can get it back.

Aurumix accepts bank money only. It does not accept cryptocurrency or stablecoins.

### 5.4 What happens when you pay

Every purchase follows the same order: **money, then title, then token.** If the gold cannot be placed in your name, no token is created.

1. **You pay** from your registered bank account.
2. **Your money lands in the client account** within one calendar day. It is still your money.
3. **The price is set** at the first LBMA gold price published after your money clears. Neither you nor Aurumix can choose it.
4. **Your grams are calculated**: your payment, less the entry fee, divided by the price per gram.
5. **Gold is allocated to you** under the trust, the same day.
6. **AURX is created**, one token per gram, and sent to your account.
7. **The entry fee becomes Aurumix's money** only at this point, once the gold is yours.
8. **Your records update**: your gold balance, and one counted month on your score if this was your monthly payment.

Aurumix aims to complete all of this within 24 hours of your money clearing.

### 5.6 Large purchases

| Purchase size | What happens |
|---|---|
| Up to AED 3,500 (about USD 950) | Standard purchase |
| Above AED 3,500 | Extra sender details are collected, as UAE transfer rules require |
| Above AED 50,000 | Bank transfer, enhanced checks and proof of source of funds |
| Above about one bar | Aurumix buys a bar directly from the dealer for you. You get an indicative price first, then a same-day purchase. Settlement takes longer and the threshold is shown in the app |

### 5.7 How a USD 20 purchase gets real gold: the gold float

Dealers sell gold in bars. The smallest practical bar is 100 g, worth about USD 11,000. Savers buy fractions of a gram. Aurumix bridges this gap with a **gold float**.

The float is a small stock of bars that Aurumix buys with its own money and keeps in the vault. When you buy, your grams move out of the float and into your allocated holding under the trust, the same day. When the float has given out a bar's worth of gold, Aurumix buys a new bar to refill it.

The float belongs to Aurumix. It never counts as backing for AURX, and it is never mixed with customer gold. Aurumix carries any change in the gold price while the float refills. The float also takes in gold that customers sell back, so most exits never need a sale to the dealer.

## 6. Selling your gold back

You can sell any amount of your gold back to Aurumix for cash at any time. This is how you exit. Aurumix does not deliver physical gold.

- **Price:** the next LBMA gold price published after your request.
- **Fee:** none. No exit fee of any kind.
- **Payment:** to your own bank account, in AED, or in USD where available.

### 6.1 How it works

1. **Request** a sale in grams or in currency, full or partial.
2. **Checks run first**: identity, sanctions screening, residence, your bank account name, and that the grams are not pledged to a loan.
3. **The price is set** at the next LBMA fix after your request.
4. **Your AURX is removed** from circulation (burned).
5. **The grams return** from your holding to the Aurumix float.
6. **You are paid** to your registered bank account.

Checks run before the price is set, so a failed check never leaves a price hanging.

*Example: Sophie sells 10 g. The next gold price is USD 110.00 per gram. She receives USD 1,100.00. Nothing is deducted.*

### 6.2 How long it takes

| Sale size | Paid within |
|---|---|
| Small | The next business day |
| Medium (may need a sale to the dealer) | Up to three business days |
| Large | Up to five business days, possibly in stages |

The exact size bands are published in the app and the client agreement.

If you hold an active Gold Card, a sale request waits 48 hours so card payments already made can settle first.

### 6.3 If many people sell at once

The buyback is designed to hold up when many customers sell together.

- Most sales are covered by new purchases. The float passes sold grams to the next buyer.
- Settlement times scale with size, so the largest sales can be paid in stages.
- Sales can be paused only in narrow cases: the gold market is closed, the vault fails, or an event outside anyone's control occurs. A shortage of cash at Aurumix is never a reason to pause.
- Your gold is held by the trust. If Aurumix could not pay cash, you would still own your gold.

## 7. The Investor Conviction Score

### 7.1 What the score does

The Investor Conviction Score (ICS) runs from 0 to 100. It measures how you save:

- how long you have saved,
- how regularly you save now, and
- how much of your gold you keep.

It never looks at how much money you put in or how much gold you hold. A saver paying USD 20 a month and a saver paying USD 2,000 a month reach the top tier on the same day.

Your tier sets your benefits. The amount you save sets the size of things like your credit limit. Your tier sets the terms.

### 7.2 The first step: six months in a row

Your score starts after **six monthly payments in a row**, each of at least USD 20. This is called **Confirmed SIP**.

- Before that, the app shows a countdown ("4 of 6, two months to go") instead of a score.
- If you miss a month before reaching six, the count starts again.
- Once you reach six, Confirmed SIP is permanent. A later miss never removes it.
- Everyone who reaches it starts at the Silver tier, with a score of 25. Your score never falls below 25 after that.
- Before Confirmed SIP you can still buy gold, sell it, and use credit and the card at base terms.

### 7.3 How the score works

Three facts come from your payment and gold records:

- **Record:** how many months you have paid since your six-month run began. Your first year takes your record to 50. The next four years take it to 100. It never goes down.
- **Recent:** how many of the last 12 months you paid. Each month is worth the same.
- **Kept:** how much of your gold you still hold compared with a year ago, counting what you added since.

Your score is **the lower of your Record and your Recent**, reduced only if you sell or move out more than 30% of your gold in a year. Up to 30% a year, nothing happens at all.

Using the lower of the two means you need both a long history and recent steady saving. A long history cannot make up for stopping. Recent saving cannot make up for a short history.

A few practical rules:

- **Lowering your payment is free.** Any amount from USD 20 counts the same.
- **You have a 5-day grace period** after your payment date. It never ends on a weekend or public holiday.
- **A missed month stays missed.** You cannot pay for past months later. Late money is offered as a spot purchase.
- **Moving AURX to your own wallet counts as gold leaving your account** for the score, because Aurumix can no longer see it.
- **Moving gold to a family member's sub-account does not count** as a sale.
- **The score belongs to you.** It is tied to your verified identity. It does not move with your tokens, and closing and reopening an account does not reset it.

### 7.4 The tiers

|---|---|---|---|
| No tier | No score yet | Six payments in a row not yet reached | |
| **Silver** | 25 | Six payments in a row | Month 6 |
| **Gold** | 50 | 12 months paid, and 6 of the last 12 | Month 12 |
| **Platinum** | 75 | 36 months paid, and 9 of the last 12 | Month 36 |
| **Sovereign** | 100 | 60 months paid, all 12 of the last 12, and no more than 30% sold in the last year | Month 60 |

Silver at six months, Gold at one year, Platinum at three years, Sovereign at five.

Tiers are fixed thresholds. Your tier depends only on your own record. Other customers cannot push you down.

Your tier is updated once a month. Every benefit uses that monthly tier.

### 7.5 What a missed month costs

A missed payment lowers your Recent count by one for 12 months. Then it drops out. Nothing happens to your gold.

*Example: Mark has reached Sovereign.*

| Months missed in a row | Mark's tier |
|---|---|
| 1 | Platinum |
| 3 | Platinum |
| 4 | Gold |
| 7 | Silver |
| 12 | Silver (the floor) |

When Mark starts paying again, his long record is still there. He only needs to rebuild his recent months. About nine steady months take him back to Platinum. A new saver needs 36.

A lower tier only affects the future. Nothing you have already received is taken back. It never closes your card or credit line, and it never triggers a margin call.

**A lower score changes your benefits only. It never touches your gold.** Your grams go down only when you sell or send gold, or when part of your gold is sold to repay a loan after a fall in the gold price (section 9.4).

### 7.6 If you move to a country Aurumix cannot serve

If a rule stops Aurumix accepting your money, for example because you become resident in India, your account is paused automatically.

- You keep your gold.
- Your score and Confirmed SIP are frozen. Months Aurumix cannot accept your money never count against you.
- Existing credit runs to its end, with no new draws.
- If the rules require your gold to be sold, the grams leave and your score does not change.

## 9. Credit and the Gold Card

### 9.1 One credit line, two ways to use it

Aurumix offers one gold-secured credit line. You can use it in two ways:

- **Cash:** draw money to your bank account.
- **Card:** spend with the Gold Card. Card spending draws on the same line.

Both use the same gold as security and share one borrowing limit in the app. This lets you use the value of your gold without selling it. Pledged gold keeps counting toward your score.

A licensed lender provides the money and owns the loans. A licensed card issuer issues the card. Aurumix runs the app, values your gold, checks each card payment against your limit and services your account.

### 9.2 Your borrowing limit

**Your limit = your eligible grams × the gold price × your loan-to-value.**

- **Eligible grams** are grams you have held for at least 90 days and have not already pledged.
- **Loan-to-value** is 40% for everyone, rising to 50% at Gold, 65% at Platinum and 80% at Sovereign.
- **Your ratio is set when you open the line.** An annual review resets it to your current tier, never below 40%. Money you have already borrowed keeps the ratio it was borrowed at.

*Example: Emma holds 100 g, all held for more than 90 days. The gold price is USD 109.31 per gram, so her gold is worth USD 10,931. At base terms she can borrow up to USD 4,372.40. At the Gold tier the limit is USD 5,465.50. At Sovereign it is USD 8,744.80.*

Pledged grams stay in your Aurumix account under a registered security. You cannot move or sell them until the loan is repaid.

Borrowing costs (such as setup, servicing and lending charges) are set by the licensed lender. They are shown to you before you draw.

### 9.3 The Gold Card

Every customer can have the card at level L1. Higher tiers give better terms.

- **Gold tier:** lower FX margin (2.0%) and a monthly ATM allowance of AED 1,000.
- **Platinum:** the L2 card, 1.5% FX margin and AED 2,500 ATM allowance.
- **Sovereign:** the L3 card, 1.0% FX margin and AED 5,000 ATM allowance.

A card upgrade happens after three months in a row at the higher tier. The card itself never downgrades. Card fees follow your tier on each statement, down to the base terms.

Each card payment is checked live against your gold. If the gold price falls, your available limit falls with it.

A tier fall never closes your card or credit line. Only the rules in section 9.4, you, or the lender can close them.

### 9.4 If the gold price falls

When you borrow, a fall in the gold price raises the share of your gold that the loan represents. Aurumix warns you early and sells only as much gold as needed.

| Loan as a share of your pledged gold | What happens |
|---|---|
| Up to 80% | Normal |
| **85%** | A notice in the app |
| **88%** | A formal written notice. You have **14 days** to add gold, repay part of the loan, or add cash |
| **92%** | Aurumix sells only enough gold to bring the loan back to 88%. Never all of it. You keep the credit line |

Any sale is at the LBMA gold price, through the float, with no discretion. Costs are itemised and any surplus is yours.

How far the gold price would need to fall before a sale, if you borrowed your full limit:

| Tier | Loan-to-value | Gold price fall before a sale |
|---|---|---|
| No tier and Silver | 40% | 57% |
| Gold | 50% | 46% |
| Platinum | 65% | 29% |
| Sovereign | 80% | 13% |

A 13% fall in the gold price can happen within a year. Sovereign borrowers who use their full limit carry real risk. Borrowing less than your limit gives you more room.

## 10. Gold Rewards

**Gold Rewards is monthly cashback on your Gold Card spending, paid in grams of gold.**

| Tier | Gold Rewards rate on card spend |
|---|---|
| No tier and Silver | None |
| Gold | 0.15% |
| Platinum | 0.45% |
| Sovereign | 0.75% |

Your reward is your tier's rate times your card spending that month. It is worked out at month end and credited in grams at the next gold price.

*Example: Emma is at the Platinum tier and spends USD 800 on her Gold Card in March. Her reward is 0.45% of USD 800, which is USD 3.60. At a gold price of USD 109.31 per gram, she receives 0.0329 g.*

Where the money comes from matters:

1. **It is paid from card and credit revenue.** When you pay with the card, the merchant's side pays a small fee called interchange. Aurumix receives a share of it. That share funds Gold Rewards. It never comes from other savers' fees or from Aurumix's profits.
2. **It is capped** at the revenue your own card and credit use brings in.
3. **It depends on spending and tier.** The amount of gold you hold plays no part.
4. **It is cashback on spending.** It is never a payment for holding gold, and Aurumix never describes it as one.

Reward grams are yours like any other gold. They do not count as a monthly payment for your score. They count as eligible for credit after 90 days.

Gold Rewards starts when the card goes live.

## 11. Family Portfolio and succession

### 11.1 Two family features

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Family Portfolio** | Lets you set aside gold for named family members during your life. They can see their designated grams. The gold stays yours until it moves |
| **Beneficiary Transfer Instruction** | A standing instruction to move gold to named people when a set event happens |

Both are open to every customer who pays for the family plan. Your tier lowers the price.

The family features work on gold held in your Aurumix account. Gold you move to your own wallet is outside them.

### 11.2 Three kinds of trigger

| Trigger | Example | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **A date** | A child's 18th birthday | Gold moves on the date, while you are alive |
| **A condition** | Starting university, getting married | Gold moves when the event is shown to have happened |
| **Death** | | Gold moves after a court grant of probate is received |

Date and condition triggers are gifts during your life. They are the core of the product: an education fund, a wedding gift.

On death, the law still requires probate. Probate is the court process that confirms who inherits. Aurumix cannot avoid it. What Aurumix offers is speed: your beneficiaries are named, verified and screened in advance, so the transfer can happen within days of the court grant arriving.

### 11.3 How a transfer happens

1. **You name beneficiaries** and set their shares and triggers. Each is verified and screened.
2. **The trigger happens.** On death, the account is frozen as soon as Aurumix is credibly notified.
3. **Authority is confirmed.** For date and condition triggers, your instruction is enough. On death, Aurumix waits for a grant of probate or a court order.
4. **Each beneficiary is checked again**: identity, sanctions and residence.
5. **Any loan is settled first** (section 11.4).
6. **The gold moves** as AURX from your account to the beneficiary's verified account.

A beneficiary who wants cash can open an account and sell the gold back like any other holder, free of charge.

**For beneficiaries resident in India:** lifetime gifts are not available. On death, their share is paid in cash.

## 12. Partners, agents and referrals

### 12.1 The partner channel

Digital wallets, neobanks and payment apps can offer Aurumix gold inside their own apps. Their customers buy and hold AURX without opening an Aurumix account. Aurumix supplies the gold, the storage, the token and the licence. The partner owns the customer relationship.

**How it works:**

1. **The partner signs and connects** to Aurumix's register. Each partner's records are kept separate from every other partner's and from Aurumix's direct customers.
2. **The partner's customer buys gold** in the partner's app, at one all-in price the partner sets.
3. **The gold is bought and held exactly as for a direct customer.** Allocated bars, the same trust, one AURX per gram, the same ownership protections.
4. **Aurumix invoices the partner monthly.** The partner pays a platform fee, a small yearly percentage of the gold its customers hold (typically 0.50 to 0.75% a year, set in each partner contract).

Partner customers do not have an ICS score or tier benefits. The partner handles their payments, so Aurumix cannot see their saving pattern. The partner agreement says this clearly.

## 14. Safety and transparency

### 14.1 The backing rule

**The gold held in the trust must always be at least equal to the AURX in circulation.**

The system checks this continuously. Minting stops automatically if:

- the float cannot supply the gold,
- the vault's confirmation lapses, or
- the title record cannot be confirmed.

Aurumix's own float gold never counts as backing.

### 14.2 The monthly Allocation Report

Every month Aurumix publishes:

- the serial number of every bar held,
- the assay certificate for each bar,
- the total AURX in circulation and the total gold held for customers,
- the gold prices used,
- the float balance, shown separately.

Anyone can compare the gold held with the tokens outstanding.

An independent title register. Beside the stack, a monthly calendar icon with the Allocation Report's contents, and a gauge showing "gold held ≥ AURX outstanding".

### 14.3 Your money and your gold stay separate

- **Your money** goes into a client account at a UAE bank within one calendar day. It is not Aurumix's money and stays outside Aurumix's estate. It becomes the entry fee and the gold cost only when the gold is yours.
- **Your gold** is held by the DIFC trust, apart from Aurumix's own assets.
- **Aurumix's float** is held separately, in the vault and in the accounts.
- **Your tokens** are, under VARA's rules, your property and outside Aurumix's estate.

### 14.4 Several layers of ownership

No single legal tool carries the whole ownership claim. Four layers each record that the gold is held for you:

1. **Allocated bars.** Specific, serial-numbered bars with a weight list.
2. **The customer terms.** The trust is written into your agreement.
3. **The DIFC trust.** Under DIFC trust law, assets properly placed in a trust are protected from the insolvency of the person who placed them there.
4. **An independent title register.** An electronic record of which bars sit in which vault for which owner, kept outside Aurumix's systems.

If one layer is challenged, the others still stand.

### 14.5 If Aurumix winds down

Aurumix keeps a wind-down plan at all times, as VARA requires. Because the gold is held for customers, the plan never depends on selling customers' assets. It sets out a published claim window, the buyback of tokens as holders come forward, and a freeze on transfers where needed. Most customers pay monthly, so Aurumix can reach them directly.

## 17. Risks

**Gold price risk**
The value of your AURX moves with the gold price. It can fall.
*Mitigations:* none can remove this risk. It is the nature of holding gold. Aurumix prices every purchase and sale at the published LBMA price so you always receive the market value.

**Borrowing risk**
If you borrow against your gold and the gold price falls, some of your gold can be sold. At the Sovereign tier and full use of your limit, a 13% fall starts a partial sale.
*Mitigations:* two notices before any sale, a 14-day window to act, partial sales only, and sales at the published gold price with any surplus returned to you. A tier fall never triggers a sale.

**Legal structure risk**
The ownership structure rests on legal readings that no court has tested (section 16.3).
*Mitigations:* four independent layers of ownership, so one challenge does not collapse the claim. The token contract can switch to verified holders only if the law requires it.

**Aurumix failure risk**
Aurumix could fail as a business.
*Mitigations:* your gold is held by the DIFC trust, your money is held in a segregated client account, and a wind-down plan is kept at all times. The plan never depends on selling customer assets.

**Buyback pressure risk**
Many customers could sell at once, and Aurumix may need to sell gold to the dealer in a falling market.
*Mitigations:* the float absorbs day-to-day sales, settlement times scale with size, and larger sales can be paid in stages. Sales cannot be paused for lack of cash.

**Partner risk**
Aurumix depends on a bullion dealer, a vault, a lender and a card issuer. A partner could fail or leave.
*Mitigations:* the float separates daily operations from the dealer's timing. The sourcing terms allow bars from other accredited refiners. Customer gold sits in the trust, apart from any single partner.

**Card system risk**
If Aurumix's systems are unreachable, card payments may be approved under fixed backup rules without a live gold check.
*Mitigations:* backup approvals are limited to small amounts with a hard total limit, no ATM and no cross-border use.

**Secondary market risk**
AURX may trade on third-party venues at prices below the gold price.
*Mitigations:* the buyback at the LBMA gold price is always available to verified holders.

**Regulatory change risk**
Rules in the UAE or elsewhere could change and affect the product, where it is offered, or who can hold it.
*Mitigations:* the product is built to follow VARA's rules, the contract can be upgraded, and a regulatory pause protects customers whose eligibility changes.

**Payment risk**
Payment providers could change their prices or policies, which could affect the USD 20 minimum or how customers pay.
*Mitigations:* push payments mean nothing ever bounces and no fees apply to a missed payment. The prefunded balance offers a second way to pay.
