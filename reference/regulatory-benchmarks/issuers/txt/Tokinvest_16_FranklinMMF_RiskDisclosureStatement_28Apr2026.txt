1. Purpose of this Notice
This Notice provides a description of the risks associated with investing in and transacting in
Virtual Assets, including the FT Money Market Fund Token (the "Token").
This Notice should be read together with the Whitepaper and all relevant fund documentation.

2. Risk of Loss
Investment in the Token involves a risk of loss of part or all of the invested capital.
Returns are not guaranteed.
The value of the Token reflects the performance of the underlying money market fund and may
fluctuate due to changes in market conditions, interest rates, credit conditions, and liquidity
dynamics. Investors may receive less than their original investment, or nothing at all.

3. Nature of the Product and Rights
The Token is a digital representation of an investor's economic exposure to an underlying money
market fund accessed through Tokinvest's platform.
The Token:

     · does not represent legal or beneficial ownership of fund units or underlying assets;
     · does not constitute a claim on the fund, its issuer, or its portfolio; and
     · does not constitute a claim on the general assets of Tokinvest.

Tokenholders have contractual rights solely against Tokinvest in respect of:

     · platform access;
     · submission of subscription and redemption instructions; and
     · receipt of economic outcomes as determined and recorded in Tokinvest's internal

          systems.

All rights are contractual in nature and depend on the proper functioning of Tokinvest's platform
and the wider distribution chain.

4. No Direct Relationship with Fund Issuer
Investors:

     · are not registered as unitholders in the underlying fund;
     · have no direct contractual or legal relationship with Franklin Templeton or any fund

          entity; and
     · cannot exercise rights directly against the fund or its service providers.

5. Intermediation Chain Risk
Investment in the Token relies on a multi-party structure involving:

     · Tokinvest (platform operator and sub-distributor);
     · appointed distributor(s), including Pinetree; and
     · the fund issuer and its administrators and custodians.

The ability to access, transact in, or realise value from the Token depends on the continued
operation of all parties within this distribution chain.

Failure, delay, or disruption at any level may:

     · delay or prevent execution of subscriptions or redemptions;
     · affect pricing, settlement, or availability of liquidity; or
     · impair investor access to economic outcomes.

6. Tokinvest Custody Model
Virtual Assets are held within a Tokinvest-operated platform environment and supported by
third-party custody providers.
Tokinvest is not a VARA-regulated custodian and provides custody only as an incidental activity
to its Brokerage Services, utilising third-party VARA-regulated custodians and/or custody
technology providers.
Custody arrangements may involve multiple layers of infrastructure. Failures or disruptions at
any level may affect access to Tokens.

7. No Investment Advice
Tokinvest does not provide financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.
Nothing in this Notice constitutes a recommendation or personalised advice.
Investors are responsible for conducting their own independent assessment.

8. No Clearing or Third-Party Brokerage
Tokinvest does not provide clearing services for other VASPs and does not introduce clients to
other VASPs.

9. Not Exhaustive
This Notice does not set out all risks.
The risks applicable to any investment depend on individual circumstances and transaction
terms.

11. Tokinvest Insolvency Risk
In the event of Tokinvest insolvency:

     · investors rely on contractual rights, internal records, and custody arrangements;
     · investor entitlements are determined solely by Tokinvest's internal records, which are

          not independently recognised by the Issuer, Pinetree, or the fund registrar;
     · access to the platform and Tokens may be restricted or suspended;
     · investor positions may be subject to reconciliation processes across internal records,

          custody providers, and upstream distributors; and
     · recovery of value will depend on applicable legal frameworks, custody arrangements,

          and Tokinvest's rights within the distribution chain.

Delays, partial recovery, or total loss may occur.
There is no assurance that investor interests will be fully protected or that access to underlying
fund exposure can be preserved in a timely manner.

12. Distributor and Counterparty Risk
Tokinvest relies on third-party distributors and service providers to access and transact in the
underlying fund.
Failure or disruption of these parties may:

     · delay or prevent execution;
     · impact pricing or settlement; and
     · impair investor access to underlying exposure.

13. Commingling and Omnibus Account Risk
Underlying fund positions may be held in omnibus or commingled accounts at distributor or
intermediary level (including Pinetree).
Tokinvest is recognised as a single investor at the distributor level, and underlying investors are
not recorded or recognised by Pinetree, the Issuer, or the fund registrar.

As a result:

     · investor holdings are not individually segregated at the fund or distributor level;
     · investor entitlements are determined solely by Tokinvest's internal records;
     · errors, delays, or discrepancies in reconciliation may affect investor entitlements; and
     · in insolvency or operational failure scenarios, recovery of interests may be delayed,

          reduced, or uncertain.
